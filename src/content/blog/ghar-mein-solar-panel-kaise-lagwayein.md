> **Process overview।** घर में solar लगवाने के 7 steps हैं: (1) Eligibility और site check, (2) System sizing, (3) PM Surya Ghar application, (4) Vendor selection, (5) Installation, (6) Net metering, (7) Subsidy receipt। Physical installation सिर्फ 1-3 दिन का काम है।

[PM Surya Ghar](/glossary/pm-surya-ghar/) ने residential solar का process एक national portal से जोड़ा है। जानिए exactly क्या करना है।

## Step 1: Site Assessment - Roof Suitable Hai?

Solar panel लगाने से पहले roof की suitability check करें। Complex या large roofs ke liye Heaven Designs' site survey and land feasibility service ek detailed professional assessment provide karti hai।

### The 4-Point Roof Check Framework

**Check 1, Available Area**
- 3 kW system के लिए minimum 25-30 square feet shadow-free area
- 1 kW = 8-10 square feet (4-5 panels × 2 sq ft each approximately)
- Measure करें: length × width of unshaded area

**Check 2, Shadow Analysis**
- सुबह 9 बजे से शाम 4 बजे तक shadow किसी भी panel पर नहीं पड़ना चाहिए
- Trees, water tanks, neighbouring buildings, AC units - सब shadow sources हैं
- Partial shadow भी production significantly कम करती है

**Check 3, Roof [Azimuth](/glossary/azimuth/) / Direction**
- South-facing: Best generation potential
- South-East / South-West: Good, thodi kam generation
- East या West only: Acceptable, kam generation (installer ki simulation se exact farq dekhein)
- North-facing: Avoid

**Check 4, Structural Condition**
- Roof 25+ साल तक panels का weight (15-25 kg per panel) support करे
- Terrace के लिए structural engineer से check करवाएँ (large systems के लिए)
- Old या cracked roof, first repair, फिर solar

## Step 2: System Size Decide करें

**Monthly consumption calculate करें:**
1. Last 12 bills देखें
2. Average monthly units निकालें (usually bill पर printed)
3. System size = Monthly units ÷ 100 (approximately, for average India irradiance)

## Step 3: PM Surya Ghar Application

> [!WARNING] Pehle application, phir installation
> Subsidy के लिए पहले application, फिर installation - यह order important है। Installation से पहले PM Surya Ghar apply न करने पर subsidy नहीं मिलेगी।

### Application Process

<!-- steps -->
1. **pmsuryaghar.gov.in** पर जाएँ
2. State और [DISCOM](/glossary/discom/) select करें (electricity bill देखें)
3. Consumer number enter करें
4. Aadhaar OTP से verify करें
5. Documents upload करें:
   - Latest electricity bill
   - Aadhaar card
   - PAN card
   - Bank passbook/cancelled cheque
   - Property proof

Application submit होने के बाद DISCOM feasibility approval देती है; इसमें लगने वाला समय आपके DISCOM पर निर्भर है।

[PM Surya Ghar documents complete guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-documents-required/), state-wise requirements।

## Step 4: Vendor Selection - Critical Step

Portal पर आपके district के approved vendors दिखेंगे। यह selection सबसे important decision है। Portal ke bahar bhi nearby installers dhundhne ka tool reference ke liye use kar sakte hain।

### The 5-Point Vendor Selection Framework

**Point 1, ALMM Compliance**

Verify: [mnre.gov.in/almm-list/](https://mnre.gov.in/almm-list/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}

**Point 2, Multiple Quotes**
Minimum 3 vendors से same specification पर quote लें।
Same-spec comparison: same kW, same ALMM-certified brands।

**Point 3, Warranty Terms**
- Product warranty (panel और inverter separately)
- Workmanship warranty (installation quality)
- Written warranty documents, verbal नहीं

**Point 4, Reference Sites**
Vendor से पिछले 5 installations के addresses माँगें।
उन homeowners को call करके experience पूछें।

**Point 5, After-Sales Service**
- Servicing timeline commitment - कितने दिन में response?
- Local service team है या भेजते हैं?
- Monitoring support included है?

[Empanelled vendor selection guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/), complete vendor evaluation।

## Step 5: Installation Process

Vendor confirm होने के बाद installation:

### Installation Day: Kya Hoga

**Pre-installation:**
- Vendor team आती है, roof final survey करती है
- Mounting structure और cable plan finalize होता है

**Day 1, Mounting और Panels**
- Mounting structure (galvanized steel) roof पर fix होती है
- Solar panels mounting structure पर installed होते हैं
- DC cables panels से inverter location तक laid होती हैं

**Day 1-2, Inverter Installation**
- Inverter suitable wall पर mount होता है (shade में)
- DC connections, panels से inverter
- AC connections, inverter से main electrical panel

**Day 2-3, Commissioning**
- System test, panels generating, inverter converting
- Monitoring app setup, WiFi configure
- Generation verification, kW output check

**Quality Check करें:**
> [!CHECKLIST] Installation ke baad quality check
> - Panel tilt angle correct (10-25 degrees)
> - All MC4 connectors properly mated
> - Earthing wire connected
> - Inverter shade में, ventilated
> - App showing real-time generation

[Solar installation quality checklist](/blog/solar-annual-maintenance-checklist-india/), post-installation verification।

## Step 6: Net Metering Activation

Installation के same day या next day vendor net metering application submit करे।

**Process:**

<!-- steps -->
1. Vendor DISCOM को net metering application submit करता है
2. DISCOM engineer आकर system inspect करता है
3. [Bidirectional meter](/glossary/bidirectional-meter/) install होता है (old meter replace)
4. Net metering active, import और export दोनों track होता है

**Timeline:** Net meter लगने का समय DISCOM और क्षेत्र (शहर या ग्रामीण) के हिसाब से अलग होता है; अपने DISCOM से current timeline पूछें।

> [!IMPORTANT] Net meter se pehle export credit nahi
> Net meter active होने से पहले export credit नहीं मिलता। Generation होती है लेकिन exported units waste होते हैं। इसलिए immediately application जरूरी है।

[Net metering guide](/blog/net-metering-india-complete-guide/), state-wise timelines और process।

## Step 7: DISCOM Inspection और Subsidy

Net meter install होने के बाद DISCOM inspector आता है:

**Inspection में क्या check होगा:**

- Installation quality (wiring, earthing, mounting)
- System working correctly (live generation देखेंगे)
- Net meter reading baseline

**After inspection:**
- DISCOM clearance certificate
- MNRE को report
- Subsidy bank में आने का समय portal और DISCOM की processing पर निर्भर है

> **₹78,000** directly आपके bank account में - PFMS के through। SMS notification आएगा।

## First Month After Installation: What to Monitor

The first 30 days after installation are critical for catching any issues early:

> [!FORMULA] Expected generation
> Expected kWh = system size (kW) × peak sun hours × 0.8 × days

**Week 1, System Commissioning**
- Check app daily, is generation appearing?
- Compare generated kWh with expected (system size × peak sun hours × 0.8 × days)
- 10-15% below expected: acceptable (new system break-in, cloudy weather)
- 30%+ below expected: call vendor immediately

**Week 2-4, Performance Baseline**
- Record first month's total kWh generated
- Compare with your monthly electricity bill to calculate import reduction
- Net metering meter, check if it shows both import and export readings
- Submit net metering application if vendor hasn't done so

**Month 1 Bill Check**
- First bill after solar should show reduced import units
- Net metering credit should appear (if meter is installed)
- If bill has not reduced at all, investigate: system may not be connected to the main panel correctly

Monitoring ke pehle 30 din mein issues jaldi pakad mein aate hain, isliye app aur apne meter readings dono check karte rahein.

## How Solar Panels Work with Different Roof Types

**RCC flat roof (most common urban rooftop):**
- Mounting structure with 10-15° south tilt
- Anchor bolts into concrete
- Rainwater drainage designed around mounting
- Best option for most installations

**Sloped/tiled roof:**
- Clamps attach to roof framing, not tiles
- Tiles around penetration points need waterproofing
- Slightly complex installation but structurally better for panels
- Pre-existing tilt usually optimal for India

**Metal sheet roof (factory, warehouse style):**
- Clamps to metal roofing without penetration
- IP66 inverter more important for industrial environments
- Large roof area often available, good for 5-10 kW systems
