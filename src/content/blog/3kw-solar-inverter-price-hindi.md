## On-grid aur hybrid quote ko ek jaisa kyun nahi samajhna chahiye?

**On-grid inverter ka main kaam solar DC ko grid-synchronised AC mein badalna hai.** Grid fail hone par anti-islanding protection ke kaaran normal on-grid system supply band kar deta hai. Hybrid inverter compatible battery, charging logic aur ek defined backup output bhi sambhal sakta hai, lekin har hybrid model ka behaviour ek jaisa nahi hota.

Hybrid quote mein inverter ke alawa battery, compatible BMS, essential-load distribution board, changeover arrangement aur extra commissioning ka scope dekhna zaroori hai. Sirf inverter box ka rate compare karna total ownership cost nahi batata. Battery ki capacity (kWh), chemistry aur BMS communication ke hisaab se alag quote aayega, aur battery ka sourced rate is page par nahi diya gaya kyunki alag models ka comparison tabhi sahi hota hai jab same usable capacity ho.

Hybrid kab sochna chahiye? Agar aapke area mein roz kai ghante power cut aata hai aur aap fridge, fans, lights aur router ko backup par chalana chahte hain, tab hybrid ka sawal banta hai. Agar cut kabhi-kabhar hota hai, to on-grid system plus ek chhota alag backup arrangement ya sirf on-grid kaafi ho sakta hai. [On-grid aur hybrid comparison](/blog/on-grid-vs-hybrid/) mein operating difference detail mein diya gaya hai.

## 3 kW par PM Surya Ghar calculation kya hai?

Eligible residential installation ke liye MNRE residential CFA guidelines standard central calculation batati hain:

| Installed solar-module capacity | Standard central assistance |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW ya usse zyada | Up to ₹78,000 |

Calculation: pehle 2 kW par ₹30,000 per kW (₹60,000) plus teesre kW par ₹18,000, total ₹78,000. 3 kW se upar koi extra central assistance nahi hai, isliye 5 kW ya 10 kW system par bhi cap ₹78,000 hi rehta hai. Rate general-category states ka hai. Special-category states aur UTs mein CFA aur benchmark alag hain.

Subsidy ke baare mein aur teen baatein:

- **Yeh inverter ka alag discount nahi hai.** MNRE guideline ke mutabik CFA inverter ke size par nahi, installed modules ki rated DC capacity par nikalti hai.
- **DCR modules shart hain.** Same guideline domestically manufactured cells aur modules ki requirement rakhti hai.
- **Amount approval par depend karta hai.** Eligibility, DISCOM inspection aur application approval ke baad hi assistance process hoti hai.

Benchmark par arithmetic yeh banta hai: ₹1,45,000 minus ₹78,000 = ₹67,000. Yeh sirf scheme ka arithmetic hai. Aapka gross contract isse zyada ya kam ho sakta hai, aur subsidy aane se pehle aapko gross amount (ya uska loan) tay karna pad sakta hai. Apne application ka current status aur amount [official PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) par check karein. Poori calculation slab-wise dekhne ke liye [PM Surya Ghar subsidy amount](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-amount/) guide dekhein.

## 3 kW size sahi hai ya nahi kaise decide karein?

Inverter size ko sirf BHK ya monthly bill se fix na karein. Pichhle 12 mahine ki unit consumption, sanctioned load, phase, roof area, shading, proposed module capacity aur backup requirement dekhein. Hybrid system ke liye essential loads ki wattage aur required backup hours alag calculate karein.

**Generation ka example (assumption, guarantee nahi):** agar aap 4 units per kW per day maanein, to 3 kW array se roz 3 × 4 = 12 units aur saal mein 12 × 365 = 4,380 units bante hain, yaani mahine ke lagbhag 365 units. Shade, orientation, temperature, dust aur downtime se yeh badal jata hai, aur kuch jagah par seasonal gap bhi hota hai. Quote dene wale vendor se uska irradiation source aur loss assumptions maangein.

**Roof area:** chhat par kitni jagah lagegi, yeh module ke wattage aur size par depend karta hai. Upar ke example mein 540 Wp ke 6 panel hain, aur har panel ka size uske datasheet mein diya hota hai. Exact area aur shadow-free jagah vendor ke site survey se hi pata chalegi.

**Load check:** 3 kW AC rating ka matlab yeh nahi ki ghar ka saara load ek saath chalega. On-grid system mein din ka load pehle solar se aur baaki grid se aata hai. Hybrid system mein backup ke liye alag se essential loads ki wattage ginni padti hai. Agar ghar mein do-teen AC, borewell pump ya bada geyser ek saath chalte hain, to 3 kW ke badle bade size ya hybrid backup ka alag calculation karna hoga. Kuch ghar 3 kW par aaram se chal jate hain, kuch ko 5 kW ki zaroorat padti hai.

## Payback ka example (assumptions ke saath)

Yeh kisi real customer ka result nahi hai. Yeh ek transparent illustration hai, jisme aap apne numbers daal sakte hain.

**Assumptions:** 3 kW, 4 units per kW per day, 365 din, net cost ₹67,000 (benchmark minus standard CFA). Tariff alag-alag examples mein ₹6, ₹7 aur ₹8 per unit liya gaya hai. Yeh tariff aapke DISCOM ka slab nahi hai.

| Tariff (assumed) | Saal ki generation | Saal ka value | Simple payback on ₹67,000 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ₹6 per unit | 4,380 units | ₹26,280 | lagbhag 2.5 saal |
| ₹7 per unit | 4,380 units | ₹30,660 | lagbhag 2.2 saal |
| ₹8 per unit | 4,380 units | ₹35,040 | lagbhag 1.9 saal |

Yeh best-case arithmetic hai kyunki net cost benchmark-based hai aur saari generation ko retail tariff par offset maan liya gaya hai. Agar aapka real gross quote ₹1,80,000 ho, to subsidy ke baad net ₹1,02,000 hota hai, aur ₹7 per unit par payback lagbhag 3.3 saal ban jata hai (₹1,02,000 divided by ₹30,660).

Teen cheezein is hisaab ko hila sakti hain:

1. **Net metering settlement.** Jahan export ki value retail tariff se kam hoti hai, wahan sirf self-consumed units hi poori value par ginne chahiye.
2. **Shading aur orientation.** Shaded roof par generation assumption se kam aayegi.
3. **Hybrid ki extra cost.** Battery aur backup scope jodne par net cost badhta hai, aur battery replacement bhi alag kharcha hai. Is liye hybrid ka payback on-grid se lamba ho sakta hai.

Apne DISCOM ka current tariff aur net-metering rule dekhe bina payback ko promise na samjhein. Hum yahan koi savings ya payback guarantee nahi dete.
