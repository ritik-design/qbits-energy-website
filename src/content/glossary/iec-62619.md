**IEC 62619 is a safety standard for secondary lithium cells and batteries used in industrial applications.** Stationary battery storage may fall within its scope. A report for cells alone does not establish that a complete battery pack, inverter-battery combination or installed energy-storage system has been tested to every applicable requirement.

Do not infer an Indian registration obligation, a fixed testing cost or a whole-system approval from the standard number alone.

## What does a certificate establish?

Look for the precise product named in the report: cell, module or battery pack. Check the model designation, rated electrical characteristics, standard edition, issuing body and any limitations. A distributor's brochure mentioning “IEC 62619” is not a substitute for evidence covering the equipment you will install.

| Evidence to request | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Cell or battery-pack test report | Identifies the tested product and applicable edition |
| Battery management system documentation | Explains operating limits and protection behaviour for the pack |
| Inverter compatibility statement | Confirms the proposed battery and inverter are supported together |
| Local regulatory or utility requirement | Establishes what documentation this Indian installation actually needs |

IEC 62619 does not replace an installation design, fire-safety review, electrical protection or the manufacturer's operating instructions. The [battery-sizing guide](/blog/battery-sizing-hybrid-solar/) addresses usable capacity; the [BMS guide](/blog/bms-hybrid-solar-inverter-explained/) covers communication and protection questions. Both matter when choosing a hybrid system.

## Is IEC 62619 mandatory for a home solar battery in India?

Do not decide that from a generic glossary sentence. Indian conformity obligations depend on the product category, the current BIS order and the specific application. Ask the manufacturer or supplier for the exact Indian Standard and registration route claimed for the battery model, then have the project engineer check the current legal requirement. A test report to an international standard and a valid Indian registration are different pieces of evidence.

Similarly, the [MNRE ALMM page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/approved-list-of-models-and-manufacturers-almm/) describes PV module and cell lists; it is not a battery certification directory. If a seller says a battery is “ALMM approved,” ask them to identify the actual rule and product entry rather than relying on that phrase.

For procurement or compliance, confirm the current IEC edition and model-specific Indian conformity route with a qualified reviewer. The [MNRE ALMM page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/approved-list-of-models-and-manufacturers-almm/) was checked on 23 September 2026 for the list-scope correction.
