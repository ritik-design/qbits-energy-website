## What is IEC 62109

> **TL;DR**
> - IEC 62109 is the international safety standard for PV power converters, split into Part 1 (general safety) and Part 2 (inverter-specific requirements).
> - It covers every inverter topology, from [micro inverters](/glossary/micro-inverter/) and [string inverters](/glossary/string-inverter/) to [central inverters](/glossary/central-inverter/) and [hybrid inverters](/glossary/hybrid-inverter/).
> - Indian compliance runs through IS 16221, harmonised with IEC 62109, and BIS certification is a prerequisite for [ALMM List](/glossary/almm-list/) eligibility.
> - Testing covers insulation, voltage withstand, touch current, thermal rise, EMC and abnormal operation, and takes 6 to 18 months.
> - Grid-interface behaviour like [anti-islanding](/glossary/anti-islanding/) is referenced from separate standards (IEC 61727, IEC 62116) rather than tested directly under IEC 62109.

IEC 62109 is the international safety standard for power converters used in photovoltaic power systems, published by the International Electrotechnical Commission. It is the foundational safety document for all PV inverters, including grid-tied, off-grid, hybrid, micro and central inverters.

The standard has two parts. Part 1 (IEC 62109-1:2010) defines general safety requirements applicable to all PV power converters, covering electrical safety, insulation coordination, protection against electric shock, thermal limits, mechanical robustness and electromagnetic compatibility. Part 2 (IEC 62109-2:2011) provides particular requirements specifically for inverters, including topology-specific safety considerations.

For Indian solar, the equivalent national standard is IS 16221, harmonised with IEC 62109. [BIS certification](/glossary/bis-certification/) under IS 16221 is required before any inverter can be sold in India for solar applications. ALMM listing requires IS 16221 compliance as a prerequisite, a full regulatory picture that this [BIS/IEC compliance guide for 2026](/blog/solar-inverter-regulations-india-2026-bis-iec-compliance/) walks through end to end.

## Technical deep dive

### Part 1 (general)

IEC 62109-1 covers requirements applicable to all PV power converters:

- Insulation system classification
- Protection class (Class I, II, III)
- Voltage withstand requirements
- Earthing arrangement
- Component ratings and derating
- Thermal management
- Material flammability
- Marking and labelling

### Part 2 (inverters)

IEC 62109-2 adds particular requirements for inverters:

- Topology-specific isolation
- DC and AC port requirements
- [Anti-islanding](/glossary/anti-islanding/) interface (referenced from IEC 62116)
- Grid synchronisation (referenced from IEC 61727)
- Internal DC bus management
- Capacitor discharge requirements

## Indian market context

IS 16221 is the Indian equivalent, harmonised with IEC 62109.

BIS certification under IS 16221 is mandatory for inverters sold in India for solar applications.

ALMM listing requires BIS certification as prerequisite.

DISCOM commissioning verifies certificate validity, a step that also confirms the inverter's anti-islanding protection is active before grid connection is approved.

Major certification bodies operating in India: TUV Rheinland India, TUV SUD India, Intertek India, UL India, Bureau Veritas India. EPCs verifying a specific model should work through the [9-standard inverter certification checklist](/blog/solar-inverter-certifications/) rather than accepting the manufacturer's certificate list at face value.

IEC 62109 is the foundational international safety standard for PV inverters. Indian inverters comply through IS 16221, harmonised with IEC 62109. BIS certification under IS 16221 is mandatory for Indian sale and is a prerequisite for ALMM listing. The standard covers electrical, thermal, mechanical, EMC and material safety.
