## The True Cost of Going Solar for a 1 BHK

The **True Cost of Going Solar** framework identifies five cost components for any solar system. For a 1 BHK:

1. **Solar panels** - 2 to 4 panels of 500–550 Wp. Panel cost at ₹22–₹26/Wp: ₹22,000–₹57,200 depending on system size and panel quality.
2. **Inverter** - A 1kW or 2kW ALMM-listed on-grid inverter: ₹10,000–₹25,000. Smaller inverters cost less in absolute terms but have similar quality tiers.
3. **Balance of System (BOS)** - Mounting structure, cables, junction box, SPD, earthing, net meter interface. For 1–2kW: ₹12,000–₹20,000.
4. **Installation and commissioning** - Labour, civil, DISCOM application. For 1–2kW: ₹8,000–₹15,000.
5. **GST** - 12% on panels, 12–18% on inverter and BOS. Approximate GST on 1–2kW system: ₹6,000–₹12,000.

## 1kW vs 2kW - The Sizing Decision for a 1 BHK

This decision deserves an honest table. Many installers push 2kW even for no-AC homes because margins are higher on larger systems.

| Factor | 1kW System | 2kW System |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Best for** | No-AC 1 BHK, usage < 200 units/month | 1-ton AC 1 BHK, usage 250–400 units/month |
| **Daily generation** | 3.5–5 kWh | 7–10 kWh |
| **Monthly units generated** | 105–150 | 210–300 |
| **Gross cost** | ₹65,000–₹85,000 | ₹1,00,000–₹1,30,000 |
| **Subsidy** | ₹30,000 | ₹60,000 |
| **Net cost** | ₹35,000–₹55,000 | ₹40,000–₹70,000 |
| **Payback at ₹7/unit** | 4.5–7 years | 3.5–5.5 years |
| **Roof space needed** | 6–9 sq.m. | 12–18 sq.m. |
| **Panels** | 2 panels | 3–4 panels |
| **Can run AC?** | ✗ Not reliably | ✓ 1-ton comfortably |

**Decision rule:** If your monthly bill is consistently below ₹1,200 and you have no AC, choose 1kW. If your bill is ₹1,500–₹3,000 and you have or plan to add a 1-ton AC, choose 2kW. Do not buy a 3kW system for a genuine 1 BHK unless you have extremely high consumption or plan to add a second AC within 2 years. [IRENA's residential solar guidance](https://www.irena.org/Energy-Transition/Technology/Solar-energy) consistently recommends right-sizing based on actual consumption rather than subsidy maximisation, as oversized systems export at lower feed-in tariffs than they save on self-consumption.

## Common Mistakes 1 BHK Buyers Make

- **Buying a 3kW system on the salesperson's advice** - A 3kW system for a 200-unit/month 1 BHK will export most of its generation to the grid at a net metering rate that may be lower than the tariff you pay. The payback advantage disappears and you spend ₹50,000–₹70,000 more unnecessarily.
- **Choosing a non-ALMM inverter** - Saves ₹3,000–₹5,000 on the inverter but loses ₹30,000 to ₹60,000 in PM Surya Ghar subsidy. A straightforward error with a large financial consequence.
- **Not checking net metering eligibility** - Apartment dwellers need to confirm that the DISCOM meters their unit individually (not the entire building) and that net metering is permitted in the building's electricity connection terms.
- **Ignoring installation quality** - A 1kW system has only 2 panels. Saving ₹2,000 on a cut-rate installer who does not earth the system correctly creates a genuine safety risk. The [BIS certification mandate](https://bis.gov.in/) under IS 16221 and the CEA safety standards are non-negotiable for all grid-connected residential installations in India.
- **Forgetting to apply for net metering** - Without a bi-directional net meter from your DISCOM, surplus solar power is wasted. The [net metering application guide](/blog/how-to-apply-net-metering-india/) walks through the process state by state.
