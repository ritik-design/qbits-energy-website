## Real Workflow: Address to Branded Proposal in 20 Minutes

1. Type the address. [AI 3D roof modeling](https://surgepv.com/3d-solar-roof-design) builds a 3D model in under 60 seconds.
2. Call [Clara AI](https://surgepv.com/clara-ai). Natural-language design brief produces the first draft.
3. Run [8,760-hour shade analysis](https://surgepv.com/shadow-analysis). Module-level shading complete in under 30 seconds.
4. Generate the [bankable yield report](https://surgepv.com/generation-financial-tool). P50, P75, P90 produced inline.
5. Run the financial model. Country tariffs, cashflow, IRR, NPV.
6. Export [DXF/DWG for AutoCAD](https://surgepv.com/solar-autocad-integration). Single-line diagram and BOQ auto-generated.
7. Send the [branded solar proposal](https://surgepv.com/solar-proposals). Interactive web link plus PDF, e-signature included.

Under 20 minutes end to end. That is the workflow benchmark by which we ranked the 12 platforms.
