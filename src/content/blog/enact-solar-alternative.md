## Why Installers Look for an Enact Solar Alternative

### Engineering depth
Enact's strength is residential proposal velocity, and the product is positioned around the customer conversation rather than lender-facing engineering. If 8,760-hour module-level shading and [bankable yield](/glossary/p50-p90/) reporting are on your requirement list, ask Enact what it offers there today rather than assuming. SurgePV ships [solar shading analysis](https://surgepv.com/shadow-analysis) and [bankable yield reports](https://surgepv.com/generation-financial-tool) on every paid plan.

### Multi-country and multi-language
Enact positions itself across many countries, but its depth of local tariff, incentive and compliance modelling varies by market, so verify coverage for the specific market you sell into. SurgePV ships proposals in 9 languages and code libraries for NEC, IEC, AS/NZS, IS plus country-specific [tariffs](/glossary/tariff/).

### C&I scope
For installers growing into [C&I rooftop](/c-i-solution/) or ground-mount, the question is how much of the platform was built around residential deal flow. Enact is positioned around the customer-facing residential and small commercial sale, so run a real large project through it before you assume the fit. SurgePV covers all segments in one license.
