## The Hybrid Inverter Surge in India: Why This Comparison Matters Now

India's residential solar market has shifted. Through 2022, [on-grid (grid-tied) systems](/blog/on-grid-vs-hybrid/) dominated because net metering was available and power cuts were bearable. By 2025, two trends changed the calculus: power cut frequency increased across UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, and parts of Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh, and LFP rack batteries became widely listed in India. Dated listings checked on 14 October 2026 worked out to about ₹21,100 to ₹24,000 per nominal kWh for 5.12 kWh packs (Loom Solar CAML-10051 at ₹1,08,000 and Deye SE-F5 PLUS at ₹1,23,000).

> **30%.** Share of new residential solar installations in India in 2025 that included a battery storage component, up from approximately 8% in 2022. *Source - [Mercom India Q4 2025 Solar Report](https://www.mercomindia.com/), Mercom Capital Group.* IRENA's [Battery Storage for Renewables report](https://www.irena.org/publications/2015/Jan/Battery-Storage-for-Renewables-Market-Status-and-Technology-Outlook) tracks global cost decline curves that underpin this residential hybrid adoption shift.

The [hybrid inverter](/glossary/hybrid-inverter/) is the enabling technology for this shift. It manages the solar generation, the grid connection, the battery charge/discharge, and the home loads simultaneously, in a single device. For buyers, choosing the wrong hybrid inverter means being locked into a battery network that limits future choices, or being dependent on a service network that cannot reach them when something fails.

### Reading the table

**AC output rating.** The Growatt datasheet lists a maximum AC output of 4,000 W for the SPH 5000TL BL-UP, and the "5000" in the name reflects the PV and model class. The Deye datasheet lists 5,000 W rated. If you need 5 kW of AC output, compare the AC row and not the model name.

**PV voltage window and maximum DC voltage.** The Growatt datasheet lists a 120 to 550 V MPPT range and a 550 V maximum. The Deye datasheet lists 150 to 425 V and 500 V. A wider window gives more string-design room, and a higher maximum gives more margin on long strings in cold weather. Both are design constraints, not quality grades, so check your string's hot-day maximum-power voltage against the lower limit and its cold-morning open-circuit voltage against the maximum.

> [!FORMULA] Cold-morning string check
> Number of modules in series × module Voc × (1 + temperature coefficient × (lowest site temperature − 25 °C)) must stay below the inverter's maximum DC voltage

**Battery side.** The Deye datasheet lists up to 120 A charge and discharge current and the Growatt datasheet lists 85 A, both on a nominal 48 V class battery range. Check the battery's own continuous current limit, because the lower of the two limits governs.

**Backup.** The Growatt datasheet lists a UPS function with a switch time below 10 ms. The Deye datasheet text we read lists off-grid peak power of twice rated power for 10 seconds and no switch time. If sensitive loads such as computers or medical equipment sit on the backup output, ask for the switch time in writing for the exact model.

**Generator and retrofit.** Deye's datasheet lists support for storing energy from a diesel generator and DC and AC coupling to retrofit an existing solar system. The Growatt BL-UP datasheet lists smart load management via a dry contact and does not mention a generator input. Confirm any generator or retrofit design with the supplier before ordering.

**Compliance lines.** Neither datasheet prints Indian standard numbers. That does not show that either product lacks Indian registration. Ask each seller for the BIS registration and current certificates for the exact model, and do not rely on a datasheet line.

## Battery compatibility

The [battery management system (BMS)](/glossary/bms/) determines how the inverter communicates with the battery, manages charge and discharge, and protects cells. Both datasheets list RS485 and CAN communication, and the Deye datasheet lists self-adaption to the BMS for lithium batteries. A communication port does not mean every battery works with every inverter.

> [!WARNING] Get the exact battery pairing approved in writing
> Getting BMS compatibility wrong can damage an expensive battery pack. Before buying a battery, ask the seller for the manufacturer's current compatibility list for the exact inverter model and firmware, and check the battery's own charge and discharge current limit against the inverter's. Do not rely on a spec-sheet line or a verbal assurance.

Buyers sizing the battery bank alongside the inverter should also work through a proper [battery and energy storage sizing exercise](https://surgepv.com/hub/energy-storage/battery-sizing/) rather than defaulting to whatever pack the distributor has in stock.

## Which to choose when

These rules are starting points. Your string design, battery and the service route for your district override them.

- **Long strings or a high-voltage array.** The Growatt datasheet lists a 120 to 550 V window and 550 V maximum, and the Deye datasheet lists 150 to 425 V and 500 V. Run your cold-morning open-circuit voltage and hot-day maximum-power voltage against both.
- **You need 5 kW of AC output.** The Deye datasheet lists 5,000 W rated. The Growatt SPH 5000TL BL-UP lists 4,000 W, so check the AC row of whichever Growatt model you are offered.
- **A large or fast-charging battery bank.** The Deye datasheet lists up to 120 A against 85 A for Growatt. Confirm the battery's own limit and the approved pairing.
- **Sensitive loads on backup.** Growatt lists a switch time below 10 ms. Ask Deye's seller for the switch time of the exact model in writing.
- **A diesel generator or an existing solar system to retrofit.** The Deye datasheet lists generator energy storage and AC coupling. Ask whichever seller you use to confirm the design for your site.
- **Hot or cold site.** Deye lists -40 to +60 °C with derating above 45 °C, and Growatt lists -25 to +60 °C. Check the derating curve for the exact model.
- **You want the warranty settled before you buy.** Both datasheets list 5 years with a longer option. Get the document that applies to your sale, the remedy and who issues it.
- **Remote or small-town site.** Ask each seller for the named service partner for your pincode and what they stock. The brand's national contact does not answer this.

## Common Mistakes Hybrid Buyers Make When Comparing These Brands

- **Comparing by model name instead of the AC row.** The Growatt SPH 5000TL BL-UP lists 4,000 W AC output. Compare the datasheet rows, not the number in the name.
- **Pairing a battery without a written compatibility check.** Confirm the exact battery model and firmware against the manufacturer's current list, not the spec sheet alone.
- **Assuming service is available in your city.** A national contact point is not a local service route. Ask for the named partner within reach of your pincode before purchase.
- **Treating a 5-year warranty as adequate for a 25-year asset.** Both datasheets list the same standard term. See [the solar inverter warranty guide](/blog/solar-inverter-warranty/) for why you should plan for replacement.
- **Not factoring battery cost into the price comparison.** The hybrid inverter price is only one part of the system cost, and the battery may need replacing within the inverter's life.
- **ALMM scope:** Verify current PV module and cell requirements. Check inverter documents separately.
