## Does HDFC Have a Green Home Loan for Solar?

Older articles, including an earlier version of this guide, described an HDFC "Green Home Loan" for solar. We found no such product on HDFC Bank's own pages. The personal loan page (read 14 October 2026) mentions no solar or green loan. The home loan page mentions top-up loans (maximum tenure of 15 years or retirement age, whichever is lower) but gives no top-up rate and no solar use. It states that home loan pricing is benchmarked to the policy repo rate. Where an HDFC branch is willing to finance a solar system through a home loan top-up, treat it as a bank-specific arrangement and get the rate, tenure and fees in writing before you rely on it.

### HDFC Personal Loan: The Published Route

HDFC's Personal Loan page advertises "starting at 9.99%" with loans of up to ₹1 crore, and its EMI calculator ranges from 9.99% to 24% per annum and from 1 to 7 years. The page does not state a solar-specific limit or fee in the part we could read. The rate you are offered depends on your profile, so the advertised starting rate is a floor, not a promise.

> **₹78,000.** The maximum PM Surya Ghar central subsidy for a 3 kW or larger residential rooftop system (₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW), credited after commissioning and DISCOM verification. *Source - [SBI Surya Ghar loan page](https://sbi.bank.in/web/personal-banking/loans/pm-surya-ghar-loan-for-solar-roof-top) and the [PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), read 14 October 2026.*

> [!EXAMPLE] ₹3 lakh personal loan with the subsidy used for prepayment
> A ₹3,00,000 HDFC Personal Loan at the advertised starting rate of 9.99% for 5 years has an EMI of about ₹6,373 and total interest of about ₹82,358 (calculated here). If the ₹78,000 subsidy is later used to prepay principal, the interest falls and the loan closes sooner; ask the bank how it applies a prepayment and whether a charge applies.

## HDFC Solar Loan Eligibility Criteria

HDFC Bank's pages we read give no solar-specific eligibility rules, so the points below are the general ones to confirm with the bank. Do not treat them as HDFC's published criteria.

- **Age and income**: set by the bank for the Personal Loan; ask for the current minimums.
- **Credit score**: lenders look at your CIBIL score; a stronger score usually gets better pricing, and the advertised 9.99% is a starting rate.
- **Employment or business history**: salaried and self-employed applicants are assessed differently; ask for the documents the bank wants.
- **Property ownership**: not required for a personal loan, but required for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and DISCOM approval, which are tied to the roof and the connection.
- **Existing HDFC relationship**: may make processing easier for an existing customer; ask the branch.

## The HDFC Solar Loan Decision Tree

### The HDFC Solar Loan Decision Tree

Before working through the loan amount question, confirm the system size and payback assumptions with a solar cost and savings calculator. The loan amount should match what the roof and consumption profile justify, not just what a vendor quotes.

This framework helps you decide where to start in four questions:

1. **Is your loan likely to be ₹2 lakh or less?**
   - Yes → read SBI's page first: 5.75% published, no minimum CIBIL score on the page, nil processing fee.
   - No → go to question 2.

2. **Is your loan between ₹2 lakh and ₹6 lakh, with a CIBIL score of 650+ and net annual income of ₹3 lakh+?**
   - Yes → SBI's 7.90% slab applies on its page; compare it with the written offer from HDFC.
   - No → go to question 3.

3. **Are you an existing HDFC customer who needs unsecured credit quickly?**
   - Yes → ask HDFC for the Personal Loan offer in writing and compare the total interest with SBI's.
   - No → compare at least two lenders' written offers.

4. **Do you own or have permission for the roof?**
   - Yes → proceed with PM Surya Ghar registration and DISCOM approval.
   - No → rooftop solar on rented property needs the owner's permission first.

If you land on SBI or IREDA after this tree, read the linked guides ([IREDA solar loan guide](/blog/solar-loan-ireda/) and [SBI solar loan guide](/blog/solar-loan-sbi/)) before making a final call.

## How to Apply for an HDFC Solar Loan: Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Assess Eligibility**
Check your CIBIL score before applying and decide which lender's offer you will compare against.

**Step 2: Collect Documents**
Assemble the KYC, income and solar-specific documents listed earlier. Incomplete documentation is a common cause of delay.

**Step 3: Get a Solar Quotation from an Empanelled Vendor**
For PM Surya Ghar subsidy compatibility, the vendor must be empanelled on the [PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/). Request an itemised quotation with GST-registered details. Check our [PM Surya Ghar empanelled vendor guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) for how to verify vendor status.

**Step 4: Submit Application**
Apply at the HDFC branch, in the HDFC Bank app or online. Say plainly that the purpose is rooftop solar so the application is routed correctly, and ask for the rate and fees in writing.

**Step 5: Sanction and Disbursement**
On approval, HDFC issues a sanction letter. Ask how funds will go to the vendor and keep the letter that shows the rate, tenure and fees.

**Step 6: Solar Installation and Subsidy Application**
The empanelled vendor installs the residential solar system, [applies for net metering](/blog/how-to-apply-net-metering-india/) via the state [DISCOM](/glossary/discom/), and registers the installation on the [PM Surya Ghar portal](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-apply-online/). After DISCOM verification, the subsidy is credited to the homeowner's bank account.

**Step 7: Prepay with Subsidy**
Once the subsidy is credited, ask whether you can make a lump-sum prepayment and whether any foreclosure or part-payment charge applies to your loan.

## Common Mistakes When Applying for HDFC Solar Loan

- **Assuming a Green Home Loan exists for solar**: HDFC's pages show no such product; ask the bank to name the exact product and give the rate in writing.
- **Not comparing**: on ₹2,00,000 over 5 years, the published SBI rate costs about ₹24,300 less interest than 9.99% (both calculated). Check at least two lenders.
- **Missing vendor GST details**: HDFC requires a proper invoice for disbursement; installers without formal registration complicate this.
- **Not mentioning subsidy integration**: tell the loan team that the subsidy credit will be used for prepayment so they can note it on the account.
- **Low CIBIL score at application**: a low score can mean higher pricing or rejection. Check your score before you apply.
- **Not verifying the vendor**: an installer who is not empanelled on the PM Surya Ghar portal means the subsidy application cannot proceed, which removes a large part of the financial benefit.

## Integrating PM Surya Ghar Subsidy with an HDFC Loan

The [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-amount/) is ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW and ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above, paid after the DISCOM verifies the installation. The subsidy is capped, so the net amount you finance grows with the system size.

Table: Central subsidy by system size and the formula for your net loan (your quotation sets the system cost)

| System Size | Central Subsidy | Net amount to finance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 | Your quotation minus ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 | Your quotation minus ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW | ₹78,000 | Your quotation minus ₹78,000 |
| 5 kW | ₹78,000 | Your quotation minus ₹78,000 |
| 10 kW | ₹78,000 | Your quotation minus ₹78,000 |

This guide quotes no system price because no dated source supports a single figure; use your itemised quotation. State-level top-ups vary by state, so check your state nodal agency for current figures; none is assumed here. Remember that the bank lends against the quotation first and the subsidy arrives later.
