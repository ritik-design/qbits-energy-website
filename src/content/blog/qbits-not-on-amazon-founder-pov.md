A sales channel does not prove authenticity, eligibility, installation quality, or warranty coverage. Verify the seller and the exact unit with Qbits before paying, whether the offer comes from a marketplace, distributor, EPC, dealer, or direct enquiry.

> [!KEYTAKEAWAYS]
> - A sales channel does not prove authenticity, eligibility, installation quality, or warranty coverage.
> - Verify the seller and the exact unit with Qbits in writing before paying, whether the offer comes from a marketplace, distributor, EPC, dealer, or direct enquiry.
> - Ask for the exact model, the serial number before final acceptance, a tax invoice, the current datasheet and installation manual, and the current written warranty.
> - The current published evidence does not establish that every third-party listing is counterfeit or that Qbits never sells through any online marketplace.
> - A lower equipment-only price can exclude the work and accessories needed for a safe, functioning installation.

## Ask for these items

> [!CHECKLIST] Before you pay
> - Seller identity and authorisation status, confirmed in writing by Qbits.
> - Exact inverter model and, before final acceptance, serial number.
> - Tax invoice showing the seller and product identity.
> - Current datasheet and installation manual for the exact model.
> - Current written warranty with eligible seller, registration, remedy, exclusions, labour, freight, and claim route.
> - Installation, commissioning, monitoring handover, and service responsibility.
> - Return, cancellation, damage-in-transit, and delivery terms.

## Verify with Qbits

The current published evidence does not establish that Qbits never sells through any online marketplace, that every third-party listing is counterfeit, that all distributors receive protected territories, or that particular channel benefits are universal. Those earlier statements were removed.

## Do not separate equipment from system responsibility

A solar inverter must match the array, grid connection, protection design, monitoring setup, and battery where applicable. Clarify who designs, installs, commissions, registers, and supports the system. A lower equipment-only price can exclude the work and accessories needed for a safe, functioning installation.
