To apply for a **Qbits solar inverter distributorship**, open the official partner form and select `Distributor` as the business type. Prepare accurate details about your company, market, warehouse, team, capital, sales plan, and inverter experience before you start.

Submitting the form begins a review. It is not a distributor appointment. Qbits does not publish a fixed minimum investment, guaranteed margin, exclusive territory, credit limit, stock-return right, or approval timeline on the current application page. Treat each of those as unresolved until it appears in an approved written proposal or agreement.

> **Registration at a glance:** confirm that the distributor route fits your business, review the inverter range, prepare a territory and operating file, complete the five form sections, keep a copy of the submission, answer any due-diligence request through a verified channel, and review the full appointment terms before making commitments.

## Confirm that you are applying as a distributor

The Qbits form offers four choices: Dealer, Distributor, EPC, and System Integrator. Choose `Distributor` when your proposed role is to develop a wider market and supply downstream businesses under an agreed product, geography, and channel scope.

Do not choose the label because it sounds larger. Choose the route that matches what your company can support and what you want the final agreement to define.

| Route | Practical distinction for the application |
| --- | --- |
| Distributor | Describe the market, downstream customer base, stock plan, fulfilment capacity, and support structure you propose |
| Dealer | Describe a local sales route to customers or installers |
| EPC | Describe project design, procurement, installation, and commissioning work |
| System Integrator | Describe how you combine inverters with other equipment, controls, or project systems |

These are working descriptions, not contractual rights. Selecting `Distributor` does not itself grant a territory, appoint sub-dealers, authorise service work, or allow you to represent the application as approved.

If your business is local retail rather than regional distribution, use the [Qbits dealer application guide](/blog/how-to-become-a-solar-inverter-partner-in-india/) before selecting a route.

## Build a distributor readiness file before opening the form

A decision-ready application lets a reviewer understand the business without guessing. Prepare one working file with five sections.

### Market and territory

State the cities, districts, or states you can serve today. Separate an existing customer base from a market you plan to develop. Record the types of downstream buyers you already work with, such as solar EPCs, electrical dealers, retailers, or system integrators.

Avoid claims such as `strong demand across the state` unless you can explain the evidence. A useful territory note identifies current accounts, reachable prospects, buying patterns, delivery constraints, competing product lines, and the person responsible for development.

### Product and stock logic

Review the [current Qbits inverter families](/our-products/) and [model datasheets](/download-datasheets/) before describing a product plan. Identify which customer needs you expect each proposed model or family to serve. Do not state that you are authorised to sell, stock, install, or service a model before the relevant scope is approved.

Build a provisional stock worksheet with model, expected customer type, proposed opening quantity, replenishment assumption, storage need, ageing risk, and exit treatment. Leave price, discount, credit, return, and delivery inputs blank until Qbits supplies the applicable terms.

### Infrastructure and people

Record the current office and warehouse position, including location, ownership or rental status, usable space, access, security, insurance, and handling limits where relevant. List sales, technical, and service personnel separately.

Do not combine employees, contractors, and planned hires into one headcount. State what exists today and what depends on appointment, training, or commercial approval.

### Financial capacity

The form asks about annual turnover, working capital, bank loans, cash-credit limits, other capital, and the bank to which assets are hypothecated. Prepare figures from current records and keep the measurement period consistent.

These fields are not an invitation to estimate an impressive number. They help describe available capacity and existing obligations. Do not count a requested facility as approved credit, or a future investor contribution as cash already available.

### Sales and execution plan

Prepare a monthly target you can explain from named inputs: active accounts, realistic order frequency, model mix, conversion assumptions, delivery reach, sales capacity, and seasonality. A target entered on a form is not evidence of demand.

Also note how product updates, order status, serial records, customer queries, warranty intake, and escalation would be handled. This shows whether the proposed sales plan has an operating process behind it.

## Complete the Qbits distributor form section by section

As of 26 September 2026, the partner application has five visible sections. The fields marked as required are company name, registered address, official business email, 10-digit WhatsApp number, and GST number. Other visible questions provide additional context for the review, but the public page does not state a universal pass score for them.

| Form section | What the current form asks | What to prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company information | Company name, registered address, business email, WhatsApp number, website, and GST number | Details that match current business and tax records |
| Business profile | Business type, annual turnover, inverter brands currently used, and a PM Surya Ghar name for EPC applicants | Select Distributor; use current figures and disclose relevant product relationships accurately |
| Infrastructure and team | Office type, warehouse availability and size, sales team, technical team, service team, and service-centre details | Present capacity, locations, role counts, and any clear limitations |
| Financial details | Working capital, bank-loan amount, cash-credit limits, other capital, and hypothecated bank | Figures from a consistent date, without double counting facilities or debt |
| Sales commitment | Proposed monthly sales, readiness to promote the brand, and previous inverter experience | A supportable target, execution plan, and concise evidence of relevant work |

The form currently asks for entered information rather than presenting a public document-upload checklist. Keep supporting records ready for any later verification, but send sensitive identity or financial documents only through a confirmed Qbits channel. Never share passwords or one-time passcodes.

## Use answers that can survive verification

The best application is not the one with the largest numbers. It is the one in which every material answer is current, consistent, and supportable.

Use these rules while completing it:

1. Enter the legal or registered company name consistently.
2. Use an email address and WhatsApp number monitored by the business.
3. Select `Distributor`, then describe distributor capability rather than EPC project history alone.
4. State `No`, `Not applicable`, or the current position when a facility or team does not exist.
5. Separate owned, rented, contracted, and planned resources.
6. Use the same date and basis for turnover, working capital, debt, and credit facilities.
7. Explain the assumptions behind a sales target instead of presenting it as guaranteed volume.
8. Disclose current inverter brands accurately where the form asks.
9. Keep a dated copy of every answer submitted.

One inconsistency can make the rest of the file harder to assess. For example, a large territory with no delivery plan, or a high sales target with no downstream customer route, needs explanation rather than another optimistic figure.

## Follow the registration process in seven steps

1. **Review the product scope.** Read the current family pages and exact-model datasheets relevant to the market you propose.
2. **Choose the correct role.** Confirm that distribution, rather than local dealing or project execution, is the requested route.
3. **Prepare the readiness file.** Complete the market, stock, infrastructure, financial, and execution sections described above.
4. **Submit the partner application.** Select `Distributor` and enter only current, supportable information.
5. **Save the submission record.** Keep the date, answers, version of the form, and any acknowledgement.
6. **Support due diligence.** If contacted, confirm the request through an official route before providing additional records.
7. **Review the proposed appointment.** Verify the parties, scope, commercial schedules, operating duties, and termination terms before spending against the opportunity.

The current page states that Qbits reviews the submitted details and contacts applicants about the relevant route. It does not publish an automatic acceptance rule or response time. An application acknowledgement should not be used as proof of appointment.

## Do not spend against terms that are still unknown

Distributor economics cannot be assessed from the application form alone. Before committing to inventory, premises, vehicles, hiring, marketing, or customer delivery, obtain the proposed commercial and operating terms.

At minimum, resolve:

| Decision | Written answer required |
| --- | --- |
| Appointment | Legal parties, start condition, term, renewal, and authority to represent the brand |
| Product scope | Exact families or models included, additions, withdrawals, and change notices |
| Territory and channels | Geography, customer types, online sales, reserved accounts, cross-territory sales, and exclusivity status |
| Targets | Measurement basis, period, returns treatment, review, cure, and consequences |
| Price and incentives | Controlling schedule, change process, taxes, freight, rebates, evidence, and reversals |
| Credit and security | Approved limit, due dates, security, suspension, review, and invoice disputes |
| Stock and returns | Order acceptance, title, risk, inspection, damage, slow stock, model changes, and end-of-term inventory |
| Warranty and service | Obligor, coverage, claim process, labour, freight, parts, escalation, and customer communication |
| Marketing and data | Approved claims, brand assets, lead handling, customer records, access, retention, and closure |
| Exit | Termination triggers, notice, open orders, receivables, stock, customer support, and brand removal |

Use the [solar dealer and distributor agreement checklist](/blog/solar-dealer-agreement-checklist/) to review these documents with appropriate legal, tax, accounting, and operational advisers.

If a proposal includes supplier credit or channel protection, use the [distributor credit and territory review guide](/blog/solar-distributor-credit-territory-terms/) to model the terms together before funding stock or accepting a target.

## Run a pre-submission consistency check

Read the application once as a reviewer. Each answer should connect to the same operating story.

- Does the proposed territory match the current sales and delivery reach?
- Does the model mix fit the stated customer base?
- Does warehouse capacity match the provisional stock logic?
- Are sales, technical, and service roles counted separately?
- Can the financial figures be tied to one date and supporting records?
- Does the monthly target show its assumptions?
- Are current brand relationships disclosed where requested?
- Are planned resources clearly separated from resources already available?
- Has anyone made a promise that still needs to be written into the proposal?

Correct the file before submitting. Do not create a second application to repair an avoidable inconsistency unless the Qbits team asks you to resubmit.

## The appointment test

A distributor registration is ready for submission when the company can explain its market, customers, stock logic, fulfilment, team, capital, sales assumptions, and current limitations without relying on a promised margin, credit facility, protected territory, return right, or approval date.

If that describes your business, complete the [official Qbits partner application](/become-our-partner/) and select `Distributor`. Treat the submission as the start of review, and treat every later commercial point as a proposal until the authorised agreement and schedules establish it in writing.
