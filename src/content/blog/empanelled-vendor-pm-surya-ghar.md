To verify a **PM Surya Ghar vendor**, start at the [official PM Surya Ghar National Portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), not a search advertisement or a link sent on WhatsApp. Find the vendor through the current consumer route for your state and DISCOM. Then match the business shown there with the legal name, GSTIN, address, contact and payment beneficiary on the quotation.

Do this before paying an advance. A portal match confirms only one part of the decision. It does not approve the quote, authenticate the salesperson or guarantee subsidy release.

> **Short version:** Check the official record yourself. Match the exact business, not just a familiar brand name. Put every component and responsibility in the quote. Keep government registration separate from a manufacturer's partner directory. Pause if the name, contact, bank beneficiary or project scope does not reconcile.

This guide reflects the national scheme documents and portal route checked on **26 September 2026**. State electricity rules, DISCOM processes and live portal screens can change. Confirm any unclear record with the relevant authority before acting.

## Use the official portal, not a copied vendor list

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy's **residential Central Financial Assistance guidelines, as amended on 7 July 2025**, say that a consumer seeking CFA must choose a vendor registered on the National Portal. The amended guidelines describe vendor profiles, comparison information, contacts, ratings, feedback and project-performance information.

That makes the National Portal the starting point for a PM Surya Ghar vendor check. A spreadsheet, social post, search snippet or screenshot can be stale. Even a genuine old registration document may not show the vendor's current status or operating scope.

Use this order:

1. Type `pmsuryaghar.gov.in` into the browser or follow the link from an official government page.
2. Use the current consumer application, vendor-selection or vendor-comparison route.
3. Check against the state and DISCOM attached to your electricity connection.
4. Record the vendor's exact displayed name, contact details and any identifier or operating area shown.
5. Save a dated screenshot or PDF for your purchase file.
6. Repeat the check before the final commitment if significant time has passed.

Portal labels can change. This guide therefore does not freeze a button-by-button path that may be wrong later.

If you are an installation business trying to register, use the separate [PM Surya Ghar vendor registration guide for installers](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-vendor-registration-installers/). The page you are reading is for homeowners choosing a vendor.

## What the official vendor record proves, and what it does not

**A matching record is evidence that the named business appears in the official scheme vendor system at the time of your check.** It is not a government guarantee covering every person, quote, component or outcome connected to that name.

| A matching official record can support | It does not establish by itself |
| --- | --- |
| The named business appears in the scheme's vendor system | The caller or WhatsApp account genuinely represents that business |
| The portal information published for that vendor at the time checked | The quotation uses the same legal entity or bank beneficiary |
| Contact, profile, rating or performance information that the portal actually displays | The rating methodology, data freshness or suitability for your roof unless the portal explains it |
| The vendor's scheme relationship within the scope shown | Approval of your consumer application, system or subsidy |
| A government route for vendor comparison and grievance handling | A fixed price, installation date, service response time or payment date |

Registration is a process control. Treating it as a quality certificate is the mistake that causes trouble.

MNRE's guidelines divide the remaining decisions among the consumer, vendor and DISCOM. The consumer and vendor agree the design, components and commercial terms. The vendor installs and documents the system. The DISCOM inspects the completed installation and decides the application step under its authority. None of those later decisions is completed merely because a vendor appears in a list.

## Match the portal record to the exact business taking your money

A vendor can trade under a short brand name while its invoice uses a different legal entity. That difference may be legitimate. It still needs a written explanation that joins the records together.

Compare these fields before accepting the quote:

| Identity check | What to compare | Stop and clarify when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business name | Portal name, quotation header, GST registration and invoice name | Spellings or entity suffixes differ without explanation |
| Address | Portal or official contact record, quote and GST details | The quote gives no verifiable business address |
| Contact | Portal contact and the person issuing the quote | The salesperson cannot be confirmed through an official business channel |
| Tax identity | GSTIN on the quote and the legal business name | The GSTIN belongs to another entity or is absent where a tax invoice is expected |
| Payment beneficiary | Account or UPI name and the contracted seller | Payment is requested to an unexplained personal or unrelated account |
| Scheme reference | Any registration identifier, state, DISCOM or scope displayed | The seller offers only a screenshot and refuses an independent portal check |

A personal payment destination is not automatic proof of fraud. It is a mismatch that must be explained and documented before money moves. The same rule applies when an agent, franchisee or subcontractor says another company will invoice the work.

Contact the business through a number or email you obtained independently. Ask it to confirm the salesperson, quote number, site address, amount and payment instructions. Do not hand over your portal password or OTP so someone else can perform a check you can complete yourself. The MNRE guidelines give consumers the right to undertake portal activities without relying on the vendor, although a vendor may assist with entries through the consumer login.

## Verify the quote, not only the vendor

The amended MNRE guidelines say the consumer and vendor mutually decide the system design, components, additional functions and financial terms. They also include a model agreement, while noting that the actual agreement can vary by project.

That means an itemised written scope is part of the verification. Ask for:

- total proposed solar-module capacity in kWp and inverter capacity in kW;
- exact module manufacturer, model, wattage and quantity;
- exact inverter manufacturer, model and quantity;
- mounting structure, cables, protection devices, earthing and monitoring included;
- civil, waterproofing, meter, application and inspection work included or excluded;
- taxes, payment milestones and the rule for additional work;
- who uploads documents and who attends the DISCOM inspection;
- serial-number, test-record and warranty-document handover;
- complaint contact and the installation-service responsibility after commissioning; and
- cancellation, delay and refund terms agreed between the parties.

Keep the contract price separate from any expected CFA. A vendor can explain the process, but cannot guarantee your eligibility, authority approval, inspection result, bank validation, payment date or final disbursement.

The same separation applies to equipment. Government vendor registration does not certify every module or inverter the vendor might quote. Verify the exact models and the current scheme, technical and DISCOM requirements that apply to your project. A brand logo without model numbers is not an equipment schedule.

For a broader commercial review, use the [solar quotation checklist](/blog/solar-quotation-checklist/) after the identity check.

## Confirm the five-year scheme duty in the contract

The amended MNRE guidelines describe a **five-year Comprehensive Maintenance Contract period from commissioning** for systems under this CFA process. They also say the registered vendor must give the consumer the component manufacturers' warranty documents.

That does not make every responsibility interchangeable. Separate them in writing:

| Responsibility | Questions to settle before signing |
| --- | --- |
| Vendor's scheme maintenance duty | What work is included, when the period starts, how a complaint is logged and which exclusions apply under the current scheme and contract |
| Installer workmanship | Who corrects roof, structure, wiring, earthing or installation defects |
| Manufacturer warranty | Which company covers each component, what the written remedy is and where a claim begins |
| DISCOM process | Who prepares documents, attends inspection and corrects a rejected or returned submission |
| Chargeable work | What sits outside the agreed system, maintenance duty or warranty |

Do not accept a verbal claim that one warranty covers everything. The installation contract and each manufacturer's written terms may have different owners, periods, remedies and exclusions. No directory listing supplies those missing terms.

## PM Surya Ghar registration and Qbits authorisation are different

This distinction matters when a quote includes a Qbits inverter.

The **PM Surya Ghar National Portal** is the government scheme route. It is used to check vendor registration for the CFA process and to support consumer application steps.

The **Qbits Authorised Service Partner directory** is a Qbits-owned directory for installation, maintenance, warranty-route and partner-verification enquiries. It can help you check whether a person or business appears in Qbits' current directory and where it is listed. It is not an MNRE, REC or DISCOM registration record.

Use the two checks independently:

1. Check the solar vendor in the official PM Surya Ghar consumer route.
2. Check the Qbits relationship in the Qbits directory when the quote includes Qbits equipment or Qbits service.
3. Match both records to the exact legal entity, contact and quote.
4. Confirm the exact inverter model, installation scope and service responsibility in writing.

A government-registered vendor is not automatically authorised by Qbits. A Qbits directory listing is not proof of PM Surya Ghar registration, subsidy eligibility, government approval or DISCOM acceptance. Neither listing promises availability, coverage, price, response time or project outcome.

## If the records do not match

Do not argue from a screenshot. Pause the payment and build a clean evidence file.

Keep the portal result, quote, GST details, payment request, messages, application reference and the date of each check. Ask the vendor to explain the mismatch in writing. Verify that answer through the official contact or relevant DISCOM and portal route.

If the issue concerns an existing application, use the current portal or DISCOM grievance route for that specific step. Describe observable facts: the name shown, the name on the quote, the amount requested, the date and the answer you need. Save the acknowledgement or tracking reference. Do not assume that the vendor controls a portal, bank, inspection or DISCOM decision.

For an electrical or equipment fault, do not open an inverter or touch exposed conductors to gather evidence. Make the area safe and use a qualified service route.

## Your final check before paying

You should be able to answer yes to every relevant question:

- Did I reach the official National Portal independently?
- Does the vendor appear under the correct business name for the relevant route?
- Does the quotation identify the same legal entity and GSTIN?
- Does the payment beneficiary match the contracted seller or have a documented explanation?
- Are all module and inverter models, quantities and capacities written down?
- Are application, metering, inspection, handover and maintenance responsibilities assigned?
- Are price, taxes, milestones, exclusions and refund terms written down?
- Have I kept CFA expectations separate from the contract price?
- If Qbits equipment is proposed, did I check the Qbits relationship separately?
- Do I know which organisation handles each complaint after installation?

If a Qbits relationship is part of the proposal, [search the current Qbits Authorised Service Partner directory](/authorized-service-partners/) by state and district. Confirm the listing, exact location, service scope and commercial terms before relying on it. Then return to the official PM Surya Ghar portal for the separate government vendor check.
