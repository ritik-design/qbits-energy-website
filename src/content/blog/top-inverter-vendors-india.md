Selecting the right inverter vendors is critical for EPC companies. The inverter acts as the brain of every solar installation — converting DC to AC, managing grid interaction, and monitoring system health.

## Why Choosing the Right Vendor Matters

Poor vendor selection creates cascading problems:

- Delayed warranty claims
- Unreachable support
- Non-compliant products
- Regional performance issues

Strong vendor relationships deliver faster timelines, better pricing, and superior warranty terms.

## Market Landscape

- India targets **500 GW renewable capacity by 2030**
- Market divides into on-grid, hybrid, and off-grid inverters
- All products must carry **BIS certification** and comply with IEC standards
- Domestic manufacturers are closing quality gaps with imported brands

## 8 Critical Evaluation Criteria

1. Manufacturing capabilities and quality control
2. Complete product range (on-grid, hybrid, C&I)
3. Warranty terms and full replacement guarantees
4. Technical support and service infrastructure
5. Efficiency ratings under Indian conditions
6. Smart monitoring and remote diagnostics
7. Delivery timelines and supply chain reliability
8. Regional presence and service networks

## Due Diligence Checklist

- Factory audits with QC observation
- Certification verification (BIS, IEC, IP rating)
- Warranty documentation review
- Financial stability assessment
- EPC reference checks (3+ existing partners)
- Digital tools evaluation
- Sample unit testing under realistic conditions
- Surge protection and weather protection verification

## Key Questions for Vendors

- What component manufacturers supply your inverters?
- What's your annual failure rate from field data?
- How is warranty service delivered in tier-2/3 cities?
- What's the warranty claim turnaround time?
- What monitoring platform features are included?
- What partner program benefits do you offer?
