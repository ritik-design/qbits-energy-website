## Per-watt sochna: kisi bhi quote ko comparable ₹/W mein badlein

Per-watt rate hi woh single number hai jo do alag size ke quotes ko comparable banata hai. Formula simple hai: gross payable divided by watts. Asli galti denominator par hoti hai, kyunki DC watts aur AC watts do alag cheezein hain.

DC watts ka matlab module array ka total Wp hai. AC watts ka matlab inverter ka rated output hai. Rooftop systems mein DC capacity usually AC rating se thodi zyada hoti hai, isliye ek hi quote dono par alag ₹/W deta hai. Jo quote denominator nahi batata, uska per-watt number compare karne layak nahi hai.

**Worked example, labelled as arithmetic on stated illustrative inputs and not a market price observation:**

Stated inputs:

- Gross payable for a complete installed system: ₹1,80,000. Yeh ek illustrative input hai, market price claim nahi.
- Module array: 5 modules of 650 Wp = 3,250 Wp DC.
- Inverter AC rating: 3 kW = 3,000 W AC.

Calculation:

1. ₹/W on DC = ₹1,80,000 divided by 3,250 = **₹55.38 per W DC**.
2. ₹/W on AC = ₹1,80,000 divided by 3,000 = **₹60.00 per W AC**.
3. Dono numbers same quote ke hain. Gap sirf denominator ka hai, about 8 percent.

Ab isko ek published benchmark ke saamne rakhein. PIB (2024) ka formula pehle 2 kW par benchmark cost ka 60 percent aur 2 se 3 kW slice par additional cost ka 40 percent deta hai, 3 kW par capped. Published slabs ₹30,000 for 1 kW, ₹60,000 for 2 kW, aur ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above hain.

**Derived benchmark, labelled as arithmetic from the published formula and not a quoted MNRE figure:**

- ₹30,000 divided by 0.60 = ₹50,000 per kW for the first 2 kW, yaani **₹50.00 per W**.
- Cross-check: ₹60,000 divided by 0.60 divided by 2 = ₹50,000 per kW. Same answer.
- Teesre kW ka increment ₹18,000 hai, aur ₹18,000 divided by 0.40 = ₹45,000 per kW, yaani **₹45.00 per W**.
- 3 kW ka total derived benchmark = 2 times ₹50,000 plus ₹45,000 = ₹1,45,000, yaani ₹1,45,000 divided by 3,000 = **₹48.33 per W**.

Comparison: illustrative quote ka ₹55.38 per W DC benchmark ke ₹48.33 per W se roughly 15 percent upar hai. Yeh apne aap mein overcharging prove nahi karta, kyunki benchmark scheme calculation ke liye hai aur aapke quote ka scope isse zyada ya kam ho sakta hai. Lekin yeh ek defensible reference point deta hai jispar aap dealer se sawal kar sakte hain.

Do caveats yahan zaroori hain. Pehla, yeh benchmark poore rooftop system ka hai, isme modules, structure, cabling, protection aur labour shamil hain. Yeh inverter ka rate nahi hai. Doosra, benchmark 3 kW par cap ho jata hai, isliye usse bade systems ke liye koi published per-kW benchmark nahi hai.

## PM Surya Ghar net rate ko kaise badalta hai, aur 3 kW cap kya karta hai

Central financial assistance aapke gross rate ko reduce nahi karti. Woh aapke net outflow ko reduce karti hai, aur woh bhi 3 kW tak. Yeh distinction zaroori hai, kyunki kai quotes net number ko headline rate ki tarah dikhate hain.

MNRE residential CFA guidelines (2024) ka published structure yeh hai.

| Installed solar module capacity | Standard central assistance |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW ya usse zyada | Up to ₹78,000 |

Cap ka matlab per-watt terms mein saaf dikhta hai. 3 kW par ₹78,000 ka assistance 3,000 W par ₹26.00 per W hai. 6 kW par wahi ₹78,000 ab 6,000 W par ₹13.00 per W ban jata hai. Assistance same hai, par per-watt benefit aadha ho gaya. Yeh arithmetic hai, prediction nahi, aur isi wajah se bade systems ka net per-watt rate badhta hai.

Kuch aur verified points jo net rate ko affect karte hain:

- Special category rates ₹33,000 aur ₹19,800 hain, according to the MNRE operational guidelines.
- CFA rated DC module capacity par compute hoti hai aur inverter size se independent hai. Inverter badalne se assistance nahi badalti.
- DISCOM approval ke baad 15 din ka clock chalta hai, aur grievances par 30 din ka.
- Registered vendor ko commissioning se 5 saal ka Comprehensive Maintenance Contract dena hota hai. Isko quote par likhit maangein.
- 20 March 2026 tak 26.21 lakh rooftop systems aur 9.56 GW install ho chuke the, 32.4 lakh households ko benefit karte hue, according to MNRE and PIB material (March 2026).
- Collateral-free loans repo rate plus 50 basis points par available hain, us date par 5.75 percent per annum, tenure 10 saal tak. Yeh rate repo ke saath float karta hai.
- Grievance call centre 15555 hai, 12 bhashaon mein.

Slab detail [PM Surya Ghar assistance breakdown](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-amount/) mein hai, aur apna approved amount [official portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) par hi verify karein. Rules state aur DISCOM ke hisaab se badalte hain, aur koi slab kisi reader ke liye guaranteed nahi hai.

## DCR aur non-DCR: yeh module ka sawal hai, inverter ka nahi

Yeh section ek common quote trick band karta hai. DCR ka matlab Domestic Content Requirement hai, aur woh domestically manufactured solar cells aur modules par apply hota hai. Inverter is category mein nahi aata.

MNRE ka ALMM List-I solar PV modules ke liye hai, aur List-II cells ke liye, jo 1 June 2026 se in force hai. MNRE inverter ki koi list publish nahi karta. Isliye "ALMM listed inverter" ya "DCR inverter" ek scheme requirement nahi hai, aur na hi ek valid rate justification hai. Agar koi dealer inverter line par DCR premium bol raha hai, to written source maangein.

Module line par tradeoff asli hai. Domestic content sourcing supply base ko restrict karta hai, aur restricted supply base usually cost ko upar rakhta hai. Doosri taraf, kuch programme routes domestic content condition rakhte hain, isliye galat module choose karne se eligibility ja sakti hai. Structural tradeoff yeh hai: module cost bachana aur scheme eligibility, dono ek hi decision mein baithe hain.

Isliye teen cheezein maangein.

1. Module ka exact model code aur uska ALMM List-I registration reference.
2. Cell ka origin declaration, khaas taur par 1 June 2026 ke baad wale List-II context mein.
3. Likhit confirmation ki chuna gaya module aapke applicable scheme conditions ko meet karta hai.

Inverter ke liye same documents na maangein. Inverter par model-specific datasheet, applicable IS aur IEC standard numbers, aur DISCOM ki technical conditions relevant hain. Inverter compliance ka route [Qbits product range](/our-products/) ke datasheets aur DISCOM conditions se hokar jata hai, ALMM se nahi.

## GST rate ko badal deti hai, isliye tax line-wise maangein

Do quotes ka total same hardware par bhi legitimately alag ho sakta hai, aur wajah tax classification hoti hai. Yeh negotiation ka point nahi hai, yeh documentation ka point hai.

Notification 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate), dated 17 September 2025 and effective 22 September 2025, Schedule I entry 437 mein renewable energy devices 5 percent par rakhta hai, aur Schedule II entry 477 mein heading 8504 static converters 18 percent par. Ek inverter ko kaise classify kiya gaya hai, yeh us line ka tax badal deta hai.

Notification 78/2020-Central Tax, dated 15 October 2020, invoices par HSN digits require karta hai. Iska practical fayda aapko milta hai: HSN code maangein aur classification khud verify karein.

Ek myth bhi clear kar dein. CGST Act ke Section 15(2)(e) ke mutabik government subsidies value of supply mein include nahi hoti. Matlab CFA aapke invoice ka GST amount kam nahi karti. Gross invoice apne applicable rates par banega, aur assistance uske baad separately process hoti hai.

Classification detail aur HSN ki full picture [GST on solar inverters](/blog/gst-on-solar-inverters-2026/) mein hai.

## Sabse sasta rate dus saal mein sabse mehanga kyun nikalta hai

Yeh is page ka contrarian section hai. Rate ek din ka number hai, ownership dus saal ka. Sasta quote usually teen jagah se paisa wapas leta hai: chhota warranty term, weaker service depth, aur zyada downtime.

Downtime ko quantify kiya ja sakta hai, aur yeh arithmetic hai, field data nahi.

**Worked example, illustrative inputs, no guarantee of outcome:**

- System 3 kW, assumed specific yield 4.0 kWh per kWp per day. Yeh ek assumption hai, apna site figure use karein.
- Assumed retail tariff ₹7.00 per kWh.

1. Daily generation = 3 kW times 4.0 = 12 kWh.
2. Daily value lost during full downtime = 12 times ₹7.00 = ₹84.
3. Ek 30-day outage = 30 times ₹84 = ₹2,520.
4. Dus saal mein do such outages = ₹5,040 of lost value.

Yeh number chhota lag sakta hai, lekin do cheezein isko badal deti hain. Pehli, agar warranty term chhota hai to paid repair ya replacement ka cost isme add hota hai. Doosri, bada system par yeh linearly badhta hai: same arithmetic 10 kW par ₹280 per day deta hai.

Isliye rate ke saath yeh chaar terms compare karein.

| Ownership factor | Kya likhit maangein | Rate par kaise map karein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warranty term | Base years aur capacity carve-outs | Chhota term ek future cost line hai |
| Warranty type | Full unit replacement ya repair-only | Repair-only mein labour aur wait add hota hai |
| Exclusions | Surge, water ingress, rodent damage, unauthorised work | Excluded fault ka pura cost aapka hai |
| Claim route aur turnaround | Dealer, brand portal, ya service partner, aur written dispatch commitment | Lambi cycle downtime days badhati hai |

Service par koi current written dispatch commitment bhi published nahi hai, to claim approval ke baad kya hoga yeh likhit mein poochhein. Hybrid units par published phrase UPS switching within 10 seconds hai.

Product warranty aur installation warranty do alag cheezein hain. Inverter ka brand warranty aapke cabling ya structure workmanship ko cover nahi karta. Full detail [solar inverter warranty guide](/blog/solar-inverter-warranty/) mein hai.

## Quote par likhit mein kya lena hai

Zubani rate ka koi value nahi hai. Final decision se pehle yeh document set aapke paas hona chahiye, warna aap rate compare nahi kar rahe, aap memory compare kar rahe hain.

1. Itemised quote, line-wise, jismein gross payable total aur valid-until date ho.
2. Har line par HSN code aur applicable GST rate.
3. Bill of quantities, jismein module model, module count, total Wp aur inverter model code ho.
4. Explicit exclusions list, khaas taur par metering, DISCOM charges, liaisoning, freight aur civil work.
5. Warranty statement: term, capacity carve-outs, coverage type, exclusions, claim route.
6. Service statement: nearest authorised partner aur written dispatch commitment.
7. Single line diagram aur string layout, MPPT-wise.
8. Confirmation ki subsidy estimate ko approved payment ke roop mein nahi likha gaya hai.

In aath documents ke saath do quotes ka rupee gap explainable ho jata hai. Uske bina sasta number sirf ek claim hai.

## The Bottom Line

Solar inverter ka rate ek number nahi, ek scope statement hai. Jo buyer scope pehle fix karta hai, uske paas rupee gap ka explanation hota hai, aur negotiation technical ban jati hai.

Per-watt sochna is kaam ka sabse aasan tool hai, bashart ki denominator likha ho. Aur net rate ka sabse bada structural fact yeh hai ki central assistance 3 kW par cap hoti hai, isliye usse upar ki capacity ko consumption aur roof se justify karein, subsidy se nahi.

- Apna required scope ek line mein likhein, phir teen vendors se same bill of quantities par gross payable maangein.
- Har quote ko ₹/W mein convert karein, DC aur AC dono denominators par, aur derived ₹48.33 per W benchmark ke saamne rakhein.
