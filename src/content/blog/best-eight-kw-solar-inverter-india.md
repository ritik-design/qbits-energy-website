## How the three models qualified

An 8 kW search result can mix grid-tied, hybrid, solar PCU and three-phase products. Comparing those in one score table would conceal the most important difference: which circuits remain powered during an outage.

For this shortlist, a manufacturer had to publish an exact **8 kW class, single-phase hybrid** model with a battery input, PV tracking information and a separately stated backup output. Qbits QBH-8KS48P, GoodWe GW8000-ES-C10 and Deye SUN-8K-SG05LP1-EU meet that document threshold. Other credible products exist. Their absence here is not a judgement on quality.

## An 8 kW nameplate cannot size your backup system

The inverter's power rating and the battery's energy capacity answer different questions. Power is the load that may run at one moment. Energy is how long those loads can run. A large inverter with a small battery may deliver a brief high load and then stop.

Create an essential-load schedule before requesting quotes:

1. Mark the circuits that must remain live during an outage.
2. Add their expected simultaneous running watts.
3. Record compressor or pump starting demand separately.
4. Choose the number of backup hours required.
5. Check the battery's usable energy and permitted discharge current at its lowest operating voltage.
6. Have the installer confirm protection, isolation, transfer behaviour and the supported operating mode.

For illustration, a steady 2 kW load for four hours needs **8 kWh delivered to the loads**. If a design assumes 90% conversion and 80% usable battery capacity, the arithmetic is `8 ÷ (0.90 × 0.80) = 11.11 kWh` nominal before any reserve or ageing allowance. Those percentages are assumptions, not a Qbits, Deye or GoodWe outcome. The [battery sizing guide](/blog/battery-sizing-hybrid-solar/) explains the additional checks.

Battery current also matters. Multiplying a nominal 48 V by 190 A gives 9.12 kW on paper, but voltage changes with state of charge and current can be limited by the battery or firmware. That multiplication cannot prove continuous AC output, surge support or a safe battery pairing. It is especially unsafe while the Qbits current discrepancy remains unresolved.

## Documents to request before comparing quotes

Give each seller the same list. A missing answer is an open design item, not a reason to invent a favourable specification.

| Request | What it must settle |
| --- | --- |
| Full inverter code and current regional datasheet | Phase, PV inputs, battery window, backup rating and temperature limits |
| Exact battery make and model | BMS protocol, firmware, cable, charge and discharge limits, usable energy and warranty pairing |
| Single-line diagram | Grid isolation, backup board, protection, metering, CT location and PV and battery paths |
| Backup load schedule | Running load, starting surge, simultaneous use and expected duration |
| Written warranty and service terms | Issuing entity, term, remedy, exclusions, registration, labour, freight and claim route |
| Compliance and interconnection file | Exact model and the current scheme and DISCOM requirements for your location |
| Itemised commercial quote | Inverter, battery, logger, protection, wiring, commissioning and exclusions |

No ordinary grid-tied inverter keeps the house energised when the utility supply fails. The backup board must be designed and tested as a supported system. An installer should witness both grid-loss and grid-restoration behaviour and record the results at handover.
