## What The End Of Scambio Sul Posto Actually Changes

Under SSP, surplus energy fed into the grid was effectively netted against consumption at close to retail value across the settlement period. Timing did not matter much. An installer could size an array against annual consumption and be broadly right.

Ritiro Dedicato is a different product. GSE buys the surplus at 8 to 14 cents per kWh, guaranteed for 20 years, which is attractive as a floor but sits well under what the same kWh costs to buy back. The consequence is that every exported unit now represents value given away, and the return concentrates in the energy used on site.

> **What most Italian quotes will get wrong through 2026.** They will keep sizing to annual consumption, which was correct under SSP and is wrong under Ritiro Dedicato. On a household empty during the day, a system sized to cover annual usage will export most of its output at a fraction of what the evening import costs.

Three design responses follow directly, and none of them can be argued from an annual kWh figure.

**Orientation.** An east-west split flattens the generation curve and lifts self-consumption, often returning more money from less total electricity. Our [east-west roof solar design](/blog/east-west-roof-solar-design/) guide covers the trade-off in detail.

**Storage.** Shifting a kWh from a 10 cent export to an avoided import at Italian retail prices is worth several times the export value. Sizing it properly requires simulating charge and discharge against actual hourly surplus rather than applying a rule of thumb. See our [solar plus storage design software](/blog/solar-plus-storage-design-software/) comparison.

**Load shifting.** Heat pumps, EV charging and water heating scheduled into daylight hours raise self-consumption without hardware, and the proposal should quantify the effect rather than mention it.

The parallel with the Netherlands, where the salderingsregeling ends on 1 January 2027, is exact. Both markets built a large installed base on net metering and are now withdrawing it, and in both the winning installers are the ones who can model hours.

## The Rest Of The Italian Requirement Set

**CEI 0-21 and CEI 0-16.** CEI 0-21 governs connection of generators to the low-voltage distribution network, covering protection settings, anti-islanding and power quality. CEI 0-16 applies at medium and high voltage. These are connection prerequisites checked by the distributor, not optional design considerations.

**The 50% detrazione.** The Restructuring Bonus deduction remains available for primary residences in 2026, on eligible spend up to 96,000 euros per property unit. It is a deduction spread across tax years rather than an upfront grant, so the cashflow profile differs from a rebate and the financial model should reflect that. Regional and municipal schemes may stack on top, and the applicable set should be confirmed per project rather than assumed.

**Yield modelling across a long country.** Italy spans a wide latitude range and Sicily behaves very differently from Lombardy on both irradiance and temperature. A national default will be wrong at both ends, so the simulation needs proper location-specific weather data and a thermal model that handles southern summer conditions.

## Worked Example: 6 kWp Near Bologna, SSP Versus Ritiro Dedicato

An illustrative calculation using published Ritiro Dedicato ranges, not a specific customer project. Inputs: 6 kWp, 1,250 kWh per kWp specific yield, 7,500 kWh generation, 3,600 kWh consumption, import at 28 cents per kWh, RID buyback at 11 cents per kWh, self-consumption 33% without storage.

| Line | Under Scambio sul Posto | Under Ritiro Dedicato | RID plus 6 kWh battery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Generation | 7,500 kWh | 7,500 kWh | 7,500 kWh |
| Self-consumed | 2,475 kWh | 2,475 kWh | 3,240 kWh |
| Exported | 5,025 kWh | 5,025 kWh | 4,260 kWh |
| Value of self-consumption at 28c | EUR 693 | EUR 693 | EUR 907 |
| Value of export | EUR 1,407 netted near retail | EUR 553 | EUR 469 |
| Year 1 benefit | EUR 2,100 | EUR 1,246 | EUR 1,376 |

The same system loses roughly 41% of its annual benefit when SSP is unavailable. The battery recovers a modest share, and the more effective lever on this profile is actually load shifting, because the household's consumption is low relative to generation. That is the useful conclusion: on a low-consumption household the honest recommendation after SSP may be a smaller array rather than a bigger battery, and only an hourly model will tell you which.
