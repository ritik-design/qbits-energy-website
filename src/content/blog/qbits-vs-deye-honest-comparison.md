the current published documents do not prove a universal replacement remedy, a fixed response time, a universal WhatsApp or AI-monitoring feature, or a single grid-voltage window across the range. ## How to compare the electrical design

Start with the array, not the logo. Check maximum string open-circuit voltage at the site's minimum design temperature, operating voltage inside the MPPT window, current per MPPT, permitted strings per tracker, and AC phase and capacity. A qualified designer should validate those values against the exact module and inverter documents.

For hybrid equipment, obtain written confirmation for the exact battery model, nominal voltage, current limit, BMS protocol, backup output, transfer behaviour, black-start behaviour, and operating modes. “Lithium compatible” alone does not prove interoperability.

## How to compare warranty and service

Compare the signed or supplied warranty document, not the marketing headline. Record who accepts a claim, what evidence is required, whether the remedy is repair, replacement, credit, or another option, who pays removal and transport, whether labour is included, and which events are excluded. Ask the seller to identify the document version in the quotation.

Local service also needs written evidence. Confirm the entity responsible for the site, the ticket channel, working hours, parts route, chargeable travel, and any response target. A directory entry or salesperson's message should not be treated as a guaranteed outcome.

## Monitoring and data questions

Confirm which app or portal serves the exact model, whether a logger or SIM is required, who owns the plant account, how often data updates, which faults create alerts, how long history is retained, where data is processed, and how access can be transferred. Qbits publishes iOS and Android app listings, but that alone does not establish every feature for every model.

## Compliance in India

MNRE's current ALMM page publishes lists for solar PV modules and cells, not an inverter list. Verify inverter certificates and scheme or DISCOM requirements separately. Obtain the exact inverter certificates requested by the applicable scheme, tender, utility, DISCOM, or authority. Product, engineering, and scheme owners should approve any statement that a particular model is eligible.

## A fair decision rule

Choose the exact model that passes the electrical design, safety, certificate, warranty, service, monitoring, and commercial checks for the project. If a seller cannot supply a controlling document for a load-bearing promise, record that item as not established and do not award it points.
