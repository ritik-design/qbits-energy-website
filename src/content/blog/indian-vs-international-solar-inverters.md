Whether an inverter is Indian or imported is a weak signal on its own. What decides the outcome is the exact model's datasheet and certificates, the written warranty and who honours it, the service route for your pincode, and the dated landed price. This guide sets out what to compare and how to compare it, and it quotes no price gap, response time or lead time because none has a dated source we can cite.

> [!KEYTAKEAWAYS]
> - Peak and weighted efficiency, IP rating and operating range are on each datasheet; compare them model against model rather than assuming one origin is ahead.
> - No dated source supports a general Indian-versus-imported price gap, so ask for dated, like-for-like landed quotes for both options.
> - The longest standard inverter term we can verify in print is 10 years (Havells Enviro GTi datasheets), against 5 years on the Deye and Growatt hybrid datasheets and 7 years on Polycab's 2 kW to 125 kW range. Compare the remedy and exclusions, not the years.
> - Service time and delivery time are not published in comparable form; get a named service partner, a committed time from ticket to fix and a delivery date in writing.
> - International brands still make sense where a lender, utility or specification names a model, or where a technical requirement is met only by that model.

This guide covers what to compare in performance, components, price and total cost of ownership, warranty and support, monitoring, India-specific engineering checks, lead time, and the decision framework for each project type.

## Compare Models, Not Countries

Indian inverter makers differ widely in manufacturing depth. Some assemble from imported sub-assemblies, some design and build more of the product in India, and some rebrand imported units, as our [made-in-India solar inverters](/blog/made-in-india-solar-inverters-2026/) guide explains with a three-tier test. Imported brands differ in the same way. Component brands, test coverage and certifications vary by maker and model, so ask for the bill of materials and the quality documents for the unit you are quoted. The remaining differentiators are the written warranty, the service route, the India grid-code setting and the dated price.

## Performance Comparison

Table: Datasheet items to compare on the exact Indian and imported models you are quoted

| Metric | Where to read it | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Peak efficiency | Datasheet efficiency table | Peak figure and the measurement standard |
| Weighted efficiency (Euro/CEC) | Datasheet efficiency table | Which weighting is quoted; do not mix Euro and CEC |
| Start-up voltage | Datasheet input section | Compare against your string's morning voltage |
| Thermal management and derating | Datasheet derating curve | Output at your site's summer ambient temperature |
| IP rating | Datasheet and nameplate | The exact rating for the exact model; ratings differ across a range |
| DC oversizing | Datasheet maximum DC input | Maximum DC power, current and voltage limits |
| Operating temperature | Datasheet environmental section | Full-load temperature range, not only the survival range |
| MPPT voltage range | Datasheet input section | Fit with the string design for your modules |

Specifications can be close between leading models of both kinds, and they differ by model, so a single comparison across all Indian and all imported inverters would be misleading.

### Total cost of ownership

Beyond the unit price:

- Origin can affect duty, freight, currency and lead-time exposure, but the result depends on the legal seller, supply route, inventory and contract.
- A domestic brand name does not prove domestic manufacture, local stock, faster delivery or a particular tax treatment.
- Warranty administration and spare-parts availability must be checked for the exact seller and model.

For a commercial installation, compare dated landed quotes and delivery commitments instead of applying a generic "Indian" or "international" discount. Tax and customs treatment requires invoice-specific advice from a qualified professional.

## India-Specific Engineering Checks

Any inverter sold in India, Indian or imported, should be checked against the conditions it will face. Read these from the exact model's datasheet and manual, and ask the seller to confirm in writing:

- **AC voltage and frequency window** in the India grid-code profile, and whether it matches your DISCOM's connection conditions
- **Thermal derating** at your site's summer ambient temperature
- **Enclosure rating** and the rated protection for monsoon, coastal and dust-storm conditions
- **Start-up voltage** against your string's morning voltage
- **Coating and corrosion protection** if the site is coastal or humid
- **Altitude derating** for Himalayan installations
- **ALMM scope:** MNRE's current page publishes PV module and cell lists, not an inverter list. Verify the exact module/cell models and check inverter documents separately.

## Decision Framework: When to Choose Each

### An Indian manufacturer may suit when

- The exact model's documents, written warranty and named service partner for your town are stronger than the alternatives
- The seller commits in writing to stock and delivery for your project
- The project is a PM Surya Ghar or PM-KUSUM subsidy project, and the model's certificates meet the scheme's and the DISCOM's requirements (ALMM applies to modules)
- Commercial buyers want the written warranty term to span the depreciation window they plan for

### An international brand may suit when

- A lender or utility names an approved-OEM list for a large project
- A client specification names a particular imported model
- A technical requirement, such as a particular grid-forming or advanced control function, is met only by a specific model
- Multi-country corporate standardisation requires one platform
