A connected inverter should be judged at the exact model and installation level. An app-store listing shows that an application exists. It does not show that every inverter includes the required logger, every feature works on every SKU, or every alert and data field is available without configuration.

## What to verify before purchase

| Item | What to ask for |
| --- | --- |
| Compatible interface | Exact inverter, logger, dongle, meter, CT and firmware combination |
| Network | Wi-Fi band, Ethernet, cellular, SIM and offline behaviour |
| Data | Power, energy, voltage, current, battery data and update interval |
| Alerts | Supported faults, delivery channel, recipients and delay |
| Account | Owner access, installer access, transfer and recovery process |
| History | Retention period, export format and deletion process |
| Privacy | Data controller, hosting location, sharing and account closure |
| Support | Who diagnoses an alert and whether a site visit is chargeable |

## Commissioning handover

The owner should receive the plant account, device identifiers, login recovery route, logger details, network requirements, and a demonstration using live site data. Confirm that the displayed inverter and meter values correspond to the installed equipment. Record who retains installer or fleet-level access.

## What monitoring can and cannot show

Monitoring can show reported operating data and fault states from connected equipment. It cannot, by itself, confirm the cause of a fault, validate wiring safety, replace insulation or earthing tests, or guarantee that an alert will be delivered. Missing data may result from the logger, router, internet connection, account, server, meter, or inverter.

## Comparing apps and portals

Use the same live acceptance test for every shortlisted system: verify setup time, update interval, data completeness, alert behaviour, history, export, account transfer, recovery, and operation after an internet outage. Do not score screenshots or marketing descriptions as measured field performance.
