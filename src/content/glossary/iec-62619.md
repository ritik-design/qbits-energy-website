## What is IEC 62619

IEC 62619 is the international safety standard for secondary lithium cells and batteries used in industrial applications, including stationary battery energy storage systems (BESS) for solar, telecom backup and uninterruptible power supplies. The standard covers cells, modules and battery systems containing lithium chemistries (LFP, NMC, NCA, LCO, LTO).

Published by IEC TC 21 (Secondary Cells and Batteries), the standard specifies safety requirements for industrial battery use, complementing IEC 62133 (portable) and IEC 62660 (automotive). It includes electrical, mechanical, thermal, environmental and propagation tests.

For Indian solar storage, the equivalent standard is IS 16893, harmonised with IEC 62619 for lithium chemistries. BIS certification under IS 16893 is required for stationary battery products. Hybrid inverter battery packs from major brands typically carry IEC 62619 certification; the [BMS decode of Qbits hybrid inverter compatibility](/blog/bms-hybrid-solar-inverter-explained/) notes it as a mandated type-test requirement for overcharge and overdischarge protection.

## Why IEC 62619 matters

Battery safety. Sets the engineering baseline for stationary lithium storage.

Customer protection. Recourse against certified products.

Insurance. BESS insurance requires IEC 62619 or equivalent.

Hybrid solar adoption. Residential storage rests on certified safety, a factor weighed alongside cycle life in the [LiFePO4 vs NMC battery comparison](/blog/lifepo4-vs-nmc-solar-battery-india/) for Indian conditions.

Recall prevention. Pre-market testing catches design defects.

## Test categories

| Category | Test |
|---|---|
| Electrical | Overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit |
| Mechanical | Crush, impact, vibration |
| Environmental | Temperature cycling, humidity |
| Functional | BMS function, communication |
| Thermal | External fire, thermal abuse |
| Propagation | Single-cell thermal runaway containment |

## Technical deep dive

### Key tests

| Test | Method |
|---|---|
| External short circuit | Short cell terminals, observe response |
| Overcharge | Charge above rated voltage |
| Over-discharge | Discharge below cutoff |
| Crush | Mechanical pressure until failure |
| Penetration | Nail through cell |
| Thermal | Heat to thermal runaway |
| Cell propagation | Single-cell heat, observe pack |
| Vibration | Per shipping standards |
| Drop | Mechanical impact |

### Pass criteria

- No fire (LFP), or controlled venting (NMC)
- No explosion
- No external thermal runaway propagation
- BMS responds appropriately to abuse

### Chemistry-specific behaviour

| Chemistry | Test outcome |
|---|---|
| LFP | No fire under most abuse conditions |
| NMC | Controlled venting, potential flame |
| LTO | Very stable, no thermal runaway |
| Sodium-ion | Variable, emerging standards |

## Compliance pathway

1. Manufacturer engages accredited test lab.
2. Sample cells and packs submitted.
3. Test sequence over 4 to 8 months.
4. Compliance report issued.
5. Factory inspection.
6. Certificate granted.
7. Surveillance and re-certification.

## Common compliance problems

Single-cell propagation failure during thermal runaway test.

External short circuit causing pack damage.

BMS communication failure under fault.

Mechanical abuse leading to fire.

## Best practices

Use only IEC 62619 / IS 16893 certified packs.

Verify certificate validity on date of supply.

Demand cell-level test reports for premium installations.

Plan cabinet ventilation per battery thermal envelope.

Use compatible BMS firmware versions.

## Indian market context

IS 16893 is the Indian harmonised standard.

BIS certification under IS 16893 is mandatory for stationary battery storage sold in India.

ALMM has a battery storage list parallel to inverter and module lists.

Major Indian BESS providers (Pylon Tech, BYD, Goodwe Storage, Tata Power Solar) carry IEC 62619 certification, and this [Pylontech battery review](/blog/pylontech-battery-india-review/) cites the standard's 270°C LFP thermal runaway onset threshold as the reason the chemistry suits enclosed residential installations in India's heat.

PLI for ACC drives domestic Indian cell manufacturing capacity.

IEC 62619 is the international safety standard for industrial lithium battery storage. Indian compliance is through IS 16893 / BIS certification. The standard tests cells and packs against electrical, mechanical, thermal and propagation abuse. Hybrid inverter battery packs and utility BESS require IEC 62619 / IS 16893 certification. Quality of certification directly affects insurance, warranty and operational safety.
