## What is IEC 61215

IEC 61215 is the international standard for design qualification and type approval of terrestrial photovoltaic modules, published by the International Electrotechnical Commission. The standard defines a sequence of tests that PV modules must pass to demonstrate adequate durability and electrical safety for 25+ year service life under typical outdoor conditions.

The standard covers both crystalline silicon (mono and poly) and thin-film modules. Tests include visual inspection, electrical performance measurement at STC and NOCT, insulation resistance, wet leakage current, thermal cycling, humidity-freeze cycling, damp heat exposure, UV exposure, mechanical static load, hail impact, hot spot endurance and bypass diode thermal test.

For Indian solar, the equivalent national standard is IS 14286, harmonised with IEC 61215. BIS certification under IS 14286 is required for any module sold in India. ALMM listing requires IS 14286 compliance as a prerequisite.

## Why IEC 61215 matters

Module durability. Sets baseline for 25+ year service.

Market access. Required for sale in most international markets.

ALMM eligibility. Required for Indian subsidised projects.

Insurance. Insurers require certified modules.

Customer protection. Warranty enforceability depends on compliance.

Quality benchmark. Distinguishes qualified products from unverified ones.

## Test sequence overview

| Test | Stress |
|---|---|
| Visual inspection | Manufacturing defects |
| Performance at STC | Baseline electrical output |
| Insulation test | Dielectric integrity |
| Wet leakage current | Moisture protection |
| Performance at NOCT | Operating condition output |
| Performance at low irradiance | Low-light response |
| Outdoor exposure | Real-world short-term |
| Hot spot endurance | Partial shade resilience |
| UV preconditioning | Encapsulant stability |
| Thermal cycling 50 + 200 | Thermal expansion stress |
| Humidity-freeze 10 | Freeze-thaw cycle |
| Damp heat 1000 hours | Long-term humidity |
| Mechanical load | Snow/wind load |
| Hail impact | Hail resistance |
| Bypass diode thermal | Diode reliability |
| Final performance check | Post-stress degradation |

## Technical deep dive

### Stress test conditions

| Test | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Thermal cycling | -40°C to +85°C, 200 cycles |
| Humidity-freeze | 85% RH at +85°C and -40°C, 10 cycles |
| Damp heat | 85°C, 85% RH, 1000 hours |
| UV preconditioning | 15 kWh/m² UV |
| Mechanical load | 2400 Pa (front), 2400 Pa (back) static |
| Hail impact | 25 mm hailstone at 23 m/s |
| Hot spot | Partial shade, peak power for 1 hour |

### Pass criteria

After full test sequence:
- Power output retention: ≥ 95% of initial Pmax
- No visible major defects
- Insulation resistance maintained
- Wet leakage current within limits

## Compliance pathway

1. Manufacturer engages accredited test lab.
2. Sample modules submitted (typically 10 to 12 modules).
3. Test sequence performed over 3 to 6 months.
4. Compliance report issued.
5. Factory inspection (additional step for some certifications).
6. Certificate granted.
7. Re-certification on design changes.

## Common compliance problems

Hot spot endurance failure on partial-shade test.

Damp heat causing back-sheet delamination.

Hail impact cracking glass.

Mechanical load deformation.

Insulation failure after thermal cycling.

## Indian market context

IS 14286 is the Indian harmonised standard.

BIS certification under IS 14286 is mandatory for Indian sale.

ALMM List I requires IS 14286 compliance.

Major test labs in India: NISE, TUV Rheinland, TUV SUD, Intertek, UL India, Bureau Veritas.

PM Surya Ghar requires ALMM-listed, IS 14286 certified modules.

## Key takeaways

IEC 61215 is the foundational international qualification standard for PV modules. Indian compliance is through IS 14286 / BIS certification. The standard tests durability through accelerated stress sequences targeting 25-year service. ALMM listing requires compliance. Certification takes 3 to 6 months and costs ₹15 to ₹40 lakh per module type. Modules without IEC 61215 / IS 14286 certification are not legal for Indian sale.
