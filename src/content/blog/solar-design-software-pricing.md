## What Is Gated Behind Upper Tiers

A pricing comparison is only useful if you can see what each tier ships. Here is the gating pattern by platform.

### SurgePV
Every paid plan ships: AI 3D roof modeling, [8,760-hour shade analysis](https://surgepv.com/shadow-analysis), bankable yield, [solar financial modeling](https://surgepv.com/generation-financial-tool), Clara AI, [AutoCAD-compatible solar design](https://surgepv.com/solar-autocad-integration), [branded solar proposals](https://surgepv.com/solar-proposals). Pricing scales by team size, not by feature.

### Aurora Solar
Entry tier omits AI Mode and Sales Mode, both of which are separately priced add-ons that Aurora does not list publicly. Sales Mode adds proposal functionality. Deeper shading simulation is tier-gated, so confirm which simulation tier your lender will accept before choosing a plan.

### HelioScope
Entry caps project count. Module-level shading and advanced layout tools sit on mid and higher tiers.

### OpenSolar
OpenSolar's model is a free core platform with paid extensions, so check which of the templates, integrations, and advanced features you rely on sit inside the free tier and which are chargeable extensions.

## Hidden Costs to Watch For

1. **AI add-ons**: Aurora AI Mode is a recurring cost on top of the base plan
2. **Paid extensions**: check which OpenSolar features you need sit outside the free core
3. **Volume-based fees**: Scanifly's pricing model scales with project count
4. **Drone operating cost**: Scanifly workflows
5. **Windows machine requirement**: PVsyst, PV*SOL
6. **Separate proposal tool**: HelioScope, PVsyst
7. **Separate CRM**: most tools (SurgePV pairs with [QuickEstimate](https://quickestimate.co/))
8. **Onboarding and training**: ask enterprise-quoted vendors such as PVcase and RatedPower whether either is charged separately
9. **Project allowance caps**: HelioScope entry, some Aurora plans
10. **Lender-specific report requirements**: occasional parallel PVsyst run
