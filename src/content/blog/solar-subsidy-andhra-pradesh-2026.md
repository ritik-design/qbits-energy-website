## What PM Surya Ghar Provides in Andhra Pradesh: The Central Subsidy

The central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is administered through [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) at nationally standardised rates:

Table: PM Surya Ghar central subsidy by system size

| System Size | Rate | Total Central Subsidy |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Andhra Pradesh State Solar Policy: NREDCAP's Framework

NREDCAP (New and Renewable Energy Development Corporation of Andhra Pradesh) is the state nodal agency for renewable energy. The state's Integrated Clean Energy Policy 2024 (G.O.Ms.No.37, Energy Department, 30 October 2024, valid for five years) covers utility, commercial and distributed solar, and supports rooftop generation through central schemes such as PM Surya Ghar. For residential consumers in 2026:

- PM Surya Ghar applications run through the national portal and your DISCOM, not through a separate state scheme
- We found no separate residential cash subsidy from the state on top of the central scheme; confirm with NREDCAP and your DISCOM
- NREDCAP's [nredcap.in](https://www.nredcap.in/) portal provides state policy orders and updates

AP's industrial solar sector (particularly in the Krishna–Guntur belt and Visakhapatnam's industrial corridor) has created a well-developed installer network that benefits residential buyers in adjacent areas. ## The 5-Pillar AP Subsidy Stack Framework

Five sequential steps define the successful PM Surya Ghar claim process in Andhra Pradesh.

### The 5-Pillar AP Subsidy Stack

<!-- steps -->
1. **PM Surya Ghar portal registration**: Register at [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) with your DISCOM (APEPDCL, APCPDCL or APSPDCL) consumer number, Aadhaar details, and bank information before installation begins. Do not allow any installer to begin physical work before this step is complete.

2. **DISCOM technical feasibility**: Your DISCOM reviews the application and, where needed, visits the site through its divisional office. No sourced AP-wide timeline exists for this stage; ask the DISCOM for its current service standard and track the application on the portal.

3. **Installation**: After feasibility approval, the vendor installs the system. Installation time depends on system size, roof access and the vendor's schedule.

4. **Net meter commissioning**: Post-installation inspection by the DISCOM, followed by the meter change and the commissioning report. This is the stage where delays most often sit, so ask the vendor to chase it through the divisional office if it stalls.

5. **Subsidy disbursement**: After the DISCOM uploads the commissioning report to the PM Surya Ghar portal, the central subsidy goes to your bank account. Secondary reports put the scheme's target at about 30 days from the commissioning report (confirm the current standard on the portal). AP does not have a parallel state disbursement.

## Andhra Pradesh's Three DISCOMs: Coverage, Process, and Contact

Andhra Pradesh's electricity distribution is split among three companies: APEPDCL (Eastern), APCPDCL (Central) and APSPDCL (Southern). Districts were reorganised in 2022, so the lists below can lag newer changes and an older bill may name a predecessor district. Treat your own electricity bill as the deciding document. Applications for PM Surya Ghar go through the national portal for all three.

### APEPDCL: Eastern Andhra Pradesh (Visakhapatnam)

APEPDCL (Eastern Power Distribution Company of Andhra Pradesh) lists these districts on its own website: Srikakulam, Vizianagaram, Visakhapatnam, East Godavari, Eluru, Alluri Sitharamaraju, Anakapalli, West Godavari, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Konaseema, Kakinada and Parvathipuram Manyam. Visakhapatnam (Vizag) is the largest city in this zone and has a well-developed solar installer market.

APEPDCL's consumer portal: [apeasternpower.com](https://www.apeasternpower.com/), which has a PM Surya Ghar solar page and a net-metering explainer. Applications are primarily routed through the PM Surya Ghar national portal. Contact your divisional office for physical follow-up.

> [!IMPORTANT] Coastal AP: specify IP66
> **Coastal note**: Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, and Machilipatnam face direct Bay of Bengal exposure. Salt-laden sea breeze accelerates corrosion on standard IP65 enclosures. IP66 inverters with marine-grade cable connections are the appropriate specification for these locations.

### APCPDCL: Central Andhra Pradesh (Vijayawada)

APCPDCL (Central Power Distribution Corporation of Andhra Pradesh) has its headquarters in Vijayawada. Wikipedia's power-sector page lists its districts as Krishna, NTR, Guntur, Bapatla, Palnadu, Prakasam and Markapuram. APCPDCL's own website, [apcpdcl.in](https://apcpdcl.in/), did not load when we checked it on 14 October 2026, so confirm the district list and the solar application steps with the DISCOM directly. Vijayawada and Guntur fall in this zone, not in APEPDCL's.

### APSPDCL: Southern Andhra Pradesh and Rayalaseema (Tirupati)

APSPDCL (Southern Power Distribution Corporation of Andhra Pradesh) has its headquarters in Tirupati. Its website lists circles named Ananthapur, Annamayya, Chittoor, Kadapa, Kurnool, Nandyal, Nellore, Srisathyasai and Tirupati, and Wikipedia's power-sector page lists the matching nine districts. This is a geographically large service area spanning Nellore on the coast to the semi-arid Rayalaseema region. Kurnool and Anantapur in Rayalaseema receive among the highest solar irradiance in AP (5.5 to 5.8 kWh/m²/day), making them particularly strong solar economics zones.

APSPDCL consumer portal: [apspdcl.in](https://www.apspdcl.in/). Its site links PM Surya Ghar registration, a solar applications page and an online solar agreement signing service. Contact your divisional office for physical follow-up.

## A Visakhapatnam Homeowner Case Example: 3 kW, APEPDCL (Illustrative)

This is an illustrative scenario with assumed figures, not a real customer. Take an independent house in Visakhapatnam, served by APEPDCL, a few kilometres from the coastline. Assumed monthly APEPDCL bill: ₹3,400 (400–460 units per month with air conditioning).

> [!EXAMPLE] Visakhapatnam 3 kW (illustrative scenario)
> **Costs and subsidies** (all assumed):
> - Gross system cost: ₹2,00,000 (installed with coastal-grade specifications)
> - Central PM Surya Ghar subsidy: ₹78,000
> - Net out-of-pocket: ₹1,22,000 (homeowners who prefer not to pay this upfront can spread it through [EMI financing](/blog/solar-on-emi/))
>
> **Energy economics** (Visakhapatnam, ~5.1 kWh/m²/day):
> - Annual generation estimate: approximately 4,470 kWh (3 × 5.1 × 365 × 0.80 = 4,467)
> - Annual savings at 85% self-consumption (₹7.00/unit blended, assumed): approximately ₹26,600
> - Annual export earnings (15% at an assumed ₹3.00/unit): approximately ₹2,010
> - Total annual benefit: approximately ₹28,600
> - Post-subsidy payback: approximately 4.3 years (₹1,22,000 ÷ ₹28,600)
>
> **Timeline**: This example does not assume a processing time. Ask your DISCOM for its current service standard, then track registration, feasibility, inspection, meter change and subsidy credit on the portal.

> [!FORMULA] Payback for the same 3 kW system in other AP cities (illustrative)
> Payback = ₹1,22,000 ÷ (annual kWh × (85% × ₹7.00 + 15% × ₹3.00)) = ₹1,22,000 ÷ (annual kWh × ₹6.40). Kurnool: 4,993 kWh gives ₹31,955 a year and 3.8 years. Visakhapatnam: 4,467 kWh gives ₹28,589 a year and 4.3 years. Rajahmundry: 4,380 kWh gives ₹28,032 a year and 4.4 years.

**Key observation**: For a site this close to the sea, ask the installer to quote an IP66 inverter and show the price difference against an IP65 unit on the written quote. IP66 gives stronger protection against water jets than IP65, which matters when salt-laden sea breeze reaches the enclosure.

## Common Mistakes AP Solar Applicants Make

- **Not specifying IP66 in coastal locations**: The IP65 vs IP66 distinction is commercially significant in Vizag, Kakinada, and Machilipatnam. The [IP66 vs IP65 comparison](/blog/ip65-vs-ip66-solar-inverters-weather-protection-guide/) quantifies why the additional cost is justified in coastal environments.
- **Ignoring cyclone wind load requirements**: AP's coastline is within the Bay of Bengal cyclone belt. Mounting structures should be rated for the appropriate wind speed zone. Ask vendors for IS/IEC wind load certification on proposed mounting systems, or commission independent structural and civil engineering calculations for larger or coastal-exposed installations.
- **Applying to the wrong DISCOM**: APEPDCL, APCPDCL and APSPDCL have distinct geographic coverage, and Vijayawada and Guntur are not in APEPDCL's area. Your electricity bill is the only reliable guide.
- **Not verifying ALMM status for proposed panels**: AP's active C&I solar market means many panel models are available; not all are ALMM-listed. Verify at the [MNRE ALMM list](https://mnre.gov.in/almm-list/) before signing.

The [net metering India complete guide](/blog/net-metering-india-complete-guide/) explains the net metering billing mechanics that APERC's framework sets for all three DISCOMs (APEPDCL, APCPDCL and APSPDCL).
