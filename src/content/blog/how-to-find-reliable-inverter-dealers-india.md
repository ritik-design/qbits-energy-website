For solar EPCs scaling operations across India's expanding renewable energy market, selecting trustworthy inverter dealers is critical to project success and business reputation. This guide provides systematic evaluation criteria for vetting dealers.

## 1. Why Dealer Selection Matters

- Inverters represent **15–20% of total project costs**
- Poor selection causes warranty delays, inventory shortages, inadequate technical support
- Regional availability is crucial for multi-state operations

## 2. Technical Expertise

Evaluate:

- Understanding of BIS/IEC certification requirements
- Knowledge of on-grid vs hybrid system differences
- Technical training capabilities for installation teams

## 3. Warranty and After-Sales Service

- Review warranty claim processing workflows and turnaround
- Assess service centre network coverage
- Verify replacement part availability and response time commitments

## 4. Inventory and Supply Chain

- Stock availability across inverter models
- Lead times for bulk orders
- Logistics capabilities and seasonal demand management

## 5. Manufacturer Relationships

Authorised dealers maintain **direct partnerships with inverter manufacturers**, receiving official appointment letters and ensuring:

- Genuine products
- Valid warranties
- Direct claim processing

## 6. Pricing Structure

- Compare comprehensive costs, not just unit prices
- Negotiate volume discounts and credit terms
- Clarify all hidden charges upfront (logistics, GST handling, installation support)

## 7. Regional Presence

- Map dealer locations against your target markets
- Verify service capabilities in rural and urban areas
- Confirm local language support for end-user communication

## 8. Digital Capabilities

- Online ordering with real-time inventory tracking
- Digital warranty systems
- Multiple communication channels (WhatsApp, email, phone)

## Vetting Checklist

### Documentation

- GST registration
- Manufacturer authorisation letter (current)
- BIS certifications for stocked models
- Insurance documentation

### References

Contact 3+ current EPC clients. Verify:

- Delivery reliability
- Warranty claim experiences
- Technical support quality
- Payment terms honoured

### Financial Stability

- Review 2–3 years of financials
- Check credit ratings
- Verify GST and tax compliance

### Trial Orders

Start with 5–10 units to test:

- Product authenticity and quality
- Delivery accuracy and speed
- Documentation completeness
- Initial service responsiveness

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Reluctance to provide documentation
- Vague technical answers or pressure tactics
- Counterfeit products without genuine warranties
- Overpromising delivery timelines
- Lack of operational transparency
- Prices substantially below market rates

## Building Strategic Partnerships

Move beyond transactional relationships through:

- Regular business reviews
- Shared project pipeline visibility
- Collaborative technical training
- Structured feedback mechanisms
- Joint marketing initiatives

## Closing

The right dealer relationship compounds over years through better availability, faster warranty resolution, and stronger technical support. Apply the vetting criteria rigorously upfront, document everything, and treat dealer selection as a long-term strategic decision — not a procurement transaction.
