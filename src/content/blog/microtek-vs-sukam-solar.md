## The Two Brands at a Glance

Microtek and Su-Kam are both Indian power backup pioneers, but they sit in very different positions today. Microtek remains an active, stable company with a solar range it updates regularly. Su-Kam, founded in 1988 and once India's most innovative inverter brand, went through insolvency proceedings around 2019 and now operates at a fraction of its former scale.

Microtek's strength has always been distribution. Its products reach electrical shops in district towns where global brands simply do not stock. Su-Kam's strength was product innovation; it built some of India's first solar PCUs (Power Conditioning Units) and held over 25 patents at its peak. The distinction matters because you are not just buying hardware. You are buying ten to fifteen years of service, spares, and warranty honouring.

The founder of Su-Kam has described how the company's dealer network dissolved during the insolvency period, per [Inverter Man of India](https://invertermanofindia.com/posts/blog/what-really-happened-to-su-kam), 2026. Long-standing dealers were left stranded. That is not a reason to avoid a working Su-Kam product at the right price. It is a reason to treat after-sales verification as your first checkpoint.

## Subsidy and Compliance: The ALMM Gate

This section matters more than any spec comparison if you are buying a subsidised system. Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, the central subsidy pays ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 per kW for the third. It caps at ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above, according to the [PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), 2026.

Three rules trip buyers up:

1. **The inverter must be ALMM-listed.** Listing is model-specific, not brand-wide. Microtek has grid-tie models on the list. Always ask for the ALMM certificate number for the exact model quoted, and verify it on the [MNRE portal](https://mnre.gov.in/).
2. **The system must be grid-connected.** Off-grid PCUs from either brand do not qualify, regardless of ALMM status. A Microtek or Su-Kam PCU with batteries is an off-grid or backup system in the scheme's eyes.
3. **The vendor must be registered.** The subsidy application flows through the PM Surya Ghar portal via an empanelled installer.

For buyers in power-cut areas who want both subsidy and backup, the compliant route is a grid-connected hybrid inverter, which qualifies when ALMM-listed. This is a real gap in both legacy brands' ranges and one reason hybrid-first brands have grown so fast since 2024.

## How to Verify Before You Buy Either Brand

Whichever way you lean, run these five checks with your dealer before paying an advance:

1. Ask for the exact model number and check its ALMM status on the MNRE portal if you want subsidy.

3. Get the warranty term in writing, including whether it is full replacement or repair-only, and who honours it locally.
4. Ask the dealer how many warranty claims they have processed for that brand in the past 12 months.
5. Compare at least one quote from a modern specialist brand on the same capacity, so the warranty-per-year math is visible.
