**An eligible Kerala household can apply for the central PM Surya Ghar rooftop-solar assistance through the [official national portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/).** The published central calculation reaches a usual cap of ₹78,000 at 3 kW of eligible solar capacity, subject to the current rules. KSEB handles the local electricity-connection steps. An installer quote should show the full project cost and any assistance separately.

The figures below do not include an extra Kerala cash top-up. Verify any advertised state benefit against a current government order before budgeting for it. Request a site-specific generation estimate and your current tariff before estimating payback.

## Central assistance available in Kerala

The [MNRE residential CFA guidelines](https://mnre.gov.in/en/notice/operational-guidelines-for-implementation-of-the-component-central-financial-assistance-to-residential-consumers-of-pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli-yojana/) describe the standard central calculation for eligible households:

| Eligible solar-module capacity | Standard central assistance |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW and above | Up to ₹78,000 |

The assistance is for a qualifying residential installation. It is not a separate payment for each panel, inverter or battery. Confirm your own application and [scheme eligibility](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-yojana-complete-guide/) before signing a purchase contract.

## What KSEB and the vendor need to confirm

1. Use the national portal to identify the serving DISCOM and start the current application process.
2. Ask KSEB which feasibility, connection, inspection and meter steps apply to your consumer number and proposed capacity. Get any approval or objection in writing.
3. Verify that the installer is currently registered for the relevant area. Our [vendor guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) explains how to check the registration.
4. Request the exact solar PV module models and their applicable [MNRE ALMM listing](https://mnre.gov.in/en/approved-list-of-models-and-manufacturers-almm/). That page lists modules and cells, **not inverters**. Ask separately for the inverter's grid and safety documents required by KSEB.
5. Retain the itemised quote, invoice, model numbers, commissioning records and bank details used in the application.

Application and payment times depend on the stage, document quality, local workload and inspection scheduling. Do not treat a general article's “90 to 120 days” estimate as a KSEB service guarantee. If your application stalls, check the [status guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-status-check/) and the remark in your portal account.

## Estimate savings with your own roof and tariff

Kerala's monsoon, roof orientation, shade and local weather affect generation. A state average cannot replace a site-specific estimate. Ask the installer for a monthly generation model that states the weather dataset, array orientation, shading and system-loss assumptions. Compare that estimate with your own KSEB bill's energy use and applicable tariff slabs. Export credits and the final bill treatment depend on the current local rules.

The [irradiance-data guide](/blog/solar-irradiance-data-india-statewise/) explains how to obtain location-specific inputs. Compare bids with the [solar quotation checklist](/blog/solar-quotation-checklist/) rather than accepting a quoted payback without the underlying calculation.

## Does Kerala offer an extra state benefit?

This article does not assume one. If a vendor advertises a Kerala or ANERT add-on, ask for the current official order, applicant conditions and payment path. [ANERT](https://anert.gov.in/) is the state renewable-energy agency; its current notices and the national portal are better references than an undated sales graphic. Keep any possible state benefit outside your confirmed project economics until you have verified eligibility.
