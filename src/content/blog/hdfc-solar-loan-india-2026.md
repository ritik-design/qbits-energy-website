The Green Home Loan route unlocks lower interest rates and longer tenures, while the Personal Loan route is faster but more expensive. The distinction turns entirely on whether you own the property where the solar system will be installed.

## What Is the HDFC Green Home Loan and Does It Cover Solar?

HDFC's Green Home Loan is a product variant within HDFC's home loan portfolio designed for properties that meet green building standards, such as IGBC or GRIHA ratings. However, many HDFC branches also extend this facility (or a top-up on an existing home loan) to finance solar panel installation on an owner-occupied residential property, treating it as a home improvement loan.

### Green Home Loan: The Owner-Occupied Route

The Green Home Loan route is available to existing HDFC home loan customers as a top-up, and to new applicants who are purchasing or already own a home. Key parameters as of mid-2026 (subject to change, verify at the HDFC website or a branch):

| Parameter | Green Home Loan / Top-Up | Personal Loan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applicable property | Owner-occupied residential | Any (including rented) |
| Interest rate (indicative) | ~8.5%–10% p.a. | ~10.5%–24% p.a. |
| Loan amount | ₹1 Lakh – ₹10 Lakh (solar top-up) | Up to ₹40 Lakh |
| Tenure | Up to 15–20 years | Up to 5–6 years |
| Security | Property mortgage (collateral) | Unsecured |
| Processing fee | ~0.5%–1% of loan amount | ~1%–2.5% |
| Subsidy compatibility | Yes | Yes |

The lower rate on the Green Home Loan is structurally attractive, a 10-year loan at 9% versus a 5-year personal loan at 14% represents a difference of several lakhs in total interest outgo on a ₹3 Lakh solar system.

### HDFC Personal Loan: The Flexible Fallback

Homeowners who do not have an HDFC home loan, renters who need short-term financing, or anyone who cannot provide property collateral typically use the HDFC Personal Loan. The processing is faster (2–5 days for pre-approved customers), the documentation is lighter, but the interest rate is significantly higher.

> **₹78,000.** The maximum PM Surya Ghar central subsidy for a 3 kW residential system, credited directly to the beneficiary's bank account after DISCOM verification. *Source - [MNRE PM Surya Ghar Portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), 2026.*

For a homeowner taking a ₹3 Lakh HDFC Personal Loan at 14% for 5 years, the effective cost of solar financing drops materially once the ₹78,000 subsidy lands and is used for prepayment, reducing the principal to ~₹2.22 Lakh and shortening the effective tenure.

## HDFC Solar Loan Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility varies by product, but the common requirements across both routes are:

- **Age**: 21 to 65 years (at loan maturity for home loan route; 21–60 for personal loan).
- **Income**: Salaried applicants need a net monthly income of ₹15,000 or higher. Self-employed applicants need 2 years of positive ITR.
- **Credit score**: CIBIL score of 700 or above is the informal floor; 750+ unlocks better pricing.
- **Employment stability**: Minimum 2 years of employment history (salaried) or 3 years of business continuity (self-employed).
- **Property ownership**: Required for the Green Home Loan / top-up route. Not required for Personal Loan.
- **Existing HDFC relationship**: Top-up applications are smoother if the applicant already has an active HDFC home loan with 12+ EMIs paid.

### Eligibility for Salaried vs Self-Employed Applicants

| Criterion | Salaried | Self-Employed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income proof | 3 months' salary slips + Form 16 | 2 years' ITR with computation |
| Minimum net income | ₹15,000/month | ₹2 Lakh/year (net profit) |
| Employment proof | Offer letter + appointment letter | Business registration, GST certificate |
| Bank statement | 6 months | 12 months |
| CIBIL score | ≥ 700 | ≥ 700 |

Self-employed professionals and small business owners often find the Green Home Loan documentation heavier, since business income requires audited financials. For amounts below ₹3 Lakh, the Personal Loan is frequently the faster route.

## Documentation Required for HDFC Solar Loan

Gathering documents in advance cuts processing time by 5–7 working days. Below is a consolidated checklist:

**KYC and Identity**
- **PAN card**: mandatory for all loans above ₹50,000.
- **Aadhaar card**: primary address proof accepted by HDFC.
- **Passport or voter ID**: secondary identity proof.

**Income and Financial Documents**
- **Last 3 months' salary slips** (salaried) or **last 2 years' ITR** with computation and acknowledgement (self-employed).
- **Last 6 months' bank statements** (12 months for self-employed).
- **Form 16** (salaried) or **CA-certified P&L account** (self-employed).

**Property Documents (Green Home Loan / Top-Up)**
- **Registered sale deed** or **original allotment letter**.
- **Latest property tax receipt**.
- **Occupancy Certificate (OC)** or **Completion Certificate**.
- **Existing home loan statement** (if applying for top-up).

**Solar-Specific Documents**
- **Vendor quotation** with itemised system cost, GSTIN of vendor, and HSN codes.
- **MNRE empanelment certificate** of the solar vendor (recommended for subsidy route).
- **Site photograph** of the installation rooftop.

## The HDFC Solar Loan Decision Tree

### The HDFC Solar Loan Decision Tree

Before working through the loan amount question, it helps to confirm the system size and payback assumptions with a [solar cost and savings calculator](https://heavengreenenergy.com/solar-calculator/), the loan amount should match what the roof and consumption profile actually justify, not just what a vendor quotes.

This framework helps you identify your optimal financing path in four questions:

1. **Do you own the property where solar will be installed?**
   - Yes → proceed to question 2.
   - No → HDFC Personal Loan is the only viable HDFC route; compare with NBFCs for better rates.

2. **Do you already have an active HDFC home loan?**
   - Yes → apply for a Green Home Loan top-up. Fastest route, best rates.
   - No → apply for a fresh Green Home Loan (secured) or check IREDA and SBI first.

3. **Is your CIBIL score 750 or above?**
   - Yes → negotiate for the lower end of the rate band (~8.5%).
   - No → expect rates toward 10%; consider improving score before applying or check SBI which is marginally less rate-sensitive.

4. **Is the loan amount below ₹2 Lakh?**
   - Yes → Personal Loan processing is faster and worth the higher rate given the smaller base.
   - No → secured Green Home Loan route saves significantly in total interest over 7+ years.

If you land on IREDA or SBI after this tree, read the linked guides ([IREDA solar loan guide](/blog/solar-loan-ireda/) and [SBI solar loan guide](/blog/solar-loan-sbi/)) before making a final call.

## How to Apply for an HDFC Solar Loan: Step-by-Step Process

The application process differs slightly between the top-up route and the Personal Loan route, but the overall journey looks like this:

**Step 1: Assess Eligibility**
Run a soft credit check on CIBIL or the HDFC website before applying. Determine which product applies (Green Home Loan top-up vs Personal Loan) using the Decision Tree above.

**Step 2: Collect Documents**
Assemble all KYC, income, property, and solar-specific documents listed in the earlier section. Incomplete documentation is the single biggest cause of processing delays.

**Step 3: Get a Solar Quotation from an Empanelled Vendor**
For PM Surya Ghar subsidy compatibility, the vendor must be empanelled on the [PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/). Request an itemised quotation with GST-registered details. Check our [PM Surya Ghar empanelled vendor guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) for how to verify vendor status.

**Step 4: Submit Application**
Visit the nearest HDFC branch, use the HDFC Bank app (for pre-approved Personal Loan), or apply online at the HDFC website. Mention the specific purpose ("solar panel installation" or "home improvement, solar") to ensure the application is routed correctly.

**Step 5: Property Valuation (Green Home Loan / Top-Up Only)**
HDFC's valuator visits the property within 3–5 working days. The property's fair market value determines the LTV ceiling.

**Step 6: Sanction Letter and Disbursement**
On approval, HDFC issues a sanction letter. Disbursement is made either directly to the solar vendor (preferred for documentation purposes) or to the applicant's bank account.

**Step 7: Solar Installation and Subsidy Application**
The empanelled vendor installs the [residential solar system](https://heavengreenenergy.com/residential-solar/), [applies for net metering](/blog/how-to-apply-net-metering-india/) via the state [DISCOM](/glossary/discom/), and registers the installation on the [PM Surya Ghar portal](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-apply-online/). Once DISCOM verification is complete, the subsidy is credited directly to the homeowner's bank account, typically within 30–90 days of grid connection.

**Step 8: Prepay with Subsidy**
Once the subsidy is credited, use it to prepay a lump sum on the HDFC loan. HDFC typically allows partial prepayment on home loans after 12 months without foreclosure penalty.

## Common Mistakes When Applying for HDFC Solar Loan

Most rejections and delays trace back to the same set of avoidable errors:

- **Applying for the wrong product**: asking for a "solar loan" at the branch and getting routed to a generic Personal Loan when the Green Home Loan top-up was available at half the rate.
- **Missing vendor GSTIN**: HDFC requires the vendor's GST registration number for the disbursement to go directly to the vendor. Many local installers without formal registration complicate this.
- **Not mentioning subsidy integration**: HDFC's loan team needs to know the subsidy credit will be used for prepayment so they can structure the loan accordingly and flag the account for a future lump-sum prepayment.
- **Low CIBIL score at application**: applying with a 680 CIBIL when a one-time credit card settlement could have taken it to 720+ in 60 days. Rushing the application costs money through higher rates.
- **Ignoring IREDA and SBI**: HDFC is not the cheapest option. If rate matters more than convenience, checking [IREDA solar loan](/blog/solar-loan-ireda/) and [SBI solar loan](/blog/solar-loan-sbi/) first is worth the extra steps.
- **Not verifying the vendor**: choosing an installer who is not empanelled on the PM Surya Ghar portal means the subsidy application cannot proceed, negating a significant portion of the financial benefit.

> **Key fact:** IREDA disbursed over ₹3,500 crore in solar home loans in FY 2024–25, demonstrating significant demand for institutional solar financing in India. *Source - [IREDA Annual Report](https://www.ireda.in/), 2025.*

## Integrating PM Surya Ghar Subsidy with HDFC Loan

The [PM Surya Ghar subsidy](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-amount/) ranges from ₹30,000 for 1 kW to ₹78,000 for 3 kW systems, paid after grid connection is verified by the DISCOM. Here is how it interacts with an HDFC loan:

| System Size | System Cost (approx.) | Central Subsidy | Net Loan Needed | Post-Subsidy Loan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 kW | ₹50,000–₹65,000 | ₹30,000 | ₹20,000–₹35,000 | Apply Personal Loan |
| 2 kW | ₹1,00,000–₹1,30,000 | ₹60,000 | ₹40,000–₹70,000 | Personal Loan |
| 3 kW | ₹1,50,000–₹2,00,000 | ₹78,000 | ₹72,000–₹1,22,000 | Personal Loan or top-up |
| 5 kW | ₹2,50,000–₹3,50,000 | ₹78,000 | ₹1,72,000–₹2,72,000 | Green Home Loan top-up |
| 10 kW | ₹5,00,000–₹7,00,000 | ₹78,000 | ₹4,22,000–₹6,22,000 | Green Home Loan top-up |

For systems above 3 kW, the subsidy plateau at ₹78,000 means the homeowner's out-of-pocket or loan exposure grows. The Green Home Loan route becomes more compelling above ₹2 Lakh loan amounts.

State-level subsidies (available in Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, and Uttar Pradesh) can add a further ₹10,000–₹30,000 credit but are paid through a different DISCOM channel and do not affect HDFC loan eligibility.
