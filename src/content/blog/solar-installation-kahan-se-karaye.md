Ab tak ka homework ho chuka hai। Aapko pata hai kitne kW ka system chahiye, on-grid lena hai ya hybrid, aur subsidy kitni milegi। Ab asli sawaal bacha hai: solar installation kahan se karaye? Yahin par zyadatar log phas jaate hain, kyunki har taraf se quote aa rahe hote hain, har koi apne aap ko "government approved" bata raha hota hai, aur rate ka difference 30 percent tak hota hai bina kisi clear wajah ke। Panel aur inverter to 25 saal chalenge, lekin unko lagane wala banda hi decide karta hai ki woh 25 saal aaram se nikalte hain ya har monsoon mein sar dard bante hain। Yeh guide sirf ek cheez par focus karti hai, sahi installer chunna, aur woh bhi verification steps ke saath jo aap aaj ek ghante mein khud kar sakte hain।

> **TL;DR**
> - PM Surya Ghar subsidy ke liye installer ka national portal par empanelled hona mandatory hai, optional nahi।
> - Vendor ka registration number pmsuryaghar.gov.in par khud check karein, screenshot par bharosa na karein।
> - Bina chhat par aaye diya gaya WhatsApp rate card quote nahi hai, usmein cable length aur structure height missing hoti hai।
> - Advance 10 se 20 percent se zyada nahi, aur payment hamesha company current account mein GST invoice ke against।
> - Warranty papers apke naam par lein, dealer ke naam par nahi, warna claim ke waqt dealer ke bina kuch nahi hota।
> - "Subsidy 7 din mein guaranteed" ek red flag hai, realistic timeline 30 se 90 din hai aur DISCOM par depend karta hai।
> - Net metering rules, inspection speed aur benchmark rates state ke hisaab se alag hote hain, isliye local DISCOM experience wala vendor value deta hai।

**Short answer.** Solar installation wahi se karayein jahan teen cheezein saath mein milein: PM Surya Ghar portal par verified empanelment apke DISCOM area ke liye, chhat par aakar kiya gaya written site survey, aur model-number level quotation jismein ALMM reference, warranty terms aur net metering responsibility clearly likhi ho। Yeh teen filters lagane ke baad 10 vendors mein se 2 bachte hain, aur wahi 2 compare karne layak hote hain।

## Empanelled Vendor: Yeh Optional Nahi Hai

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana mein subsidy tab hi milti hai jab installation ek aise vendor ne ki ho jo apke state DISCOM ke saath national portal par registered hai। Iska matlab yeh nahi ki baaki installers kaam nahi jaante। Matlab yeh hai ki unka commissioning report portal ke through submit hi nahi ho sakta, aur subsidy claim technically process nahi hota।

Yeh rule paisa bachane ke liye nahi banaya gaya, accountability ke liye bana hai। Empanelment lete waqt company DISCOM ke saath likhit agreement karti hai: sirf ALMM-listed equipment lagayengi, state benchmark rate se zyada charge nahi karengi, aur portal workflow follow karengi। Isi agreement ki wajah se aapke paas grievance ka formal raasta bhi bachta hai।

Empanelment kis tarah kaam karta hai, kaun se financial aur technical criteria lagte hain, aur grievance kaise file hoti hai, uska poora breakdown [empanelled vendor guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) mein diya gaya hai। Yahan hum sirf verification aur selection par rukenge।

Ek baat साफ samajh lein। Empanelment ek entry ticket hai, quality certificate nahi। Har empanelled vendor accha nahi hota। Isliye empanelment check karna pehla filter hai, aakhri nahi।

## Empanelment Verify Kaise Karein: 15 Minute ka Kaam

Vendor ka bheja hua PDF ya screenshot proof nahi hai। Khud verify karna itna hi lagta hai:

1. **Portal kholein।** pmsuryaghar.gov.in par jaakar apna state aur DISCOM select karein, phir registered vendor list dekhein।
2. **Naam aur district match karein।** Company ka registered naam bilkul wahi hona chahiye jo quotation aur GST invoice par hai। "Sister concern" ya "franchise" wala jawab accept na karein।
3. **Registration validity dekhein।** Empanelment ki expiry date hoti hai। Expire ya suspended vendor ke saath aage badhna khatarnak hai।
4. **DISCOM office se cross-check karein।** Kai states mein DISCOM apni website par alag list rakhta hai। Dono jagah naam hona best confirmation hai।
5. **GST number verify karein।** GST portal par company ka naam aur status active hai ya nahi, yeh 2 minute ka check hai।

Agar vendor kahe ki registration "process mein hai", to intezaar karein ya doosra vendor dekhein। Process mein hona aur registered hona alag baat hai, aur subsidy sirf doosri wali par milti hai।

## Asli Site Survey vs WhatsApp Quote

Yeh farq sabse zyada paisa bachata hai aur sabse kam log iska dhyan rakhte hain।

Ek asli site survey mein banda chhat par chadhta hai aur yeh sab note karta hai: shadow-free area kitna hai aur din bhar shadow kis taraf badalti hai, panel ka layout aur tilt kya rahega, chhat ka structure load lene layak hai ya nahi, existing meter ka type aur sanctioned load kitna hai, earthing pit kahan banega, aur inverter se meter tak cable run kitne meter ka hoga। Survey ke baad quotation mein structure height, cable length aur earthing alag line items ban kar aate hain।

WhatsApp par sirf bijli bill dekh kar bheja gaya "5 kW ka itna rate" ek estimate hai। Woh estimate installation ke din badalta hai, aur badalta hamesha upar ki taraf hai। "Sir cable 40 meter lag gayi, extra ₹8,000" wali call yahin se aati hai।

> **Jo log sabse zyada miss karte hain।** Survey ke waqt puchna chahiye ki structure ki height kitni rakhi ja rahi hai aur galvanisation kya hai। Coastal ya heavy rain area mein sasta MS structure 4 se 5 saal mein rust karta hai, aur uske baad panel to chalte hain lekin unko pakadne wala dhaancha nahi bachta।

Survey se aaya hua design kitna realistic hai, yeh dekhne ka ek aasaan tareeka hai: vendor se shading analysis ya layout drawing maangein। Aaj kal chhote dealers bhi design software se ek page ka layout nikaal dete hain। Agar woh page nahi de paa rahe, to design unke dimaag mein hai, kagaz par nahi।

## Documents: Legitimate Installer Kya Deta Hai

Yeh list quotation approve karne se pehle maangein, baad mein nahi।

| Document | Kya check karna hai | Kyun zaroori hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empanelment registration number | Portal par apne DISCOM ke under listed | Iske bina subsidy claim process hi nahi hota |
| Panel aur inverter ka model number | Quotation mein exact model, brand naam kaafi nahi | ALMM listing model-specific hoti hai, brand-wide nahi |
| ALMM listing reference | MNRE ki current list mein wahi model search karke | Non-ALMM equipment par inspection fail hota hai |
| DCR declaration | Domestic content wale cell aur module ka written declaration | Residential subsidy ke liye DCR compliance zaroori hai |
| Warranty certificates | Aapke naam aur address par, dealer ke naam par nahi | Dealer band ho jaaye to bhi claim aapka rehta hai |
| Net metering application receipt | Application number ya acknowledgement | Yahi proof hai ki file actually DISCOM mein gayi |
| GST invoice | Company current account details ke saath | Consumer forum aur warranty dono mein yahi chalega |
| Structure aur workmanship warranty | Kitne saal, kya cover hota hai | Panel warranty structure ko cover nahi karti |

Inverter warranty par ek extra minute lagayein। "5 saal warranty" likha hona kaafi nahi hai, yeh dekhein ki woh full replacement hai ya sirf repair, aur RMA turnaround kitna define kiya gaya hai। Qbits ki on-grid range (QB 3.3KTLS, QB 5KTLS, QB 6 KTLD) aur hybrid range (QBH 5KS, QBH 6KS48P) 12-year full replacement warranty ke saath aati hai, aur yeh baat installer ke quotation mein bhi likhi honi chahiye। Detail samajhne ke liye [solar inverter warranty guide](/blog/solar-inverter-warranty/) helpful hai।

## Red Flags: Yahan Ruk Jaayein

| Red flag | Aap kya sunte hain | Asli matlab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bhaari upfront advance | "80 percent abhi, baaki commissioning par" | Cash flow doosre customer se chal raha hai, aapka paisa risk par hai |
| Site visit nahi | "Bill bhej do, rate bata dete hain" | Design nahi hua, extra charges baad mein aayenge |
| Written warranty nahi | "Company ki warranty hai na, likhne ki kya zarurat" | Claim ke waqt kuch bhi enforce nahi hoga |
| Subsidy ka fixed jaldi vaada | "7 din mein subsidy account mein" | DISCOM inspection ka control vendor ke paas hai hi nahi |
| Model number chhupana | "Brand A ka panel, model baad mein decide karenge" | Substitution ki jagah bachai ja rahi hai |
| Personal account mein payment | "UPI kar do, invoice baad mein" | GST invoice na hona matlab legal recourse kamzor |
| Portal par naam nahi | "Hum bade vendor ke under kaam karte hain" | Commissioning report submit karne ka authority nahi hai |
| Net metering "aap khud dekh lena" | "Woh alag process hai sir" | Sabse thakane wala step aap par daala ja raha hai |

In patterns mein se kuch outright fraud ban jaate hain। Fake portals, forged approval letters aur advance leke gayab hone wale cases, unka detailed coverage aur paisa jaane ke baad kya karna hai, woh [solar scams se bachne wali guide](/blog/solar-scams-india-how-to-avoid/) mein hai। Yahan hum unse pehle wale stage par hain, matlab vendor chunne ke waqt hi filter lagana।

## Local Dealer, National EPC ya Online Marketplace

Teenon models chalte hain। Farq service, price aur accountability ke balance ka hai।

| Parameter | Local empanelled dealer | National EPC | Online marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Aksar sabse competitive | 5 se 15 percent zyada | Listed rate kam, add-ons alag |
| Site survey quality | Depends on individual | Standardised process | Third-party installer par depend |
| DISCOM paperwork | Local officer se familiarity | Dedicated liaison team | Aksar customer scope |
| Breakdown response | 24 se 48 ghante | 3 se 7 din, ticket based | Installer dhoondhna padta hai |
| Accountability | Reputation local hai | Contractual, escalation possible | Fragmented, blame shift hota hai |
| Best fit | 2 kW se 10 kW residential | 10 kW se upar, C&I, multi-site | Sirf equipment purchase, installation nahi |

Meri raay saaf hai। Residential rooftop mein service response sabse zyada matter karta hai, aur wahan achhi reputation wala local empanelled dealer national EPC se behtar experience deta hai, badi baat yeh hai ki woh aapke DISCOM ke inspection engineer ko personally jaanta hai। Iska exception commercial aur multi-site projects hain, jahan documentation discipline aur bank-grade paperwork zyada important ho jaate hain।

Online marketplace ka istemal equipment ka price benchmark karne ke liye karein, installation ke liye nahi। Marketplace order mein warranty registration aur DCR trail aksar toot jaati hai, aur subsidy claim mein wahi trail maangi jaati hai।

Reference maangna kabhi mat bhoolein। Vendor se apne hi city ke pichle 3 installations ke customer numbers maangein, aur kam se kam do ko phone karein। Ek sawaal kaafi hota hai: "Commissioning ke baad koi problem aayi to kitne din mein aaye the?"

## State aur DISCOM ke Hisaab se Kya Badalta Hai

Yeh guide poore India ke liye hai, lekin ground par teen cheezein state-wise alag hoti hain।

**Benchmark rate।** MNRE aur state DISCOM per kW ka maximum rate publish karte hain jo empanelled vendor subsidy beneficiary se le sakta hai। Yeh number Gujarat, Maharashtra aur UP mein alag hai। Apne DISCOM ka current benchmark dekh kar quote judge karein।

**Net metering ka process aur speed।** Kuch states mein application se meter installation tak 3 hafte lagte hain, kuch mein 3 mahine। Sanctioned load badhane ki condition, meter ki cost, aur test report ka format bhi alag hai। [Net metering apply karne ki guide](/blog/how-to-apply-net-metering-india/) mein step-wise process hai।

**State subsidy top-up।** Kuch states central subsidy ke upar apna amount dete hain। Vendor ko yeh pata hona chahiye, aur uske quotation mein woh reflect hona chahiye।

**Inspection ka rigour।** Kuch DISCOMs earthing resistance aur AC protection ko strictly check karte hain, kuch nahi। Strict DISCOM area mein sasta quote lena zyada risky hai, kyunki inspection fail hone par rework aapke kharche par hota hai।

## Installation ke Baad: Service Kya Honi Chahiye

Vendor selection ka aadha faisla is section par hona chahiye, kyunki panel lagne ke baad hi asli rishta shuru hota hai।

- **Net metering closure।** Meter lagne aur DISCOM approval milne tak follow-up vendor ka kaam hai, aapka nahi। Yeh quotation mein likhwayein।
- **Monitoring handover।** Commissioning ke din inverter ka monitoring app aapke phone par setup hona chahiye, login aapke naam par। Qbits inverters mein WhatsApp based monitoring milti hai, isliye alert aapko seedha aata hai, dealer ke through nahi।
- **Pehle saal ka inspection।** Kam se kam ek free visit, monsoon ke baad, jismein connections, earthing aur structure bolts check hon।
- **Cleaning guidance।** Dust wale areas mein har 15 se 20 din mein cleaning chahiye। Vendor se schedule aur method likhwa lein, warna generation 8 se 15 percent tak gir jaati hai।
- **Fault response time।** Inverter fault par kitne ghante mein visit, aur RMA kitne din mein, yeh number written hona chahiye।
- **Documents folder।** Warranty cards, ALMM references, commissioning report, net meter test report, sab ek jagah scan karke rakhein।

Ek chhota sa test jo aksar sach bata deta hai: installation se pehle vendor ke service number par ek normal complaint call karke dekhein। Jitni der mein woh sales se pehle uthate hain, usse zyada der sale ke baad lagegi।
