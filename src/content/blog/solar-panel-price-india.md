Solar panel price in India is not one number. For a useful budget, separate **module-only price**, **installed system cost before subsidy**, and **your amount after approved subsidy**.

Official-store and retailer listings checked on 14 October 2026 span **₹18.18/W to ₹40.74/W**, from a Loom Solar 550 W mono PERC pack at ₹10,000 per panel to a Luminous 540 W DCR mono PERC module at ₹22,000, depending on technology, DCR status and seller. Bulk ex-works prices tracked by JMK Research sit far lower, at ₹13.8 to ₹22.3 per Wp in April 2026. The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) publishes a whole-system benchmark of **₹50,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹45,000 for each additional kW**. That benchmark is not a fixed installer tariff.

Qbits Energy makes solar inverters. It does not sell solar panels or installation. Every panel figure below comes from the named manufacturer, retailer or research firm, and none is a Qbits price or a project quote.

> [!KEYTAKEAWAYS]
> - Retail listings checked on 14 October 2026 span ₹18.18/W to ₹40.74/W, while JMK Research's bulk ex-works prices for April 2026 run ₹13.8 to ₹22.3 per Wp, so panel, bulk and installed-system prices must not be mixed.
> - MNRE's installed-system benchmark is ₹50,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹45,000 for each additional kW. It is a planning and subsidy reference, not a fixed installer tariff.
> - Any non-DCR module in the installation makes it ineligible for central financial assistance. Same-brand DCR premiums in the listings run ₹2,000 to ₹6,600 per panel.
> - Panels carry 5% GST since 22 September 2025. A full-system contract can blend to about 8.9% if the service portion is taxed at 18%.
> - Ask for the quote in separate lines for modules, inverter, structure, electrical balance of system, installation, metering and taxes, with exact models.

> [!IMPORTANT] Non-DCR modules cost you the central subsidy
> **The subsidy rule.** The MNRE guideline says solar modules in an installation claiming central financial assistance (CFA) must be domestically manufactured from domestically manufactured cells, and that "use of non-DCR modules in any form in the installation shall render the installation ineligible for CFA." It also says consumers who install without CFA are free to do so without DCR modules. The standard CFA is ₹30,000 per kWp for the first 2 kWp and ₹18,000 for the third, so ₹78,000 at 3 kWp and above, and no CFA beyond 3 kWp. A DCR premium of ₹2,000 to ₹6,600 per panel, applied to a six-panel array, is ₹12,000 to ₹39,600. Compare that against the CFA your DISCOM will actually approve, using the [PM Surya Ghar subsidy amount guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-subsidy-amount/) and the [state-wise subsidy comparison](/blog/solar-subsidy-all-states/). The scheme background is in the [PM Surya Ghar glossary entry](/glossary/pm-surya-ghar/) and the [full scheme guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-yojana-complete-guide/).

If you want no central subsidy, such as on a larger commercial array, DCR is a cost decision rather than a compliance one. Confirm that choice with your DISCOM and installer before you buy.

## Mono PERC vs TOPCon vs bifacial: is the premium worth it?

Every panel technology trades price against output. There is no universal winner, only the right module for your roof and budget.

**Mono PERC versus TOPCon on price.** On non-DCR modules the gap is small. JMK Research recorded ₹13.8/Wp for mono PERC and ₹14.2/Wp for TOPCon in April 2026, a difference of ₹0.40/Wp. At retail, Waaree's 615 W TOPCon bifacial module listed at ₹18.53/W against ₹18.73/W for its 550 W mono PERC module. The technology label alone does not set the price.

**The roof-constrained case.** What matters is watts per square metre of module area. A Waaree 550 W mono PERC module measures 227.2 cm by 113.3 cm, about 2.57 square metres, which is about 214 W per square metre. When roof area limits the array, a module with a higher figure fits more capacity into the same space. Compare the exact dimensions and rated power in each datasheet, because wattage alone does not tell you the area.

**Bifacial.** A bifacial module can collect light on its rear face, but any gain depends on mounting height, rear shading and the reflectance of the surface below. A flush-mounted array on a dark roof and an elevated array over a light surface are different installations. At the one retailer where we found both, the bifacial mono PERC 550 W pack cost ₹1.82/W more than the monofacial one. Ask the installer to state the rear-gain assumption in writing; do not accept a percentage with no basis.

**Design comes first.** Tilt, shading, string layout and inverter sizing all affect annual generation, and a quote should state them. Settle the design before you pay a premium for a module label.

## GST on solar panels: what you pay

Panels fall under entry 437 of Schedule I of [CBIC Notification 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate)](https://courier.cbic.gov.in/ECCS/advisory/2025/NOTIFICATION%20NO.%209_2025-INTEGRATED%20TAX%20(RATE)%20-1759486719.pdf), dated 17 September 2025. Item (h) of that entry names photovoltaic cells, whether or not assembled in modules or made up into panels. The Schedule I rate is **5%**, and the notification came into force on **22 September 2025**. A 5% rate applies to a standalone module purchase.

For a full-system contract that supplies the modules along with other goods and the taxable installation service, the Explanation to entry 437 deems 70% of the gross consideration to be goods and 30% to be that service. If the service portion is taxed at 18%, the blended rate is 0.7 × 5% + 0.3 × 18% = 8.9% on the contract value. That blend is arithmetic on stated rates, not a rate printed on your invoice. The solar GST guide, [GST on solar inverters and panels](/blog/gst-on-solar-inverters-2026/), sets out the entry-by-entry treatment, the HSN codes and the invoice details to check, and notes that components sold separately can attract a different rate.

> [!FORMULA] Blended GST on a full-system contract
> 0.7 × 5% + 0.3 × 18% = 8.9% of contract value (70% treated as goods, 30% as installation service taxed at 18%)

Before 22 September 2025 the rate on these goods was 12%, so a printed price list or tax table from before that date overstates the tax. Ask the vendor for tax line by line and the notification number they rely on.

## Why panel prices moved in 2026

Only two drivers are sourced here, and neither is a forecast.

**Price movement.** JMK Research's April 2026 update recorded domestic mono PERC at ₹13.8/Wp and TOPCon at ₹14.2/Wp, each down 3% on the month, with DCR TOPCon at ₹22.3/Wp. Its July 2026 update reported non-DCR TOPCon and mono PERC stable, and DCR TOPCon up 3.17% on the month. JMK's pages do not give a cause for the domestic moves, so we do not assign one.

**Cell-level policy.** MNRE's ALMM covers modules in List-I and cells in List-II. MNRE's [ALMM page](https://mnre.gov.in/en/approved-list-of-models-and-manufacturers-almm/) shows the first List-II issued on 31 July 2025 and a 12th revision dated 8 October 2026, which we checked on 14 October 2026. [Energetica India](https://www.energetica-india.net/news/almm-list-ii-for-solar-pv-cells-to-be-effective-from-june-1-2026) reported the MNRE amendment requiring List-II cells for net-metering and open-access projects commissioned on or after 1 June 2026. MNRE has issued later exemptions and clarifications for some project types, covered in our [net-metering ALMM List-II exemption post](/blog/almm-list-ii-exemption-net-metering-open-access/). Check the current MNRE order for your category. The PM Surya Ghar DCR condition is separate and stated in the CFA guidelines.

ALMM lists modules and cells. MNRE publishes no ALMM list for inverters.

At the top of the DCR module range, the 3 kWp modules and inverter together take about 86% of the benchmark, which shows how much module choice moves the rest of the quote.

Your amount after CFA is the gross invoice plus excluded charges, minus the approved CFA, and the CFA is processed only after installation, documentation and DISCOM inspection. For capacity-specific cost breakdowns, see the [1 kW](/blog/1kw-solar-system-price-india/), [3 kW](/blog/3kw-solar-system-price-india/), [5 kW](/blog/5kw-solar-system-price-india/) and [10 kW](/blog/10kw-solar-system-price-india/) system guides.

A complete installed quote should separate at least seven numbers. If the vendor gives only a total and a post-subsidy amount, ask for the missing lines.

Table: Seven lines a complete installed quote should separate, and what to identify in writing for each

| Quote line | What to identify in writing |
| --- | --- |
| Solar modules | Exact make, model, rated wattage, quantity and DCR evidence where required |
| Inverter and monitoring | Exact inverter model, AC rating, phase, MPPT limits and included monitoring hardware |
| Mounting structure | Roof-specific design, material, height, fastening, wind basis and waterproofing scope |
| Electrical balance of system | DC and AC cables, isolators, breakers, surge protection, earthing and distribution boxes |
| Installation and commissioning | Labour, lifting, testing, handover records and workmanship scope |
| Metering and approvals | Which DISCOM steps and charges are included, excluded or paid directly by the homeowner |
| Taxes and delivery | HSN or service classification, tax by line, freight and any amount payable at checkout |

A battery belongs on an eighth, separate line. Adding storage changes the inverter architecture, protection, usable backup energy and total cost. An ordinary on-grid system does not power the home during a grid outage simply because panels are installed.

## Choosing panels to match your inverter

Module choice and inverter choice are one electrical design. The array's voltage and current must fall inside the inverter's MPPT input window across the temperature range the roof sees.

The installer must check cold-weather open-circuit voltage, hot-weather operating voltage, current and permitted photovoltaic input against the exact module and inverter datasheets. The [string-sizing calculator](/string-sizing-calculator/) is a useful first check, but the datasheets control the final design. Higher-wattage modules often carry a higher operating current, so a module that fits one inverter's MPPT current limit may not fit another's.

Pairing also covers capacity. Rated DC module capacity drives the CFA, while the inverter's AC rating is a separate number, and an array larger than the inverter's rating is a design choice with its own limits. If you are choosing the inverter side of the system, start from the [on-grid inverter](/on-grid-inverter/) range and confirm the exact model's input limits against your chosen modules.

## Before accepting a solar quote

> [!CHECKLIST] Seven checks before you sign
> - Compare the exact module and inverter models, not brand names alone.
> - Keep module-only, equipment, balance-of-system and installation figures separate.
> - Check whether each price includes tax, freight, roof work and utility charges.
> - Compare gross pre-subsidy totals before comparing the amount shown after subsidy.
> - Confirm DCR and scheme documents against the current rules and exact module.
> - Reject savings or generation promises that do not show the tariff, consumption, shade and yield assumptions.
> - Keep the dated quote, invoice, serial numbers and commissioning records.
