**Solar inverter depreciation ka rate sirf “inverter” shabd dekhkar fix nahi kiya ja sakta.** Section 32 ke under claim ownership, business use, asset-block classification, put-to-use date aur relevant assessment year ke prescribed rate par depend karta hai. Isliye old article ka blanket 40% rate aur guaranteed payback claim hata diya gaya hai.

> This page is general information, not tax advice. File karne se pehle chartered accountant se written classification aur calculation verify karayein.

## Inverter depreciation rate ka direct answer kya hai?

Direct answer: **current tax schedule aur aapke asset classification ko dekhe bina ek universal rate quote karna safe nahi hai.** Solar plant, standalone replacement inverter, battery-backed system and ordinary electrical equipment ko books mein kaise group kiya gaya hai, yeh outcome ko affect kar sakta hai.

Applicable law and current rules ko [official Income Tax Department portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/) se check karein. Proposal, vendor blog ya older tax article ko current prescribed rate ka substitute na maanein.

## Section 32 claim ke liye kya establish karna hota hai?

Tax adviser aam taur par in questions ko resolve karega:

1. Asset ka legal owner kaun hai?
2. Asset business ya profession ke liye use hua hai ya personal use ke liye?
3. Inverter standalone asset hai ya larger solar plant ke block ka part?
4. Asset kab put to use hua?
5. Current Income-tax Rules mein applicable block aur rate kya hai?
6. Kya subsidy, grant, input-tax credit ya other adjustment actual cost ko affect karta hai?
7. Kya disposal, replacement ya impairment ka separate accounting treatment hai?

In sawalon ka answer purchase invoice alone nahi deta. Contract, commissioning records, ledger treatment and operating evidence ko saath dekhna padta hai.

## 40% claim ko automatic kyun nahi maanna chahiye?

Search results mein solar assets ke liye 40% WDV claim frequently repeat hota hai. Lekin ek generic web statement yeh prove nahi karta ki exact inverter, tax year and taxpayer facts same classification mein aate hain. Purane rates, complete solar plant aur standalone inverter bhi search snippets mein mix ho sakte hain.

Financial model mein do versions banayein:

| Model | Tax treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Base case | Koi unverified depreciation tax saving nahi |
| Reviewed case | CA-approved block, rate, timing and tax capacity |

Purchase decision tab bhi viable hona chahiye jab reviewed benefit delayed ya lower nikle. Tax saving ko vendor discount ki tarah gross invoice se minus na karein.

## CAPEX, lease aur RESCO mein owner kaun hota hai?

**Customer-owned CAPEX:** Customer equipment kharidta hai aur asset register mein record kar sakta hai. Eligibility and rate phir bhi tax review mangte hain.

**RESCO or PPA:** Developer system own kar sakta hai aur customer generated electricity ke liye payment karta hai. Customer automatically equipment depreciation claim nahi kar sakta. [RESCO definition](/glossary/resco/) aur signed contract dono padhein.

**Lease or financed purchase:** Legal ownership, accounting treatment and tax treatment agreement par depend karte hain. “No upfront cost” ya monthly EMI wording ownership establish nahi karti.

[CAPEX vs OPEX guide](/blog/solar-capex-vs-opex-india-guide/) commercial structure samjhata hai, lekin final tax position contract-specific rahegi.

## Put-to-use evidence kya hai?

Invoice date, delivery date, installation date and put-to-use date same nahi hote. Claim ke timing ko support karne ke liye yeh records preserve karein:

- Purchase invoice and payment trail.
- Exact inverter model and serial number.
- Asset register entry and location.
- Installation, testing and commissioning certificate.
- Grid approval or meter activation record, if applicable.
- Generation or operating logs.
- Ownership or financing agreement.
- CA-prepared depreciation schedule and assumptions.

System outage ya delayed grid connection ho to tax adviser se poochein ki available facts put-to-use test ko kaise affect karte hain. Commissioning date ko automatically tax conclusion na maanein.

## Replacement inverter ka treatment kaise review karein?

Replacement ko automatically fresh asset at same rate bhi na maanein aur repair expense bhi na maanein. Review mein original asset block, insurance recovery, warranty replacement, disposal proceeds, remaining written-down value and capitalisation policy aa sakte hain.

Service report, failed-unit serial number, warranty note, replacement invoice and commissioning record preserve karein. [Solar inverter warranty guide](/blog/solar-inverter-warranty/) commercial records organise karne mein madad karta hai.

## Tax-saving calculation ka safe method

CA se yeh inputs written form mein lein:

| Input | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Depreciable cost | Invoice and allowed cost adjustments |
| Asset block | Current rules and factual classification |
| Prescribed rate | Relevant assessment year schedule |
| Timing | Supported put-to-use date |
| Business-use share | Usage and accounting records |
| Tax value | Actual taxable income and applicable tax computation |

Depreciation deduction aur cash tax saving same number nahi hain. Cash effect taxpayer ki taxable income, losses, tax regime and other provisions par depend karta hai. Payback ya IRR tabhi update karein jab calculation independently reviewed ho.

## Procurement team ke liye final checklist

1. Vendor se tax saving guarantee na lein.
2. Gross project cost aur tax effect alag rakhein.
3. Exact asset schedule and model list finance team ko dein.
4. Ownership and put-to-use records completion se pehle plan karein.
5. Current rate and assessment-year treatment CA se written form mein confirm karein.
6. Base-case project economics unverified tax benefit ke bina run karein.
