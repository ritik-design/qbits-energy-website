## What PM Surya Ghar Provides in Telangana: The Central Subsidy

The central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, administered through [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), provides nationally standardised subsidies:

| System Size | Rate | Total Central Subsidy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000/kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹30,000/kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW | ₹30,000 (first 2 kW) + ₹18,000 (third kW) | ₹78,000 |
| Above 3 kW | Capped at 3 kW equivalent | ₹78,000 maximum |

> **₹78,000.** The maximum central subsidy for a 3 kW residential system, disbursed directly to the homeowner's bank account after DISCOM commissioning is confirmed on the national portal. *Source - [MNRE PM Surya Ghar Portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), 2026.*

Eligibility requires ALMM-listed panels, BIS-certified inverter, and installation by a TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL-empanelled vendor. The [empanelled vendor guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) explains the verification process, particularly important in Hyderabad's large installer market where claims of empanelment need independent confirmation.

## Telangana Solar Policy 2021: What It Means for Homeowners

The Telangana Solar Policy 2021, administered by TSREDCO (Telangana State Renewable Energy Development Corporation), provides the state's renewable energy development framework. For residential consumers:

**What the 2021 Policy provides**:
- Regulatory commitment to net metering for residential consumers (implemented through TSERC regulations)
- Grid connectivity rights for residential rooftop systems up to the sanctioned load
- Framework for DISCOM processing timelines and dispute resolution
- Facilitation support through TSREDCO for commercial-scale adoption

**What it does not provide**:
- A direct state cash subsidy on top of PM Surya Ghar (Telangana has not added a state top-up as of June 2026)
- Separate state-level residential application portal (the national PM Surya Ghar portal is the primary channel)

Homeowners should check [tsredco.telangana.gov.in](https://tsredco.telangana.gov.in/) for any scheme updates and for TSREDCO's registered vendor information.

## The 5-Pillar Telangana Subsidy Stack Framework

Five sequential steps define the PM Surya Ghar claim process in Telangana.

### The 5-Pillar Telangana Subsidy Stack

1. **PM Surya Ghar portal registration**: Register at [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) with your TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL consumer number, Aadhaar details, and bank account before installation begins. This creates the subsidy application record in the national system.

2. **DISCOM technical feasibility**: TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL reviews the application and schedules a site visit. Hyderabad's TSSPDCL has developed a relatively efficient process for its high-volume application queue. Timeline: 20 to 30 days in Hyderabad, 30 to 45 days in TSNPDCL's northern districts.

3. **ALMM-compliant installation**: Proceed only after the written DISCOM feasibility approval. ALMM-listed panels and BIS-certified inverter are mandatory. Verify current-quarter ALMM status using the [MNRE ALMM list](https://mnre.gov.in/almm-list/). The [ALMM list guide](/blog/almm-list-phase-iii-guide/) explains the verification process.

4. **Net meter commissioning**: After installation, TSSPDCL or TSNPDCL inspects and installs the bidirectional meter. Hyderabad: 35 to 50 days post-inspection. Northern Telangana: 40 to 60 days.

5. **Subsidy disbursement**: After commissioning confirmation on the national portal, the central subsidy transfers within 30 days. Telangana does not have a separate state-level disbursement mechanism.

## Telangana's Two DISCOMs: Coverage and Process

### TSSPDCL: Southern Telangana and Hyderabad

TSSPDCL (Telangana Southern Power Distribution Company Limited) serves the southern half of Telangana including Hyderabad, Rangareddy, Medchal-Malkajgiri (Cyberabad, HITEC City area), Sangareddy, Nalgonda, Suryapet, Yadadri Bhongir, Mahabubnagar, Nagarkurnool, Wanaparthy, Jogulamba Gadwal, and Narayanpet.

Hyderabad's GHMC area (covering the core city, Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Madhapur, and HITEC City) has the highest residential solar application volume in Telangana. TSSPDCL has responded to this demand with process improvements including a dedicated solar cell at the circle office level in major Hyderabad divisions.

Average TSSPDCL Hyderabad urban timeline: 60 to 85 days. Outer Rangareddy and rural TSSPDCL areas: 85 to 110 days.

Consumer portal reference: [tssouthernpower.com](https://tssouthernpower.com/).

### TSNPDCL: Northern Telangana

TSNPDCL (Telangana Northern Power Distribution Company Limited) covers northern Telangana: Nizamabad, Karimnagar, Warangal Urban, Warangal Rural, Khammam, Bhadradri Kothagudem, Mancherial, Peddapalli, Rajanna Sircilla, Jayashankar Bhupalpally, Mulugu, Bhupalapally, Siddipet, Kamareddy, Nirmal, Adilabad, Komaram Bheem Asifabad, and Medak.

Warangal and Karimnagar are the larger urban centres in TSNPDCL's area. Northern Telangana receives slightly higher irradiance than Hyderabad (5.3 to 5.5 kWh/m²/day) but has a less mature installer market. Average TSNPDCL urban timeline: 75 to 100 days.

Consumer portal reference: [tsnpdcl.in](https://www.tsnpdcl.in/).

## Net Metering Economics: TSERC Framework for Hyderabad Homeowners

Under TSERC (Telangana State Electricity Regulatory Commission) net metering regulations, surplus solar generation credits forward monthly and settle annually at the applicable export tariff.

**Key economics for a Hyderabad 3 kW system** (average TSSPDCL tariff: ₹6.50/unit blended):

- Annual generation: ~4,950 kWh (3 kW × 5.1 peak sun hours × 365 × 0.80)
- Self-consumption (85%): 4,200 kWh × ₹6.50 = ₹27,300/year
- Export credit (15%): 750 kWh × ₹3.00 = ₹2,250/year
- Total annual benefit: ₹29,550/year
- Gross system cost (3 kW): ₹1,85,000–₹2,05,000
- Post-subsidy net cost: ₹1,07,000–₹1,27,000
- Payback: 3.6–4.3 years

> **5.1 kWh/m²/day.** Average solar irradiance in Hyderabad, adequate for 3–4 year post-subsidy payback at prevailing TSSPDCL tariffs, with northern Telangana's 5.3–5.5 kWh/m²/day offering slightly faster payback for similar system costs. *Source - [NREL Global Solar Atlas](https://globalsolaratlas.info/), 2026.*

## Common Mistakes Telangana Solar Applicants Make

- **Assuming all C&I vendors are PM Surya Ghar empanelled**: Many large Hyderabad solar EPCs focus on C&I and may not have current TSSPDCL residential PM Surya Ghar empanelment. Always verify the specific empanelment certificate.
- **Not securing apartment NOC before DISCOM application**: TSSPDCL will not process a feasibility application for a flat without the association NOC. Many Hyderabad apartment buyers discover this only after their first DISCOM submission is rejected.
- **Choosing an inverter without checking 45 °C derating spec**: Hyderabad's May–June temperatures routinely hit 44 to 47 °C. Inverters that derate above 40 °C lose 10 to 15% of output at precisely the highest irradiance months.
- **Using peak summer generation for payback calculations**: Hyderabad receives significantly higher generation in March–May than in July–September. Annual totals should be the basis for payback estimates.
- **Not checking TSSPDCL vs TSNPDCL jurisdiction for outer Hyderabad areas**: Some rapidly developing areas on Hyderabad's periphery (Shamshabad, Shadnagar, Vikarabad) fall under TSNPDCL rather than TSSPDCL. Your electricity bill confirms which DISCOM serves your address.
