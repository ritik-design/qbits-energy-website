For solar EPCs and installation companies in India's growing renewable energy market, selecting the right inverter distributors is critical to business success. The distribution landscape has evolved significantly, with sophisticated channel networks, digital warranty systems, and specialised technical support becoming standard expectations.

## Indian Distribution Landscape

| Tier | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| National distributors | 15–20 states with centralised warehousing |
| Regional distributors | 3–5 states, superior last-mile logistics |
| State-level stockists | Specific markets, rapid delivery |

Market trend: consolidation toward larger distributors with stronger manufacturer relationships.

## Critical Evaluation Criteria

1. **Regional coverage** and state-wise availability
2. **Inventory management** and stock availability
3. **Logistics infrastructure** and delivery timelines
4. **Technical support** and pre-sales engineering
5. **After-sales service** and warranty claim processing
6. **Financial stability** and credit terms

## Regional Coverage Patterns

| Region | Characteristics |
| --- | --- |
| **North India** (Delhi NCR, Punjab) | Strong logistics, manufacturing access |
| **West India** (Maharashtra, Gujarat) | Manufacturing hub, C&I focus |
| **South India** (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu) | Tech-savvy, high quality standards |
| **East India** | Emerging market, less developed infrastructure |
| **Tier 2/3 cities** | Growing solar adoption, improving distribution |

## Technical Support Infrastructure

- Pre-sales engineering consultation
- Product training programs
- Commissioning assistance
- Troubleshooting response and escalation
- Technical documentation access

## Warranty and After-Sales

- Digital claim processing
- Replacement unit availability during processing
- Service centre network strength
- Manufacturer financial backing

## 20 Essential Questions to Ask Distributors

### Coverage and Delivery

1. Which states/cities do you serve with warehousing?
2. What are standard delivery timelines to key locations?
3. Last-mile delivery to project sites available?
4. How do you handle remote/difficult locations?

### Inventory

5. Inventory levels for different models?
6. Real-time stock availability access?
7. Emergency order processes?
8. Pre-order timing for guaranteed availability?

### Technical Support

9. Technical support during design phase?
10. Product training offered?
11. On-site commissioning support available?
12. Support hours and response commitments?

### Warranty

13. Warranty claim process details?
14. Typical resolution timelines?
15. Replacement units during processing?
16. Service centre locations nearby?

### Commercial Terms

17. Pricing tiers and volume incentives?
18. Credit terms and qualification requirements?
19. Minimum order quantities?
20. Price change notice periods?

## Red Flags

- Inconsistent stock availability
- Poor communication and responsiveness
- Delayed warranty processing
- Limited technical knowledge
- Unclear pricing or hidden charges
- Weak manufacturer relationships

## Closing

The right distributor amplifies your EPC capability; the wrong one drags every project down. Apply the 20-question framework rigorously, prioritise distributors with strong manufacturer backing, and build relationships built on volume commitments and consistent communication.
