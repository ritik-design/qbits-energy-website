For commercial businesses investing in solar systems across India, understanding inverter depreciation is crucial for maximising financial returns. Inverters represent **15–20% of total system investment**, and their depreciation strategy significantly impacts ROI and tax liability.

## Understanding Solar Inverter Depreciation

Depreciation allocates an inverter's cost over its useful life for accounting and tax purposes. Two functions:

- Realistic asset value representation
- Significant **tax shields** improving cash flow

## Accelerated Depreciation Rate

**40% per annum** under Written Down Value (WDV) method for solar installations.

### Eligibility

- **Business use** (residential does not qualify)
- **Entity ownership** of assets
- Operational status during financial year
- **Minimum 180-day usage** for full first-year claim
- Proper documentation maintained

## ROI Impact

### Example: 100 kW Commercial Installation (₹40 lakh)

| Scenario | Payback |
| --- | --- |
| Without depreciation | ~7.1 years |
| With depreciation tax benefits | **~5.2 years** |
| **Improvement** | **27% faster** |

## Book Value vs Actual Value (Year 10)

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Book value | ~₹4,239 (0.6% of original) |
| Actual performance | 96–97% efficiency |
| Remaining life | 5–8 years |

Book value is an accounting construct, not an indicator of physical value.

## Strategic Timing

### Commissioning Before End of FY

- Operations >180 days = 100% depreciation
- Operations <180 days = 50% depreciation only
- Plan commissioning to fall before mid-September (Indian FY)

### Combining with PM-KUSUM and State Subsidies

- Subsidies reduce the depreciable base
- Net depreciation still meaningful
- Coordinate timing with subsidy disbursement

## Documentation Requirements

- Purchase invoice with GST
- Commissioning certificate
- Operation logs
- Annual depreciation schedule
- CA-certified depreciation calculations

## Closing

Depreciation is one of the most underutilised tools in commercial solar ROI calculations. The combination of 40% accelerated depreciation, BIS-compliant equipment, and proper timing can reduce effective costs by 25–40%, turning a 7-year payback into a 5-year payback and dramatically improving project IRR.
