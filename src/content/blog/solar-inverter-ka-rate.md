## Ek Hi Size ke Andar Rate Kyun Hilta Hai

3 kW inverter ₹13,000 mein bhi hai aur ₹35,000 mein bhi। Wahi kW, wahi output। Difference chaar cheezon se aata hai।

### 1. Phase: single-phase ya three-phase

Single-phase inverter sasta hai। Three-phase inverter same kW par 15 se 25 percent mehnga padta hai kyunki usmein teen output legs, zyada semiconductors aur behtar grid sync circuit lagta hai।

Choice aapki nahi hoti, aapke connection ki hoti hai। 6 kW tak zyadatar ghar single-phase par hote hain। Usse upar DISCOM three-phase connection deta hai aur inverter bhi three-phase hi chahiye।

### 2. MPPT count

Single MPPT wala inverter sabse sasta hai। Dual MPPT typically ₹3,000 se ₹8,000 add karta hai us size par।

Ye lagat faltu nahi hai। Agar aapki chhat par panel do alag directions mein hain, jaise kuch east aur kuch west, to single MPPT poore array ko sabse kamzor string ke level par kheench leta hai। Dual MPPT dono strings ko alag track karta hai। Ek split-orientation roof par ye saal bhar mein 5 se 10 percent zyada generation deta hai, jo do saal mein extra cost cover kar leta hai।

### 3. Warranty tier

Yahi sabse bada rate driver hai।

| Warranty | Rate impact | Kya milta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 se 2 saal | Base rate | Local repair, parts alag |
| 5 saal | Base se 20 se 35 percent upar | Brand service network |
| 10 se 12 saal | Base se 50 se 90 percent upar | Full replacement, defined RMA SLA |

12-saal warranty wala inverter mehnga dikhta hai, lekin us period mein replacement zero hota hai। Ek 25-saal solar system mein aam taur par ek inverter replacement aati hai। Lambi warranty us replacement ko aage dhakel deti hai। Iski poori TCO math [12-year inverter warranty guide](/blog/12-year-solar-inverter-warranty/) mein hai।

### 4. ALMM status

ALMM-listed inverter non-ALMM se 15 se 30 percent mehnga hai।

Lekin PM Surya Ghar subsidy ke liye ALMM listing mandatory hai। ₹5,000 bachane ke liye non-ALMM lena aur ₹78,000 ki subsidy kho dena, ye sabse mehnga faisla hai jo buyers karte hain। ALMM verification ka process [ALMM Phase III guide](/blog/almm-list-phase-iii-guide/) mein step by step diya hai।

2 kW se 10 kW jaate hue per kW rate lagbhag 19 percent gir jata hai।

Reason simple hai। Inverter ki bahut si cost size se badalti hi nahi। Enclosure, LCD display, WiFi module, control board, firmware, safety certification aur packaging 2 kW aur 10 kW dono mein lagbhag same hain। Sirf power stage, heatsink aur DC terminals badte hain।

Iska practical matlab: agar aap 3 kW aur 4 kW ke beech confuse hain aur chhat par jagah hai, to 4 kW lena per kW sasta padta hai aur future load ke liye headroom bhi deta hai। Lekin overshoot mat karein। Inverter ko apne panel array se bahut bada lena paisa barbaad karta hai, kyunki inverter apni rated capacity par kabhi chalega hi nahi। Sahi DC to AC ratio nikalne ke liye [string sizing calculator](/string-sizing-calculator/) use karein।

> **Jo log galat samajhte hain.** Sabse sasta inverter sabse sasta nahi hota। Sabse sasta wo hai jiska per kW rate theek ho aur jo warranty period mein dobara na kharidna pade।

## Ye Rate Kab aur Kyun Badalte Hain

Rate card ek snapshot hai। Ye cheezein use hila deti hain।

**ALMM list updates.** Jab MNRE list revise karta hai, kuch models temporarily unavailable ho jate hain aur listed models ka rate firm ho jata hai।
