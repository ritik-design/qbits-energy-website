## What PM Surya Ghar Provides in Andhra Pradesh: The Central Subsidy

The central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is administered through [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) at nationally standardised rates:

| System Size | Rate | Total Central Subsidy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000/kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹30,000/kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW | ₹30,000 (first 2 kW) + ₹18,000 (third kW) | ₹78,000 |
| Above 3 kW | Capped at 3 kW equivalent | ₹78,000 maximum |

> **₹78,000.** The maximum central subsidy for a 3 kW residential system, disbursed directly to the homeowner's bank account after DISCOM commissioning is confirmed. *Source - [MNRE PM Surya Ghar Portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/), 2026.*

Eligibility requires ALMM-listed panels, BIS-certified inverter, and a [DISCOM](/glossary/discom/)-empanelled installer. In AP's coastal zones, coastal salt-air conditions add an additional hardware specification consideration (IP66 rating) that buyers should factor in alongside the subsidy calculation. ## The 5-Pillar AP Subsidy Stack Framework

Five sequential steps define the successful PM Surya Ghar claim process in Andhra Pradesh.

### The 5-Pillar AP Subsidy Stack

1. **PM Surya Ghar portal registration**: Register at [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) with your APEPDCL or APNPDCL consumer number, Aadhaar details, and bank information before installation begins. Do not allow any installer to begin physical work before this step is complete.

2. **DISCOM technical feasibility**: APEPDCL or APNPDCL reviews the application and conducts a site visit. Both DISCOMs process feasibility assessments through divisional offices. Urban coastal areas (Vizag, Vijayawada) have more developed divisional capacity. Timeline: 20 to 35 days for urban, 30 to 50 days for rural.

3. **ALMM-compliant installation**: Proceed only after written feasibility approval from your DISCOM. Use ALMM-listed panels and BIS-certified inverter. For coastal AP locations, add the IP66 requirement. The [ALMM list guide](/blog/almm-list-phase-iii-guide/) explains current-quarter verification.

4. **Net meter commissioning**: Post-installation inspection followed by meter change. AP's coastal urban areas typically process meter changes in 30 to 45 days post-inspection.

5. **Subsidy disbursement**: After commissioning confirmation on the PM Surya Ghar portal, the central subsidy transfers within 30 days. AP does not have a parallel state disbursement.

## Andhra Pradesh's Two DISCOMs: Coverage, Process, and Contact

### APEPDCL: Eastern and Coastal Andhra Pradesh

APEPDCL (Andhra Pradesh Eastern Power Distribution Company Limited) covers Visakhapatnam, Vizianagaram (partially shared with APNPDCL), East Godavari, West Godavari, Krishna, and Guntur districts, the most heavily urbanised and industrialised part of AP. Visakhapatnam (Vizag) is the largest city in this zone and has a well-developed solar installer market.

APEPDCL's consumer portal: [apepdcl.in](https://www.apepdcl.in/). Applications are primarily routed through the PM Surya Ghar national portal. APEPDCL divisional offices in Vizag, Rajamahendravaram (Rajahmundry), Vijayawada, and Guntur are the primary contact points for physical follow-up.

**Coastal note**: Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, and Machilipatnam face direct Bay of Bengal exposure. Salt-laden sea breeze accelerates corrosion on standard IP65 enclosures. IP66 inverters with marine-grade cable connections are the appropriate specification for these locations.

Average APEPDCL urban timeline: 60 to 80 days.

### APNPDCL: Northern and Inland Andhra Pradesh

APNPDCL (Andhra Pradesh Northern Power Distribution Company Limited) covers Srikakulam, Vizianagaram (partially), Kurnool, Kadapa (YSR), Nellore, Anantapur, and Chittoor districts. This is a geographically large service area spanning from the northern tribal belt to the semi-arid Rayalaseema region. Kurnool and Anantapur in Rayalaseema receive among the highest solar irradiance in AP (5.5 to 5.8 kWh/m²/day), making them particularly strong solar economics zones.

APNPDCL consumer portal: [apnpdcl.in](https://www.apnpdcl.in/). Divisional offices at each district headquarters.

Average APNPDCL urban timeline: 75 to 100 days. Rayalaseema rural areas can extend to 100 to 120 days.

## Common Mistakes AP Solar Applicants Make

- **Not specifying IP66 in coastal locations**: The IP65 vs IP66 distinction is commercially significant in Vizag, Kakinada, and Machilipatnam. The [IP66 vs IP65 comparison](/blog/ip65-vs-ip66-solar-inverters-weather-protection-guide/) quantifies why the additional cost is justified in coastal environments.
- **Ignoring cyclone wind load requirements**: AP's coastline is within the Bay of Bengal cyclone belt. Mounting structures should be rated for the appropriate wind speed zone. Ask vendors for IS/IEC wind load certification on proposed mounting systems, or commission independent structural and civil engineering calculations for larger or coastal-exposed installations.
- **Applying to the wrong DISCOM**: APEPDCL and APNPDCL have distinct geographic coverage. Your electricity bill is the only reliable guide.
- **Not verifying ALMM status for proposed panels**: AP's active C&I solar market means many panel models are available; not all are ALMM-listed. Verify at the [MNRE ALMM list](https://mnre.gov.in/almm-list/) before signing.
