## Last Date and Scheme Validity

MNRE's guidelines run PM Surya Ghar through FY 2026-27, and the national portal shows the scheme closing on 31 March 2027. As of mid-October 2026 reports say the Centre is considering an extension and a successor phase, but nothing has been notified, so treat 31 March 2027 as the working deadline. The subsidy is released after the DISCOM inspects the system and commissions it, so installation alone before the date is not enough.

TGSPDCL's own simplification has a date too: it waived the application fee for systems up to 10 kWp registered between 22 June 2026 and 31 March 2027.

## Telangana's State Framework: What It Does and Does Not Provide

Telangana's state-level framework for rooftop solar has two parts that matter to a homeowner.

**The regulator's rooftop rule.** TGERC (Telangana Electricity Regulatory Commission) issued the Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive Systems Regulation, 2025, which replaced the 2016 net-metering rules in November 2025. Trade press summaries say it covers net, gross, group and virtual net metering, allows net metering up to 500 kWp, ties residential capacity to the sanctioned load, and lets systems up to 10 kWp skip the technical feasibility study. Read the gazetted text for the exact clauses.

**The nodal agency and policy.** TGREDCO is the state nodal agency. Its site lists a Telangana Clean and Green Energy Policy 2025, which is aimed at renewable project development, and a rooftop net-metering page. We found no residential cash top-up on either, and we could not confirm that a "Telangana Solar Policy 2021" exists as a document.

Homeowners should check [tgredco.telangana.gov.in](https://tgredco.telangana.gov.in/) for scheme updates and for registered vendor information. The TGREDCO helpdesk number published in October 2026 is 040-48526869.

## The 5-Step Telangana Claim Process

Five sequential steps define the PM Surya Ghar claim process in Telangana. We have not attached day counts to them because no DISCOM-level figures are published; the table after the steps shows what is documented.

### The 5-Step Telangana Subsidy Process

<!-- steps -->
1. **PM Surya Ghar portal registration**: Register at [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) with your mobile number and TGSPDCL or TGNPDCL consumer number, then select your distribution company and submit the application. This creates the subsidy application record in the national system.

2. **DISCOM technical feasibility**: The DISCOM reviews the application. Under the 2025 TGERC regulation it has 15 working days to decide a complete application, and systems up to 10 kWp need no technical feasibility study.

3. **Installation**: After approval, a vendor registered under the scheme installs the system. Ask for the module ALMM details in writing before you sign.

4. **Net meter commissioning**: The DISCOM inspects the system, installs the bidirectional meter and commissions it. TGSPDCL has said load enhancement charges can be collected through later electricity bills.

5. **Subsidy disbursement**: After verification on the national portal, the central subsidy is credited to your Aadhaar-linked bank account. Telangana does not have a separate state-level disbursement mechanism for homes.

## Telangana's Two DISCOMs: Coverage and Process

District lists below follow the public Wikipedia entries for each DISCOM and may lag boundary changes, so your electricity bill is the authority on which company serves you.

### TGSPDCL: Southern Telangana and Hyderabad

TGSPDCL (Telangana Southern Power Distribution Company Limited, formerly TSSPDCL) serves Hyderabad and the surrounding southern districts: Rangareddy, Medchal-Malkajgiri, Sangareddy, Vikarabad, Medak, Siddipet, Mahabubnagar, Narayanpet, Wanaparthy, Nagarkurnool, Jogulamba Gadwal, Nalgonda, Suryapet and Yadadri Bhuvanagiri.

Hyderabad's GHMC area has the highest residential solar application volume in Telangana. In October 2026 TGSPDCL ran a three-day Seva Setu Abhiyan (2 to 4 October) with TGREDCO, banks and solar vendors to explain subsidy, finance and net metering to households.

Consumer portal: [tgsouthernpower.org](https://tgsouthernpower.org/). The TGSPDCL 24x7 helpline is 1912.

### TGNPDCL: Northern Telangana

TGNPDCL (Telangana Northern Power Distribution Company Limited, formerly TSNPDCL) covers northern Telangana: Nizamabad, Karimnagar, Warangal, Hanumakonda, Khammam, Bhadradri Kothagudem, Mancherial, Peddapalli, Rajanna Sircilla, Jagtial, Jangaon, Mahabubabad, Mulugu, Jayashankar Bhupalpally, Kamareddy, Nirmal, Adilabad and Komaram Bheem Asifabad.

Warangal and Karimnagar are the larger urban centres in TGNPDCL's area. The application route, tariff framework and subsidy are the same as in the south, because both DISCOMs sit under the same TGERC regulations; the difference is the local division office you deal with. Consumer portal: [tgnpdcl.com](https://tgnpdcl.com/).

## Net Metering Economics: TGERC Framework for Hyderabad Homeowners

Under TGERC's net-metering framework, units you export are adjusted against units you import, and banking is allowed. Surplus left over is settled at a rate set in the regulation, not at the retail tariff, so the savings that matter most come from the units your system lets you avoid buying.

What you avoid buying is priced by TGERC's retail tariff. For FY 2026-27 (order of 30 March 2026) TGERC kept domestic LT-I tariffs unchanged. In the above-200-unit category the energy charge is ₹5.10 per unit for the first 200 units, ₹7.70 for 201 to 300, ₹9.00 for 301 to 400 and ₹9.50 for 401 to 800, before fixed charges, customer charges and duty. Check the [TGERC tariff order](https://tgsouthernpower.org/resources/PDF/Tariffs/68tarifffile.pdf) for the category that applies to you.

> [!FORMULA]
> Annual saving = sum of (units avoided in each slab × that slab's rate) × 12; Payback (years) = (gross cost - central subsidy) ÷ annual saving

> [!EXAMPLE] Hyderabad 3 kW system: annual benefit and payback (illustrative)
> **Assumed inputs** (not a real customer; replace them with your own bill and quote):
>
> - System: 3 kW. Modelled yield for the Hyderabad reference site: 1,589 kWh per kWp a year (Global Solar Atlas, Solargis v2.2.68, queried 24 September 2026). Annual generation: 3 × 1,589 = 4,767 kWh, about 397 units a month.
> - Household use: 450 units a month, so the 397 units generated are all used at home and no export credit is counted.
> - Units avoided by solar, priced at the FY 2026-27 slabs: 147 units × ₹5.10 + 100 × ₹7.70 + 100 × ₹9.00 + 50 × ₹9.50 = about ₹2,895 a month, or about ₹34,700 a year.
> - Gross system cost: ₹1,92,000, an assumed figure of about ₹64,000 per kW (not a quote).
> - Net cost after the ₹78,000 central subsidy: ₹1,14,000.
> - Payback: ₹1,14,000 ÷ ₹34,700 = about 3.3 years.
>
> This excludes fixed and customer charges, duty, panel degradation, monthly generation swings and any change of billing category once your net units fall, so treat it as a first estimate. *Generation source: our [state-wise solar irradiance guide](/blog/solar-irradiance-data-india-statewise/), which reports the Global Solar Atlas query.*

## Common Mistakes Telangana Solar Applicants Make

- **Assuming all commercial vendors are PM Surya Ghar registered**: Many large Hyderabad solar EPCs focus on commercial projects. Always verify the specific residential registration on the national portal.
- **Not securing the apartment association's permission before applying**: A shared-roof application can stall without it. Ask early and keep the written consent with your documents.
- **Choosing an inverter without checking its high-temperature derating**: Hyderabad's April to June heat is among the harshest conditions for inverters. Compare the datasheet's derating behaviour, not just the headline efficiency.
- **Using peak summer generation for payback calculations**: Generation varies through the year. Annual totals should be the basis for payback estimates.
- **Not checking TGSPDCL vs TGNPDCL jurisdiction**: Districts are split between the two companies. Your electricity bill confirms which DISCOM serves your address, and selecting the wrong one can fail validation on the portal.
