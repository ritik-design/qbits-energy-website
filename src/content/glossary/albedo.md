## What is albedo

Albedo is the fraction of incoming solar radiation reflected by a surface, expressed as a dimensionless number between 0 (perfect absorber, all light absorbed) and 1 (perfect reflector, all light reflected). The term originates from Latin "albus" meaning white, and was introduced in astronomy before becoming common in atmospheric and solar engineering.

For solar PV applications, albedo specifically refers to the reflectivity of the ground surface beneath and around solar arrays. This is critical for bifacial modules, which capture both direct front-side light and ground-reflected light hitting the rear surface. Higher albedo leads to higher bifacial gain.

For Indian solar, typical ground albedo ranges from 0.05 (dark soil) to 0.30 (white gravel preparation). Utility-scale plants increasingly use white gravel preparation to boost albedo above 0.30, supporting bifacial yield gains of 15-25 percent.

## Why albedo matters

Bifacial yield. Higher albedo means higher rear-side power generation.

Project economics. Bifacial gain reduces LCOE.

Site selection. High-albedo sites favoured for bifacial plants.

Ground preparation. White gravel and similar treatments justified by albedo-driven yield.

## Technical deep dive

### Bifacial gain vs albedo

| Albedo | Approximate bifacial gain over monofacial |
|---|---|
| 0.10 (dark) | 5-8% |
| 0.20 (typical) | 10-12% |
| 0.30 (white gravel) | 12-18% |
| 0.50 (white paint) | 18-25% |
| 0.80 (snow) | 25-35% |

### Spectral albedo

Albedo varies with wavelength. Solar cells respond to UV-VIS-NIR (300-1100 nm). Sand and soil have higher albedo at NIR wavelengths. Snow has high albedo across spectrum.

### Measurement

Albedometer: paired pyranometers, one facing up (measuring incoming) and one down (measuring reflected). Albedo = downward / upward.

On-site measurement recommended for utility projects. Multiple measurements across seasons capture variation.

### Cost-benefit of ground treatment

For a 1 MW utility plant:
- Baseline albedo 0.20: bifacial gain ~10%
- White gravel albedo 0.40: bifacial gain ~18%
- Yield increase: 8% × 1,650 MWh = 132 MWh/year
- Revenue gain at ₹2.75/kWh: ₹3.6 lakh/year
- White gravel cost (10,000 m² coverage): ₹10-20 lakh
- Payback: 3-5 years

## Best practices

Conduct on-site albedo measurement during pre-feasibility.

Specify ground preparation to achieve target albedo.

Use white gravel for cost-effective high albedo.

Maintain ground albedo through cleaning (dust accumulation reduces albedo).

Document albedo assumption in yield models.
