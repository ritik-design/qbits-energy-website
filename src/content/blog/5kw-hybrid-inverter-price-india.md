## Inverter-only, inverter-plus-battery and installed prices

A quote labelled “5 kW hybrid” can cover three very different purchases.

| Quote type | It should identify | Common exclusions to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inverter only | Exact model, phase, rated output, tax treatment and supplied accessories | Battery, protection devices, freight, installation and commissioning |
| Inverter plus battery | Both model numbers, battery quantity, BMS interface and stated usable capacity | Backup circuits, protection, cable runs, metering and labour |
| Complete installed system | Modules, inverter, battery, structure, electrical protection, installation and commissioning | Roof repairs, civil work, long cable routes, utility fees and future battery replacement |

Ask each seller to quote the same scope. A ₹20,000 difference means little if one offer includes the battery breaker, communication device and commissioning while another includes only the inverter carton.

For a full rooftop budget, use the [5 kW solar-system cost guide](/blog/5kw-solar-system-price-india/). Do not add a battery price to an inverter listing and call the result an installed-system estimate. The array, structure, protection, wiring and site work still need their own line items.

## Why two “5 kW” listings may not be comparable

Start with these five checks before comparing the rupee figure.

1. **kW versus kVA:** kW is real power. kVA is apparent power. A 5 kVA name is not enough by itself; check the rated output in kW and power factor.
2. **Single-phase versus three-phase:** the checked Luminous example is single-phase. The Qbits QBH-5KS48P3 is three-phase. Connection type and load distribution change the equipment decision.
3. **Grid output versus backup output:** some documents show different continuous ratings for the grid and backup ports. Use the rating for the load you expect that port to carry.
4. **Battery voltage and protocol:** 24 V and 48 V systems are not interchangeable. A CAN or RS485 socket alone does not prove that a specific battery-management system is approved.
5. **Maximum versus everyday operation:** maximum PV input, short overload and peak efficiency are limits or test results. They are not continuous output promises.

The [on-grid versus hybrid decision guide](/blog/on-grid-vs-hybrid/) can settle the inverter type first. If outage backup is not part of the requirement, paying for a battery interface may not solve an actual problem.

## Battery cost starts with the loads, not the inverter label

A 5 kW inverter does not automatically require a 5 kWh battery. Inverter power is measured in kW. Battery energy is measured in kWh. Size storage from the loads you want during an outage and the required duration.

Here is a transparent planning example, not a product claim. A steady 500 W essential load used for four hours requires 2 kWh of AC energy:

**0.5 kW × 4 hours = 2 kWh**

If a preliminary model assumes 90% conversion efficiency and 80% usable battery capacity, the nominal battery estimate is:

**2 kWh ÷ (0.90 × 0.80) = 2.78 kWh**

That result still needs checks for starting current, discharge-power limits, temperature, reserve, ageing and the battery manufacturer's instructions. The [battery-sizing guide](/blog/battery-sizing-hybrid-solar/) walks through the load list without pretending that one capacity suits every home.

## Build a comparable quote in one page

Send the same information to every supplier:

- location and electricity connection phase;
- recent bill and sanctioned load, if relevant to the design;
- module make, model, quantity and string proposal;
- essential-load list, starting loads and desired backup hours;
- exact inverter and battery model numbers;
- required monitoring and communication equipment;
- indoor or outdoor installation location and cable distances.

Then request these rows separately:

- inverter price, MRP if applicable, tax and quote-validity date;
- battery price, quantity, BMS equipment and usable-capacity basis;
- DC and AC protection, battery protection and backup distribution;
- freight, mounting, cabling, labour, testing and commissioning;
- written warranty terms and the service-claim process;
- utility, scheme or DISCOM checks applicable to that installation.

Do not accept “subsidy eligible” as a substitute for exact project checks. The [MNRE residential CFA guidelines](https://mnre.gov.in/en/notice/operational-guidelines-for-implementation-of-the-component-central-financial-assistance-to-residential-consumers-of-pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli-yojana/) make project, technical, domestic-content and DISCOM processes part of PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana participation. They are separate from an inverter's battery capability. Approval and assistance cannot be promised from a product label.
