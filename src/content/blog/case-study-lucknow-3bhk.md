> **Illustrative planning scenario, not a customer case study.** The 5 kW capacity is a working brief, not a recommendation. This page is not based on a signed customer record, site survey, interval load file, design, quotation, commissioning report or measured performance dataset. Every project outcome is therefore unknown.

A hybrid proposal has two separate jobs: reduce grid purchases and supply selected circuits during an outage. This scenario shows why a 5 kW label alone cannot answer either question.

> [!KEYTAKEAWAYS]
> - This is a planning scenario with no customer record, survey, load file, design or measured result, so every outcome is unknown.
> - Seven inputs must be collected before sizing, starting with the critical-load list and the outage record; a missing input stays marked unknown.
> - Size backup from selected circuits and required runtime, not bedroom count.
> - The inverter's kW rating describes power, while the battery's usable kWh contributes to runtime.
> - A dedicated UPS may remain necessary for equipment that cannot tolerate the documented transfer interruption.

## What decision does this scenario test?

The question is not whether 5 kW “works” in the abstract. The question is whether that AC capacity, paired with a documented PV array and connection design, fits the site's measured daytime load, available area, electrical infrastructure and approval route.

A credible answer must be electrically safe, acceptable under the current project process, and commercially supportable after every cost and uncertainty is shown.

A missing input should stay marked as unknown. Replacing it with a typical value creates a neat result but not a reliable decision.

## Engineering controls for this site

- Size backup from selected circuits and required runtime, not bedroom count.
- Confirm transfer behaviour for every interruption-sensitive device.
- Document battery isolation, ventilation, protection and emergency shutdown.

The inverter's kW rating describes power, while the battery's usable kWh contributes to runtime. Both depend on limits, losses and conditions.

The responsible electrical professional should sign the single-line diagram, protection settings and commissioning plan. Any safety-critical or production-critical system needs an independent continuity plan.

> [!CAUTION] A hybrid is not automatically a UPS
> A dedicated UPS may remain necessary for equipment that cannot tolerate the documented transfer interruption. Grid-connected solar is not backup unless the complete design explicitly supports that role.

## How should the commercial case be calculated?

Use measured inputs and keep the calculation reproducible:

<!-- steps -->
1. **Estimated annual generation** = simulation output for the documented array, orientation, losses and weather dataset.
2. **Self-consumed energy** = the lesser of generation and coincident site load in each interval.
3. **Exported energy** = generation minus self-consumed energy, subject to the approved metering arrangement.
4. **Annual bill effect** = avoided import charges plus documented export credit minus fixed, demand, finance, service and maintenance costs.
5. **Simple payback** = eligible project cost divided by annual net cash benefit.

Do not publish a generation, saving, return or payback result until the calculation file identifies every input, unit, date and source. Tax effects require written advice from a qualified professional. Financing results require actual lender terms.
