## 6. YouTube SEO Integration

**Recommendation:**

**Expected impact:** Dwell time +30–50% on video-embedded posts → strong indirect ranking lift.

## 8. Schema-First Content Architecture

Google's SGE (Search Generative Experience) and AI Overviews are increasingly pulling structured answers directly into SERPs. Pages with deep, well-structured schema are quoted; pages without are skipped.

**Recommendation:** Every Qbits page is built schema-first, not retrofit:
- **Glossary** → `DefinedTerm` schema (already in technical-seo-framework.md)
- **How-to guides** → `HowTo` with stepped instructions
- **Comparisons** → structured data tables that Google can extract row-by-row
- **Products** → `Product` + `Offer` + `AggregateRating`
- **Reviews** → `Review` with named author + numerical rating
- **FAQ hubs** → `FAQPage` everywhere there's a Q&A pattern
- **Local pages** → `LocalBusiness` with full geo + opening hours
- **Calculators / tools** → `WebApplication` + `SoftwareApplication`
- **Cases studies** → `CaseStudy` (or `Article` with structured outcome data)

**Editorial discipline:** Before writing any page, the writer fills in the schema fields *first*. The page is then written *around* the structured data, ensuring every entity is on-page. This guarantees Google's extractors find what they need.

**Compounding upside:** As LLM-driven search citations grow (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude), schema-first pages get cited disproportionately because LLMs trust structured data more than freeform prose. **This is the next decade of SEO — Qbits should be built for it from day one.**
