3. **Balance of System (BOS)**: Heavier mounting structure (10kW carries 16 to 18 panels at 550 to 600 W), longer cable runs, larger combiner box, three-phase-rated SPDs, and earthing system. We found no dated source for a 10 kW BOS cost, so get it as a separate line in your quote.
4. **Installation and commissioning**: Labour for the array, structural work, DISCOM three-phase meter application. We found no dated source for this line either; ask for it itemised.
5. **GST**: the 12% slab no longer exists (Notification 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate), effective 22 September 2025). That is about ₹8,900 of GST per ₹1,00,000 of pre-tax contract value. Confirm the entry your supplier relies on.

## Residential vs Commercial Use: Key Differences at 10kW

### Residential 10kW (Large Villa or Bungalow)

A large villa with 3 or more air conditioners, a swimming pool pump, water heater, EV charging, and full household loads typically consumes 900 to 1,500 units per month. A 10kW on-grid system generating 40 to 50 kWh/day produces 1,200 to 1,500 units per month, enough to offset the entire bill in most months.

The single-phase vs three-phase choice depends on the home's power supply:
- If your home has a **single-phase** connection (most 2–3 BHK homes), a single-phase 10kW inverter is required.
- If your home has a **three-phase** connection (most large villas and bungalows above 7.5kW sanctioned load), a three-phase 10kW inverter distributes generation across all phases and reduces cable losses.

### Small Commercial (Shops, Clinics, Offices, Schools)

Commercial tariffs differ by state and category and are usually higher than residential ones; this guide assumes ₹10 per unit for illustration, so replace it with your own bill. A 10kW system at ₹10/unit generating 45 kWh/day saves ₹1,64,250 per year, far faster payback than residential.

Commercial buyers do **not** qualify for PM Surya Ghar subsidy but benefit from:
- **40% accelerated depreciation** under Section 32 (plant and machinery)
- **[Net metering](/glossary/net-metering/) or [gross metering](/glossary/gross-metering/)** depending on state [SERC](/glossary/serc/) policy
- **Input tax credit (ITC) on GST** if the business is GST-registered

The [solar inverter depreciation tax benefits guide](/blog/solar-inverter-depreciation-in-india/) explains how to claim these benefits.

## ITC (Accelerated Depreciation) Benefit for Commercial 10kW Buyers

This benefit is frequently overlooked and can dramatically change the economics of a commercial 10kW system:

### The Accelerated Depreciation Calculation

1. **System cost (illustrative basis)**: ₹4,60,000, the MNRE 10 kWp benchmark used as a stand-in for a quote
2. **Depreciation rate**: 40% on the written-down value, first year
3. **First-year depreciation amount**: ₹4,60,000 × 40% = ₹1,84,000
4. **Tax saving (at 31.2% effective tax rate)**: ₹1,84,000 × 31.2% = ₹57,408
5. **Effective net cost after tax benefit**: ₹4,60,000 − ₹57,408 = ₹4,02,592

In subsequent years, depreciation continues on the reducing balance. Over 5 years the cumulative deduction is ₹4,60,000 × (1 − 0.60^5) = about ₹4,24,200, which is about ₹1,32,300 of tax saving at 31.2% and about ₹1,06,800 at 25.17%. This holds only if the business has taxable profit to absorb the deduction, and the first-year rate is halved when the plant is used for fewer than 180 days in the year. This benefit is governed by Section 32 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 and the depreciation rates in the Income-tax Rules, and applies to entities with business or professional income; confirm the current rate and your eligibility with your chartered accountant.

For proprietary firms and partnership firms, the benefit is computed at the applicable income tax slab (up to 30%). For private limited companies, the corporate tax rate (25.17% for turnover below ₹400 Cr) applies.

## 10kW Solar System Payback Period

All three cases use the same assumed generation and the ₹4,60,000 benchmark as an illustrative cost. Tariffs of ₹5, ₹7 and ₹10 per unit are assumptions, so substitute your own bill. Formula: payback (years) = net cost ÷ (annual units × tariff).

**Residential at ₹7/unit:**
- Daily generation: 45 kWh (assumed, 10 kWp × 4.5 kWh per kWp per day)
- Annual generation: 45 × 365 = 16,425 kWh
- Annual savings: 16,425 × ₹7 = ₹1,14,975
- Net cost after ₹78,000 subsidy: ₹4,60,000 − ₹78,000 = ₹3,82,000
- **Payback: ₹3,82,000 ÷ ₹1,14,975 = 3.3 years**

**Commercial at ₹10/unit:**
- Annual savings: 16,425 × ₹10 = ₹1,64,250
- Effective net cost after AD benefit: ₹4,02,592
- **Payback: ₹4,02,592 ÷ ₹1,64,250 = 2.5 years**

**Residential at ₹5/unit (lower tariff states):**
- Annual savings: 16,425 × ₹5 = ₹82,125
- Net cost: ₹3,82,000
- **Payback: ₹3,82,000 ÷ ₹82,125 = 4.7 years**

On these assumptions the economics are strong for most buyers, though they depend entirely on the tariff and the quote. The full payback methodology with degradation adjustment is in the [solar payback period guide](/blog/solar-inverter-payback-period/).

## What Loads a 10kW System Handles

A 10kW system running 45 kWh/day can power the following simultaneously during peak solar hours:

- **3 × 1.5-ton inverter ACs** (1,350 W each): 4,050 W
- **Swimming pool pump (1 HP)**: 750 W
- **Refrigerator (400L)**: 220 W
- **Electric water heater (2kW)**: 2,000 W
- **Washing machine + dryer**: 1,500 W
- **EV charger (slow, 3.3kW)**: 3,300 W
- **Fans × 8 + lights × 20**: 900 W
- **Total peak simultaneous load**: ~12,720 W

With a 10kW inverter, you can comfortably run 7,000–9,000 W of actual load simultaneously, which covers all of the above except the EV charger (or run the EV charger when the ACs are off). This is the practical operating reality, not all loads run simultaneously.

## Common Mistakes in 10kW System Purchases

- **Choosing a single-phase inverter for a three-phase home**: This causes phase imbalance and may trigger DISCOM penalties. Confirm your connection type before purchasing.
- **Skipping structural engineering**: A 20-panel [mounting structure](/glossary/mounting-structure/) on an RCC terrace carries 500+ kg. Get a structural certification from a licensed engineer, firms offering dedicated structural and civil engineering for rooftop solar can provide this as a standalone service.
- **Not claiming accelerated depreciation**: Commercial buyers who miss this benefit forgo about ₹57,000 in year one on the ₹4,60,000 worked example, and more over later years. File with your CA in the same year as installation.

- **No shade analysis**: A 10kW system on a partially shaded terrace can underperform a 7kW system by 20%. [Shading analysis](/glossary/shading-analysis/) is non-negotiable at this investment level, and dedicated [shading analysis tools](https://surgepv.com/shadow-analysis/) used by professional designers model tree and structure shadows hour-by-hour across the year rather than relying on a single site visit. [NREL tools for shade analysis](https://sam.nlr.gov/) and satellite-based irradiance software are now standard practice for reputable installers in India.
