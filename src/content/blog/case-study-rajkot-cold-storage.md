> [!IMPORTANT] Illustrative planning scenario, not a customer case study
> The 40 kW capacity is a working brief, not a recommendation. This page is not based on a signed customer record, site survey, interval load file, design, quotation, commissioning report or measured performance dataset. Every project outcome is therefore unknown.

Cold storage has continuous refrigeration duty, cycling compressors and potentially sensitive controls. This scenario tests a 40 kW working brief without presenting an invented facility, bill or operating result.

> [!KEYTAKEAWAYS]
> - This is a planning scenario, not a verified customer installation. Capacity, generation, cost, savings and payback are all unknown until site records and signed project documents exist.
> - The test is whether 40 kW of AC capacity fits the site's measured daytime load, available area, electrical infrastructure and approval route.
> - Do not assume a grid-tied inverter drives compressor motors or supplies them during an outage. Cold-chain continuity needs its own tested emergency plan.
> - Keep every missing input marked as unknown, and publish no generation, saving or payback result until the calculation file names every input, unit, date and source.

## What decision does this scenario test?

The question is not whether 40 kW “works” in the abstract. The question is whether that AC capacity, paired with a documented PV array and connection design, fits the site's measured daytime load, available area, electrical infrastructure and approval route.

A credible answer must be electrically safe, acceptable under the current project process, and commercially supportable after every cost and uncertainty is shown.

## Engineering controls for this site

- Do not assume a grid-tied inverter drives compressor motors or supplies them during an outage.
- Review motor starts, VFD interaction and protection at the point of common coupling.
- Keep cold-chain continuity under a tested facility emergency plan.

The refrigeration contractor and electrical designer should review the same measured data. A headline inverter THD value is not a site power-quality study, and no universal Qbits THD claim is supported.

The responsible electrical professional should sign the single-line diagram, protection settings and commissioning plan. Any safety-critical or production-critical system needs an independent continuity plan. Grid-connected solar is not backup unless the complete design explicitly supports that role.

## How should the commercial case be calculated?

Use measured inputs and keep the calculation reproducible:

1. **Estimated annual generation** = simulation output for the documented array, orientation, losses and weather dataset.
2. **Self-consumed energy** = the lesser of generation and coincident site load in each interval.
3. **Exported energy** = generation minus self-consumed energy, subject to the approved metering arrangement.
4. **Annual bill effect** = avoided import charges plus documented export credit minus fixed, demand, finance, service and maintenance costs.
5. **Simple payback** = eligible project cost divided by annual net cash benefit.

Do not publish a generation, saving, return or payback result until the calculation file identifies every input, unit, date and source. Tax effects require written advice from a qualified professional. Financing results require actual lender terms.
