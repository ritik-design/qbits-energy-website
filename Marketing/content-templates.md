## TEMPLATE 1 — Educational Blog Post (How-To / Explainer)

**Example title:** *"How to Choose the Right Solar Inverter for Your Home in India (2026 Guide)"*

**Purpose:** Rank for informational top-of-funnel queries; capture warm leads via embedded calculators/CTAs; build E-E-A-T authority.

**Target audience:** Residential homeowner (ICP #1) — 35–55, ₹6k–15k monthly bill, considering rooftop solar.

**Target keyword:** Primary "how to choose solar inverter for home" · Secondary "best solar inverter for home India," "home solar inverter buying guide"

**Word count:** 2,500–3,500

**Structure:**
- **H1:** How to Choose the Right Solar Inverter for Your Home in India (2026 Guide)
- **Intro (150 words):** Frame the problem — 15+ brands, confusing specs, ₹50k+ decision.
- **H2:** What does a solar inverter actually do?
- **H2:** On-Grid vs Hybrid vs Off-Grid — which one fits your home?
  - **H3:** On-grid (with subsidy)
  - **H3:** Hybrid (with battery / power-cut backup)
  - **H3:** Off-grid (rural / no DISCOM)
- **H2:** The 7 criteria that actually matter (buying-criteria table)
  - **H3:** MPPT count & efficiency
  - **H3:** Warranty (Indian-entity-backed)
  - **H3:** ALMM / BIS / MNRE compliance
  - **H3:** App + Wi-Fi/GSM monitoring
  - **H3:** Operating temperature & IP rating
  - **H3:** After-sales SLA
  - **H3:** Price vs lifetime value
- **H2:** How to size the inverter for your home (with examples)
- **H2:** PM Surya Ghar subsidy — what to know before buying
- **H2:** Top 3 inverter categories compared (table)
- **H2:** A reasonable recommendation (Qbits mention — natural)
- **H2:** FAQ (5 Q&As)
- **Closing CTA block**

**Mandatory inclusions:**
- Buying-criteria table (criteria × what to ask)
- 3-category comparison table
- 5-question FAQ with FAQ schema
- 1 natural Qbits product recommendation (linked to product page)
- PM Surya Ghar subsidy mention with calculator link
- WhatsApp CTA button (mid-article + footer)
- Author byline + "Reviewed by" engineer credit
- Last-updated date

**Tone guidelines:** Educational, not salesy. Speak like a knowledgeable friend, not a brochure. Use ₹ amounts, real load examples ("runs 2 ACs + fridge"), no jargon without an immediate plain-English follow-up.

**CTA strategy:**
- Soft CTA after the criteria section: "Use our 60-second sizing calculator"
- Mid CTA: WhatsApp "Get a free site survey"
- Hard CTA in conclusion: "Get a quote from a Qbits-certified installer near you"

**Internal linking plan (8+ links):**
- `/glossary/mppt` · `/glossary/almm-list` · `/calculator/system-sizing` · `/subsidy/pm-surya-ghar` · `/products/hybrid/5-kw-qh5` · `/best-solar-inverter-for-home` · `/compare/qbits-vs-growatt-solar-inverter` · `/blog/on-grid-vs-hybrid-explained`

**Schema:** Article · FAQPage · BreadcrumbList

  - **H3:** SH5K — 5kW hybrid
  - **H3:** SG110CX — commercial 110kW
- **H2:** Pros (4–6 honest bullets)
- **H2:** Cons (4–6 honest bullets — including service-network gaps in Tier-2/3)
- **H2:** Who Sungrow is best for
- **H2:** Price range in India (with date stamp)
- **H2:** How Sungrow compares to other Indian options (single section — Qbits + Luminous + Waaree treated together; do NOT make this a Qbits sales pitch)

- **H2:** Final verdict — rating (1–10) across Reliability / Service / Value / Tech / India-Fit

## TEMPLATE 5 — Glossary Page

**Example:** *"What is MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking)? — Solar Glossary"*

**Purpose:** Capture long-tail informational queries; build internal-linking authority; feed AI/LLM citations.

**Target audience:** Curious researcher · installer training · LLM/search-AI answer engines.

**Target keyword:** "what is mppt" · "mppt full form" · "mppt in solar inverter"

**Word count:** 600–900

**Structure:**
- **H1:** What is MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking)?
- **Plain-English definition (1st paragraph, 80–120 words)**
- **Technical explanation (2nd paragraph, 120–180 words)** — V–I curve, tracking algorithm (P&O / incremental conductance), tracking efficiency
- **H2:** How MPPT works (visual / diagram description)
- **H2:** Why MPPT matters when you buy a solar inverter
- **H2:** Single vs dual vs quad MPPT — which do you need?
- **H2:** How Qbits implements MPPT (1 paragraph — natural mention)
- **H2:** Related terms (3 internal links)
- **H2:** FAQ (2 Qs)

**Mandatory inclusions:**
- Plain-English first paragraph (no jargon)
- Technical second paragraph
- One diagram or diagram-described section
- "Why it matters to a buyer" practical implication
- Natural Qbits product mention (linked)
- 3 related glossary internal links
- 2 FAQs with FAQ schema
- DefinedTerm schema

**Tone guidelines:** Authoritative but accessible. Imagine explaining to a smart non-engineer. Use one analogy. Avoid pure dictionary tone.

**CTA strategy:** Soft — "See MPPT in action on Qbits QH5" link only. Glossary is for trust + LLM citations, not direct conversion.

**Internal linking plan:**
- 3 related glossary terms (e.g., MPPT → PWM, String, Inverter Efficiency)
- 1 product page (relevant SKU)
- 1 informational blog post

**Schema:** DefinedTerm · FAQPage · BreadcrumbList

# UNIVERSAL EDITORIAL STANDARDS (apply to all templates)

**E-E-A-T baseline (every page):**
- Author byline with photo + role
- "Reviewed by" engineer credit on technical content
- Last-updated date (refresh on substantive change)
- Sources cited where claims are made (MNRE notifications, IEC standards, etc.)

**Voice & tone (cross-template):**
- Trustworthy & Evidence-Led
- Technical-yet-Accessible
- Installer-First (mention EPC angle where relevant)
- Confidently Indian (₹, Indian load examples, Indian DISCOMs)
- Plain-Spoken & Numbers-Backed

**Formatting standards:**
- Short paragraphs (≤3 sentences)
- ≥1 visual per 400 words (image, table, diagram, chart)
- Bullet lists over walls of text
- ₹ in numerals; specs in SI units
- All numbers with context ("99.5% MPPT — meaning <0.5% energy loss")

**SEO checklist (every page):**
- Title tag ≤60 chars, primary keyword at front
- Meta description ≤155 chars, includes CTA verb
- H1 contains primary keyword (one H1 per page)
- One image with descriptive alt text per 250 words minimum
- Internal links ≥3 (per Site Architecture guide)
- External link ≥1 to authoritative source (MNRE, IEC, BIS)
- URL slug ≤5 words, hyphen-separated, lowercase
- Schema markup as per template
- Mobile-first review pass before publish

**Conversion infrastructure (every commercial page):**
- WhatsApp Business CTA (city-routed where possible)
- Sticky bottom-bar mobile (Quote / WhatsApp / Compare)
- Lead form with ≤4 fields (name, phone, pincode, monthly bill)
- Trust strip (warranty + ALMM + installer count)

**Refresh cadence:**
- Subsidy / policy pages — quarterly
- Best/list pages — every 6 months
- Product pages — on SKU change
- Comparison pages — every 6 months or competitor SKU launch
- Glossary — on standard change only
- Location pages — annually (tariff updates)
