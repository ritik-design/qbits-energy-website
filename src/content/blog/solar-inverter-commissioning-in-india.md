**A solar inverter commissioning checklist should prove that the installed system matches the approved design, exact equipment documents and applicable connection requirements.** Qualified teams should record inspections, measurements, settings, functional tests, monitoring and deviations, then give the owner an as-built handover pack. This is a review checklist, not a DIY energisation procedure.

The test method and acceptance source belong beside every result. A copied universal threshold can be wrong for the model, system voltage, site or current DISCOM process.

## What must be approved before commissioning begins?

**Commissioning should begin from a controlled document set and an agreed test plan.** Confirm the final design, equipment revisions, manufacturer manuals, risk controls, inspection status, connection permissions and acceptance responsibilities. If the installed system differs from the approved documents, resolve or formally record the deviation before energisation.

The commissioning lead should have:

| Controlled input | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Final design | Approved single-line diagram, array layout and equipment schedule |
| Exact equipment | Model, serial number, firmware and supplied accessories |
| Manufacturer documents | Current installation, operation and commissioning instructions |
| Test plan | Test method, acceptance source, instrument and witness requirement |
| Grid process | Current DISCOM permissions, meter status and required records |
| Safety plan | Isolation, stored-energy, access and emergency responsibilities |
| Handover scope | Required drawings, policies, credentials and training |

Use the [inverter datasheet guide](/blog/how-to-read-solar-inverter-datasheets/) to identify model limits, then obtain the complete manual. A product-family brochure is not a commissioning procedure.

## What should the pre-energisation inspection record?

**The pre-energisation inspection should confirm that the installed equipment, wiring, protection, labels and environment match the approved design and manuals.** Record actual observations and corrections with photos. A tick without an equipment identity or evidence is difficult to audit later.

Check the project-defined items, including:

- Inverter model, serial number, rating and mounting location.
- Module and string configuration against the final design.
- Cable type, route, support, identification and visible condition.
- Connector pairing, glands, sealing and required clearances.
- DC and AC isolation and protection identified on the drawing.
- Earthing and bonding installed to the approved design.
- Battery, meter, sensor and communications wiring where applicable.
- Warning labels, emergency information and access restrictions.
- Unfinished work, water exposure, damage or foreign material.

Do not energise to “see whether it works” when the inspection has an unresolved safety or design item. Record who owns the correction and the reinspection.

## Which electrical tests should be documented?

**Document the tests specified by the approved design, equipment instructions, project plan and applicable rules.** Each entry should name the circuit or asset, method, instrument, calibration status, measured value, acceptance source, result and tester. This article deliberately does not prescribe universal test voltages or pass limits.

A commissioning schedule may include, where applicable:

| Test group | Record to retain |
| --- | --- |
| Continuity and bonding | Circuit, method, measured result and design reference |
| Polarity | String or circuit identity and verified result |
| Voltage and current | Conditions, measurement points and expected design value |
| Insulation | Isolated equipment, test method, settings and acceptance source |
| Grid supply | Phase, voltage, frequency and observations at the defined point |
| Protection | Device identity, settings, test method and result |
| Functional operation | Start, stop, shutdown and recovery behaviour required by the plan |
| Battery system | Approved compatibility, isolation, BMS and functional records |

Live testing and stored DC or battery energy can be hazardous. It belongs to qualified personnel using the required equipment and procedure. The owner handover may explain normal indicators and emergency contacts without asking the owner to reproduce commissioning tests.

## How should inverter settings be commissioned?

**Start from the approved settings schedule, then record the actual values applied to the exact inverter and firmware.** Grid profile, protection, export control, power factor, battery, meter and communications settings can affect safety, connection compliance and operation. Factory defaults are not evidence that the project is correctly configured.

The settings record should identify:

- Inverter model, firmware and configuration date.
- Approved grid or country profile and its source.
- Protection or plant-control settings that the project requires.
- Export-control mode, meter orientation and communications path.
- MPPT or string assignment where configurable.
- Battery model, BMS protocol, limits and backup mode for a hybrid system.
- User roles, remote access and firmware-update responsibility.
- Any deviation, authorisation and retest.

Do not widen voltage, frequency or protection settings to hide nuisance trips. Record the event and investigate the grid, wiring, model configuration and applicable requirements.

## How should anti-islanding and grid functions be verified?

**Verify grid-interface functions through the applicable approved procedure and model-specific evidence.** Record the exact inverter, firmware, test method, conditions, result and acceptance source. A certificate, site test and settings record answer different questions and should not be substituted for one another.

The [anti-islanding guide](/blog/anti-islanding-protection-solar-inverters/) explains why an ordinary grid-tied inverter must not be treated as outage backup. If the project includes a hybrid backup output, commission the isolation and designated-load arrangement separately from the utility-side connection.

Any witnessed DISCOM or third-party test should be included with the site record. Do not copy a trip time from a blog into the commissioning sheet.

## How should monitoring and communications be handed over?

**Commission monitoring as an operational system, not just a successful app login.** Verify the meter and logger identity, data direction, time settings, connectivity, user roles, alarm delivery and data ownership. Give the owner administrator access or a documented transfer route and record any subscription or network dependency.

Test and record:

- Inverter, meter, battery and gateway identities.
- Correct site and time-zone assignment.
- Live power direction and comparison with a trusted site observation.
- Alarm or event visibility appropriate to the platform.
- Owner, EPC and service roles with least-required access.
- Export or retention method for data needed by the owner.
- SIM, Wi-Fi, Ethernet or platform responsibilities and recurring charges.
- Credential recovery and account-transfer process.

A screenshot proves only what was visible at that time. Include account ownership and hardware details so another service provider can support the system later.

## What belongs in the commissioning report?

**The commissioning report should connect every result to equipment, method and acceptance evidence.** Include failed or deferred tests, corrective actions and retest results instead of issuing a clean summary that hides open work. The approver should be able to reproduce why the system was accepted.

Recommended report fields:

1. Project, owner, site and commissioning-party details.
2. Equipment schedule with model, serial number and firmware.
3. Approved drawing and document revisions.
4. Test instruments and calibration status.
5. Inspection records and photographs.
6. Test method, measured result, acceptance source and pass status.
7. Final settings and access-control record.
8. Grid, meter and connection documents applicable to the site.
9. Non-conformities, deviations, corrective actions and retests.
10. Witnesses, reviewers, signatures and date.

Do not describe the system as fully commissioned while critical tests, approvals or corrections remain open. Use a punch list with owners and closure evidence.

## What should the owner receive at handover?

**The owner should receive an indexed pack that supports safe operation, warranty, service and future changes.** Provide final documents, not working drafts, and explain which party owns each ongoing task. Handover is incomplete if the monitoring account, product registrations or as-built drawings remain only with the installer.

Use this handover index:

| Handover item | Minimum record |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment | Final schedule, invoices, models and serial numbers |
| Design | As-built single-line diagram, layout and approved changes |
| Commissioning | Signed report, settings, evidence and closed punch list |
| Product documents | Manuals, project-required certificates and warranties |
| Monitoring | Owner access, account recovery and hardware details |
| Grid and scheme | Relevant application, meter, inspection and approval records |
| Operation | Normal indicators, safe shutdown reference and emergency contacts |
| Maintenance | Defined tasks, responsible party and service route |

The [installation-versus-product warranty guide](/blog/solar-installation-vs-product-warranty/) explains why workmanship, product, performance and service commitments should remain separate in the pack.

## How should final acceptance be handled?

**Final acceptance should follow the contract's defined evidence and authority, not the calendar or a payment request.** Confirm the commissioned scope, close material defects, record accepted deviations, transfer documents and access, and identify any continuing observation period without mislabelling it as completed work.

The owner or authorised representative should understand normal operation, emergency contacts, warranty routes, monitoring access and maintenance responsibilities. Acceptance does not transfer hidden design or installation defects merely because the system is generating.

Before sign-off, verify that the serial numbers, settings record and as-built drawings match the equipment on site. Attach updated pages when a component was substituted.

## A commissioning review checklist for EPC teams

Before releasing the project, confirm that:

- The exact installed models match approved and handed-over documents.
- Test methods and limits cite the controlling source.
- Every instrument and result is traceable.
- Protection and grid settings match the approved schedule.
- Monitoring access and ownership are transferred.
- Warranty policies and registrations are retained.
- DISCOM or connection records are complete for the project stage.
- Deviations and open items have owners and closure dates.
- The owner has operation, shutdown and support information.
- Qualified reviewers have approved engineering and acceptance records.

[Download current Qbits datasheets](/download-datasheets/) and use the exact product manual when preparing the test plan. For model-specific documents, [contact Qbits](/contact-us/). Commissioning and energisation must remain with qualified parties and follow current project and local requirements.
