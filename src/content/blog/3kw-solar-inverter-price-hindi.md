> **ALMM and inverter compliance:** MNRE's current ALMM page publishes solar PV module and cell lists, not an inverter list. Verify the inverter's exact model documents and applicable scheme or DISCOM requirements separately.

**3 kW solar inverter ki koi ek bharosemand all-India retail price nahi hoti.** Search result mein dikhne wala number sirf inverter unit, tax ke bina dealer rate, ya poore installed system ka net estimate ho sakta hai. Sahi comparison tabhi hoga jab exact model aur quote ka scope dono same hon.

## 3 kW solar inverter ka rate kaise pata karein?

Kam se kam teen itemised quotes lijiye. Har quote mein yeh likha hona chahiye:

| Quote item | Kya verify karein |
| --- | --- |
| Inverter | Brand, exact model, 3 kW ya kVA rating, phase aur datasheet |
| Type | On-grid ya hybrid, aur backup output included hai ya nahi |
| Solar input | MPPT count, voltage window aur permitted DC oversizing |
| Protection | AC/DC isolator, SPD, MCB/RCCB aur earthing scope |
| Commercial terms | Tax included hai ya extra, delivery, installation aur payment milestones |
| Warranty | Product aur service terms, exclusions aur claim process |

Qbits ki public website par 23 September 2026 ko model-wise current retail price list nahi mili. Isliye is page par purane fixed market bands ko current fact ke roop mein repeat nahi kiya gaya hai. [Qbits product range](/our-products/) se exact model shortlist karke written quote maangein.

## On-grid aur hybrid quote ko ek jaisa kyun nahi samajhna chahiye?

**On-grid inverter ka main kaam solar DC ko grid-synchronised AC mein badalna hai.** Grid fail hone par anti-islanding protection ke kaaran normal on-grid system supply band kar deta hai. Hybrid inverter compatible battery, charging logic aur ek defined backup output bhi sambhal sakta hai, lekin har hybrid model ka behaviour ek jaisa nahi hota.

Hybrid quote mein inverter ke alawa battery, compatible BMS, essential-load distribution board, changeover arrangement aur extra commissioning ka scope dekhna zaroori hai. Sirf inverter box ka rate compare karna total ownership cost nahi batata. [On-grid aur hybrid comparison](/blog/on-grid-vs-hybrid/) mein operating difference detail mein diya gaya hai.

## 3 kW system price mein kya-kya shamil hona chahiye?

Ek complete rooftop quote mein aam taur par solar modules, inverter, mounting structure, DC aur AC cabling, connectors, protection devices, earthing, installation, testing aur documentation shamil hote hain. Metering ya DISCOM charges aur civil work quote mein included bhi ho sakte hain aur separate bhi.

Quote par do totals maangein:

1. **Gross payable amount:** taxes aur saare agreed line items ke saath.
2. **Expected assistance ke baad owner cost:** sirf illustration ke roop mein, approved subsidy samajhkar nahi.

Vendor se verbal “all inclusive” kehne ke badle itemised bill of quantities maangein. Isse missing structure, protection ya metering cost baad mein surprise nahi banegi.

## 3 kW par PM Surya Ghar calculation kya hai?

Eligible residential installation ke liye [MNRE residential CFA guidelines](https://mnre.gov.in/en/notice/operational-guidelines-for-implementation-of-the-component-central-financial-assistance-to-residential-consumers-of-pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli-yojana/) standard central calculation batati hain:

| Installed solar-module capacity | Standard central assistance |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 kW | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW ya usse zyada | Up to ₹78,000 |

Yeh inverter ka alag discount nahi hai. Amount eligibility, installed solar-module capacity, scheme compliance aur application approval par depend karta hai. Apne application ka current status aur amount [official PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) par check karein.

## 3 kW size sahi hai ya nahi kaise decide karein?

Inverter size ko sirf BHK ya monthly bill se fix na karein. Pichhle 12 mahine ki unit consumption, sanctioned load, phase, roof area, shading, proposed module capacity aur backup requirement dekhein. Hybrid system ke liye essential loads ki wattage aur required backup hours alag calculate karein.

[Solar inverter sizing guide](/blog/solar-inverter-sizing/) capacity decision mein madad karega. Agar 3 kW aur 5 kW ke beech decide kar rahe hain, [5 kW price guide](/blog/5kw-solar-inverter-price-hindi/) ko bhi isi itemised-quote method se padhein.

## Quote sign karne se pehle checklist

1. Exact inverter aur module model numbers likhit mein hon.
2. Gross price, tax aur installation scope alag dikh raha ho.
3. Warranty kis party ki hai aur claim kahan file hoga, clear ho.

5. Scheme amount ko approved payment nahi, estimated assistance ke roop mein dikhaya gaya ho.
6. Final payment milestone testing, documentation aur agreed handover se linked ho.
