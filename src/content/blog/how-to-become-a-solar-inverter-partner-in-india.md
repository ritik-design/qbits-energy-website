This guide presents the complete framework for evaluating, structuring, and executing inverter manufacturer partnerships in India: partnership model types, manufacturer evaluation criteria, territory structures and rights, investment and margin frameworks, training and support programs, application processes, and the success factors that separate sustainable 10-year partnerships from churn-and-burn experiences.

## Partnership Model Types

Indian inverter manufacturers typically offer three partnership models with distinct economics and operational profiles:

| Partnership model | Scope | Investment | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Distributor** | Buys bulk stock, warehouses, supplies dealers and EPCs across territory | ₹20-50 lakh+ | Established trading or distribution businesses |
| **Dealer** | Smaller scale, serves specific city or district | ₹3-10 lakh | Local electrical contractors, established retailers |
| **Channel Partner** | Combines sales, installation, and service operations | ₹5-25 lakh | EPCs adding inverter sourcing to existing services |

### Territory rights structures

- **National**: Single partner for entire country (rare, very large manufacturers only)
- **Regional**: 3-5 state cluster (north, west, south, east)
- **State-level**: Single state exclusive rights
- **Multi-city dealer**: 3-5 cities in a region
- **Single-city dealer**: One city or metro area
- **Channel partner**: Defined geographic boundary with EPC focus

Exclusivity vs non-exclusive arrangements vary by manufacturer. Exclusive rights provide market protection but require higher volume commitments; non-exclusive allows multiple partners in same territory but offers lower entry threshold.

## Evaluating Manufacturers: Critical Criteria

### 1. Product quality and certifications

Verifiable certifications:

- **BIS certification** under IS 16221 (mandatory)
- **ALMM Phase III listing** (mandatory for government projects)
- **IEC 62109 and IEC 61727** (safety and grid interface)
- **IP66 weather protection** per IEC 60529
- **Efficiency ratings** - target 98% peak, 97%+ weighted

Quality testing:

- **1,000+ automated tests per unit** indicates premium QC discipline
- **Statistical process control** documented
- **Component sourcing** verified (German-grade IGBTs, Japanese capacitors)

### 2. Warranty terms

| Warranty tier | Term | Partner advantage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | 5-7 years repair | Limited sales argument for premium clients |
| Standard | 8-10 years | Acceptable for residential markets |
| **Premium** | **12-year full replacement** | **Strong competitive differentiator with quality-conscious clients** |

Premium warranties materially improve partner sales conversion and protect long-term customer relationships.

### 3. Smart features

- **AI-powered monitoring** with predictive maintenance
- **WhatsApp integration** for India-appropriate alerting
- **Remote diagnostics** via Wi-Fi, 4G, Bluetooth
- **DC oversizing support** up to 130-150%
- **Fleet dashboards** for portfolio management

### 4. Manufacturer track record

- Years in business (5+ minimum, 10+ preferred)
- Verified installed base in India (1,000+ units minimum)
- Financial stability (audited financials, credit rating)
- Channel partner program maturity
- India-based service infrastructure

### 5. Service network

- Service centres mapped to target territories
- Response time SLAs by city tier
- Spare parts inventory levels in India
- Digital warranty system for fast claims

## Investment and Margin Framework

### Initial investment components

- **Initial stock purchase** - varies by territory size and inventory commitment
- **Working capital** for credit terms and inventory turnover
- **Warehousing** infrastructure (for distributors)
- **Service vehicle and equipment** if installation included
- **Marketing collateral** initial deployment
- **Training program enrollment** costs (sometimes manufacturer-subsidised)

### Margin structure

| Tier | Typical residential margin | Typical commercial margin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base distributor margin | 15-25% | 12-20% |
| Volume rebate at tier 1 | +2% | +1.5% |
| Volume rebate at tier 2 | +3% | +2.5% |
| Annual commitment bonus | +2-3% | +1-2% |
| New territory development bonus | +1-2% | +1% |
| Strategic partner premium | Negotiated | Negotiated |

### ROI timeline expectations

- **Months 1-6**: Market development, initial sales, working through learning curve
- **Months 7-12**: Volume ramp-up, dealer network development for distributors
- **Months 12-18**: Sustainable profitability with established customer base
- **Months 18-24**: Margin optimisation through volume rebates and operational efficiency

Faster ROI in high-opportunity states with active solar adoption; slower in markets the partner is essentially developing.

## Application Process

### Required documentation

- **GST registration certificate** (current)
- **Company registration** (proprietorship, partnership, LLP, or company)
- **Bank details** and 2-3 years of statements
- **Trade references** (3+ existing suppliers or customers)
- **Territory proposal** with market analysis and growth plan
- **Business plan** with revenue projections and resource commitment
- **Identity and address proof** of principals/directors
- **Tax compliance** certificates (GST, TDS, income tax)

### Typical application timeline

| Stage | Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Initial discussions and product evaluation | 1-2 weeks |
| Due diligence (documentation, references, territory analysis) | 2-4 weeks |
| Agreement signing and training program enrollment | 2-4 weeks |
| First stock delivery and channel enablement | 2-6 weeks |
| **Total time to active selling** | **8-12 weeks** |

Prepared candidates with documentation in order can complete the process at the lower end of the range.
