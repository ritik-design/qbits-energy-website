**For a new residential rooftop-solar application in Gujarat, start with the central [PM Surya Ghar portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) and the DISCOM named on your electricity bill.** The standard central assistance reaches a usual cap of ₹78,000 at 3 kW of eligible installed solar-module capacity. Do not assume that an older SURYA Gujarat state subsidy stacks on top of it. Ask for a current official order and an application-specific calculation before counting any extra state payment.

A quoted net cost or payback depends on verified assistance, your installed price, actual consumption and the current DISCOM tariff. Do not use a generic state top-up or export-price estimate as a confirmed payment.

## What subsidy can be included in a 2026 quote?

The [MNRE residential CFA guidelines](https://mnre.gov.in/en/notice/operational-guidelines-for-implementation-of-the-component-central-financial-assistance-to-residential-consumers-of-pm-surya-ghar-muft-bijli-yojana/) set out the central calculation for eligible households: ₹30,000 for the first kW, ₹30,000 for the second and ₹18,000 for the third, subject to the scheme rules. The resulting standard cap is ₹78,000 for 3 kW and larger eligible systems.

| Project item | Evidence to request |
| --- | --- |
| Central PM Surya Ghar assistance | Amount and status in your application under the current guidelines |
| Claimed Gujarat state top-up | Current government or DISCOM order, eligibility, claim channel and funding status |
| Full system cost | Itemised module, inverter, structure, electrical, meter, labour and tax prices |
| Export bill credit | Applicable DISCOM and GERC billing terms for your connection |

An installer should show gross project cost separately from assistance. A projected subsidy is not cash already received. The older [SURYA Gujarat scheme guide](/blog/surya-gujarat-scheme-guide/) explains why historical terms cannot establish today's state benefit.

## Which Gujarat DISCOM should you contact?

Check the company name on your bill. UGVCL, PGVCL, MGVCL and DGVCL serve different regions; some consumers are served by Torrent Power. The serving licensee handles connection-specific feasibility, inspection and metering. A district-level overview may be wrong for a particular address, so use your bill and the current portal record.

## Application and vendor checks

1. Check household eligibility and begin through the [official portal](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/).
2. Confirm system capacity and any connection changes with your DISCOM before work begins.
3. Verify the vendor's registration for your area in the portal. Use our [vendor-verification guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) when comparing quotations.
4. Request exact solar-module model numbers and their applicable [MNRE ALMM entry](https://mnre.gov.in/en/approved-list-of-models-and-manufacturers-almm/). ALMM's published lists are for modules and cells, not inverters. Request inverter grid and safety documents separately.
5. Retain written approvals, invoice, installation details and commissioning records. Track the actual application stage rather than relying on a standard timeline from a sales page.

## How to estimate the real net cost

Start with an itemised gross quote and subtract only the assistance for which your application is eligible. Keep any unverified state benefit outside the base case. Then ask for a site-specific monthly generation estimate with roof shade, orientation, weather inputs and system losses identified. Compare it with your current bill and the DISCOM's actual import and export treatment. The [solar quotation checklist](/blog/solar-quotation-checklist/) and [irradiance guide](/blog/solar-irradiance-data-india-statewise/) help assess those assumptions.

No single Gujarat payback period applies to every roof. Installation price, consumption pattern, tariff slab, shade and export credit can move the outcome materially.
