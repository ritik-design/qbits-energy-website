## OpenSolar Pricing Structure

| Tier | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry (Free) | $0 | Simple residential, simplified shading |
| Pro / add-ons | Per feature | Hardware fees, proposal templates, shading |
| Hardware-routed | Implicit | Margin baked into hardware integrations |

OpenSolar does not publish a single seat-based price for the full feature set. The realised cost is the sum of paid add-ons used.
