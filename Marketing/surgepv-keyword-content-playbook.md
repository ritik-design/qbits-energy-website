## 3. SEO Guidelines

### 3.1 On-page basics (every article)
- **Title tag** ≤ 60 chars, primary keyword in first 50 chars, plus year (2026) where it fits
- **Meta description** ≤ 155 chars, includes primary keyword + a benefit number ("cuts design time 70%") + CTA verb ("Compare", "See", "Book")
- **H1** in frontmatter title only — never repeat as `# Heading` in the MDX body
- **One H2 per major section**, primary keyword in at least one H2 verbatim
- **Primary keyword density** 0.8%–1.5% (natural — do not force)
- **LSI / semantic terms** must appear: PV, photovoltaic, kW, MW, irradiance, yield, simulation, proposal, installer, EPC
- **First 100 words** must contain primary keyword once
- **Slug** = primary keyword in kebab-case, no stop words ("the", "for", "and") unless required for sense

### 3.2 Internal linking rules per article
- **Minimum 3 internal links to SurgePV pages** per article (from §2.1)
- **At least 1 of those 3 must be to `/` or `/demo`**
- **At least 1 must use an exact-match keyword anchor** (e.g. *solar design software* → `/`)
- **Maximum 2 sister-brand links** per article — only when the topic genuinely warrants it (do not bolt on)
- Avoid stacking links in the same paragraph — space them out across sections
- Link from the *first natural occurrence* of an anchor, not the heading
- Open external links with `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`; internal links never use target=_blank

### 3.3 Schema requirements
Every article in this cluster gets at minimum:
- `Article` schema with author, datePublished, dateModified
- `FAQPage` schema mirroring the FAQ block
- `BreadcrumbList`

Comparison / "best of" / "alternative" articles additionally get:
- `SoftwareApplication` schema for SurgePV (already in site-wide schema — do not duplicate; reference it via `@id`)
- `ItemList` listing the products being compared

### 3.4 Image SEO
- Hero image filename: `[primary-keyword-slug].png` — under `/public/images/blog-[slug].png`
- Alt text contains primary keyword once, descriptive, ≤ 125 chars
- All diagrams/screenshots labeled with "SurgePV" so screenshot-scrapers credit us

### 3.5 E-E-A-T requirements
Every article includes:
- Named author with title + 1-line credential
- Named editor
- `authorBio` field in frontmatter, ≥ 30 words, includes years of experience or project count
- At least one piece of *original* data (chart, internal stat, screenshot, quote) — see §6 outlines

### 3.6 Year / freshness
- Always include the current year in the H1 and intro for time-sensitive topics ("best", "pricing", "alternative")
- Update `updatedDate` field on any refresh
- Re-publish quarterly for the top 10 money keywords

### 3.7 Cannibalization rules
- If a keyword overlaps with an existing SurgePV blog or review page, **expand the existing page rather than publish a new one**
- Already-published SurgePV pages to check first:
  - `/reviews/aurora-solar`, `/reviews/helioscope`, `/reviews/pvsyst`, `/reviews/opensolar`, `/reviews/scanifly`, `/reviews/pylon`, `/reviews/arka360`, `/reviews/pvcase`, `/reviews/ratedpower`, `/reviews/solargraf`, `/reviews/enact-solar` (and many more — check `/src/content/reviews/`)
  - `/blog/ai-solar-design-software`, `/blog/best-solar-design-software-guide`, `/blog/commercial-solar-design-software-buyer-guide`, etc. (check `/src/content/blog/`)
- The "Best X" review page and the "X alternative" blog page are **distinct intents** and should be separate URLs — review = informational, alternative = switch-intent commercial

## 4. Copywriting Guidelines (the demo-booking part)

### 4.1 Voice
Professional, direct, opinionated. Short sentences. Specific numbers. No hedge words. Talk to a solar EPC owner who designs 30 systems a month, not to a student.

### 4.2 Reader → demo journey (CRO model)
Every article has to do four jobs in order:
1. **Confirm the reader is in the right place** — restate their pain in the first 80 words
2. **Demonstrate authority** — one data point or specific number in the first 200 words
3. **Recommend the answer (SurgePV)** with reasons grounded in features from §1.3
4. **Make booking a demo the obvious next step** — minimum 3 CTAs per article (top, mid, bottom)

### 4.4 In-paragraph CTA phrases (use 3–5 per article)
- *"You can [book a free SurgePV demo](/demo) and design one of your real projects on the call."*
- *"[SurgePV](/) does this in a single workflow."*
- *"Compare [SurgePV pricing](/pricing) to your current tool — it's the same outputs at a fraction of the cost."*
- *"Try the [solar shading analysis](/shadow-analysis) module — same 8,760-hour engine, no PVsyst desktop install."*
- *"Generate a [branded solar proposal](/solar-proposals) in 5 minutes flat."*

### 4.5 What never to write
- "It depends" without follow-up specifics
- "There are many great options" (you sell one of them — say so)
- "We may be biased" disclaimers — readers know who wrote the page
- Generic "look no further" / "search no more" filler
- Feature lists without a benefit-per-feature payoff
- Promises we cannot defend (e.g. "100% accurate")

### 4.6 Comparison-section template (used in every alternative / "best of" article)
Whenever a competitor is named, follow this structure:

> **[Competitor Name]**
> Best for: [actual best-fit ICP, honestly stated]
> Strengths: [2 real strengths]
> Weaknesses: [2 real weaknesses, with specifics — not "expensive", say "$159/user/mo minimum"]
> SurgePV vs [Competitor]: [1 sentence on the swap-out reason]

Honest weaknesses with specifics is what makes the recommendation credible. Vague trash-talk reads as bias and tanks bounce rate.

### 4.7 FAQ block (every article — minimum 5 questions)
Mirror the questions actual searchers type into Google (use AlsoAsked / People-Also-Ask / Search Console). At least one FAQ must contain the primary keyword. Each answer 40–80 words, direct, no fluff. Mirror in `faqs[]` frontmatter for schema.
