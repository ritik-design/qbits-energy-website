Searching for a solar panel subsidy form download leads most homeowners to a tangle of state portals, PDF links that no longer work, and forum threads that reference outdated processes. The reason for the confusion is straightforward: the PM Surya Ghar subsidy application in 2026 is not a downloadable paper form, it is an end-to-end online process on pmsuryaghar.gov.in. But it works exactly like a structured form, and filling it correctly the first time is what separates applicants who get their subsidy in 45 days from those who spend months resolving rejections.

> **Direct answer.** The PM Surya Ghar solar panel subsidy form is filled entirely online at pmsuryaghar.gov.in, there is no standalone PDF to download for the central scheme. After Aadhaar eKYC registration, the application captures your electricity bill details, personal documents, proposed system capacity, installer selection, and bank account for Direct Benefit Transfer. This guide walks through every field, describes common errors, and explains what confirmation to expect at each stage using the 5-Stage Application Tracker framework.

This guide treats the portal application as a structured form with distinct sections, explains what each section requires, and identifies the exact fields where applicants most commonly make errors, errors that lead to DISCOM rejection and weeks of delay. If you have already submitted and want to understand where your application stands, the [PM Surya Ghar status check guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-status-check/) covers the tracking process in full. ## Before You Start: What to Gather

Completing the online application in one session is strongly recommended. Partially completed applications time out after 30 minutes of inactivity on the portal. Before opening the form, assemble the following:

- **Electricity bill**: the most recent bill from your DISCOM, showing your consumer number, name, address, and tariff category. The consumer number is typically a 10 to 14-digit code at the top of the bill.
- **Aadhaar card**: the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar must be active and receiving SMS. The portal uses Aadhaar OTP for identity verification.
- **PAN card**: required for applicants whose system capacity is 3 kW or above, as the subsidy amount exceeds ₹50,000 and PAN is required for income tax reporting by the disbursing agency.
- **Bank passbook or cancelled cheque**: showing account number, IFSC code, and account holder name. The name must match your Aadhaar name exactly.
- **Roof photograph**: a clear photograph of the installation area on your roof (at least one image, JPEG format, under 2 MB). Some DISCOMs require a geo-tagged photograph taken from your phone's camera with location services enabled.
- **Property proof** (if required by your DISCOM), for flat residents, a No Objection Certificate from the building owner or housing society may be needed before the DISCOM will approve a shared-roof installation.

Scan all documents at 200 to 300 DPI. Files above 2 MB will not upload successfully and will stall the application mid-submission. Use a free PDF compressor or image resizer if your scans are large.

> **20 to 40 minutes.** The typical time to complete the PM Surya Ghar online application when documents are ready and pre-scanned. Applications attempted without pre-prepared documents average 90 to 120 minutes and have a significantly higher error rate. *Source - [PM Surya Ghar National Portal user guidance](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, 2026.*

## Step 1: Finding the Application Form on the Portal

The PM Surya Ghar portal at pmsuryaghar.gov.in is the only authorised national portal for the central government residential solar subsidy scheme. Be cautious of look-alike websites that use similar domain names, the official portal always ends in `.gov.in`.

On the homepage, look for one of two entry points:

- **"Apply for Rooftop Solar"**: a prominent button on the home banner. This takes you directly to the registration page.
- **"Consumer Login / Register"**: in the top navigation menu. Use this if you have visited before but did not complete registration.

**Image alt text: PM Surya Ghar homepage banner showing the "Apply for Rooftop Solar" button in brand green with the tagline "300 units of free electricity per month" visible above it.**

Both routes lead to the same entry point: the Consumer Registration and eKYC screen.

If you prefer to review the full form offline before filling, the portal's "Help and FAQ" section (accessible from the footer) contains a downloadable form preview document that shows all fields without the live data-entry requirement. This preview is useful for preparing information in advance but cannot be submitted, only the online submission counts.

## Step 2: Consumer Registration and Aadhaar eKYC

This is the first section of the application form. It establishes your identity and links your application to a verified mobile number and Aadhaar record.

**Fields in Section 1, Registration:**

- **Mobile number**: enter the mobile number currently active and linked to your Aadhaar. The portal sends an OTP to this number. If your Aadhaar-linked number is not the one you use daily, update it at the nearest Aadhaar Enrolment Centre before starting.
- **State**: select your state from the dropdown. This determines which DISCOMs are shown in subsequent steps.
- **DISCOM**: select your electricity distribution company. If your city has multiple DISCOMs (Delhi has BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna, and TPDDL; Mumbai has MSEDCL, BEST, and Tata Power), select the one whose name appears on your electricity bill header.
- **Consumer account number**: enter the consumer number from your electricity bill exactly as printed, including any leading zeros. Do not enter the separate account number that some DISCOMs show on bills, the portal specifically requires the consumer number.

**Common errors in Section 1:**

- **Account number vs consumer number**: both appear on some bills; the consumer number is usually labelled "CA No." or "Consumer No." or "Service No." The account number is a financial billing reference and will not match the DISCOM database.
- **Wrong DISCOM selected**: selecting MSEDCL instead of BEST for a Mumbai consumer, or JVVNL instead of AVVNL for a Rajasthan consumer. If you are unsure, check the bill header for the DISCOM name.
- **Inactive or non-Aadhaar-linked mobile number**: the OTP arrives but the eKYC step fails because the number is not registered with UIDAI. The fix is Aadhaar-mobile linking at an enrolment centre; this takes 24 to 72 hours to reflect in the UIDAI database.

After entering your mobile number, click "Get OTP." Enter the OTP within 5 minutes. You are then directed to Aadhaar eKYC, enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number and verify with another OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile. Upon successful eKYC, your name and address are auto-fetched from UIDAI records and appear in the form.

## Step 3: Electricity Connection and Property Details

Section 2 captures information about your electricity connection and the installation property. This is where document mismatch errors most commonly occur, leading to the largest category of DISCOM rejections.

**Fields in Section 2:**

- **Consumer name on bill**: auto-populated if the Aadhaar name matches the consumer record. If there is a mismatch, the field appears in red. Do not attempt to override a mismatch by typing a different name, this causes rejection at DISCOM verification. The correct fix is to update the name on your electricity consumer record at your DISCOM's customer care office.
- **Installation address**: the full address where the solar system will be installed. For most applicants this is the same as the electricity bill address. For flat residents, enter the exact unit address including flat number, floor, and wing.
- **Connection category**: the portal auto-fetches this from your consumer number. Verify that it shows "Domestic" or "Residential." If Commercial, Industrial, or Agricultural appears, your connection classification must be corrected at the DISCOM before you can proceed. The [DISCOM glossary entry](/glossary/discom/) explains what a consumer category correction involves.
- **Sanctioned load (kW)**: the contracted electricity demand for your connection, shown on your bill. This is a critical field: a sanctioned load of 5 kW allows a solar inverter up to approximately 5 kW AC capacity; proposing a higher capacity inverter will be flagged by the technical reviewer.
- **Ownership type**: select Owner (if you own the property) or Tenant (if renting). Tenant applications require a landlord NOC in most states and take longer to process.

## Step 4: Proposed System Details

Section 3 captures the technical specification of your proposed solar installation. Many applicants fill this section incorrectly because they do not yet have an installer's proposal in hand. Engage an empanelled installer and obtain a Bill of Quantities before reaching this section.

### The 4-Field System Specification Checklist

1. **Proposed system capacity (kW)**: enter the AC nameplate capacity of the solar inverter you intend to install. For PM Surya Ghar subsidy calculations, the capacity must be 10 kW or below for residential applications. Central subsidy is calculated on up to 3 kW. Entering a system capacity disproportionate to your sanctioned load results in a technical rejection.
2. **System type**: select from the dropdown: Grid-Connected (On-Grid), Grid-Connected with Battery (Hybrid), or Off-Grid. Off-grid systems are not eligible for PM Surya Ghar central subsidy. Both on-grid and hybrid selections qualify for the subsidy.
3. **Panel technology**: select the module type (Monocrystalline PERC, TOPCon, HJT, or Polycrystalline). This must match your installer's proposed module type exactly. The DISCOM cross-references this against the ALMM list.
4. **Inverter type**: select string inverter or microinverter. For most residential applications, string inverter is the correct selection. The [net metering glossary entry](/glossary/net-metering/) explains how grid-connected inverters interact with the net meter the DISCOM will install.

The table below summarises the system type options and their subsidy eligibility, so you select the correct option in the dropdown:

| System Type | Grid Connection | Subsidy Eligible | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grid-Connected (On-Grid) | ✓ Exports surplus to grid | ✓ Full PM Surya Ghar subsidy | Homes with reliable grid; fastest payback |
| Grid-Connected with Battery (Hybrid) | ✓ Exports surplus to grid | ✓ Subsidy on solar component | Homes with frequent power cuts needing backup |
| Off-Grid | ✗ No grid connection | ✗ Not eligible for central subsidy | Remote areas without DISCOM connectivity |

Do not estimate the system capacity yourself. Ask your empanelled installer for a formal system design proposal that accounts for your sanctioned load, roof area, shading analysis, and annual consumption pattern. An incorrectly sized system affects both the subsidy calculation and your payback period.

## Step 5: Document Upload Section

Section 4 is the document upload section. Upload errors cause the most preventable rejections, taking an extra 10 minutes here saves weeks of delay later.

**Required uploads and specifications:**

| Document | Format | Max Size | Key Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Electricity bill (latest month) | JPEG or PDF | 2 MB | Consumer number, name, and tariff clearly visible |
| Aadhaar card (front and back) | JPEG or PDF | 2 MB each | Both sides required; name must match bill |
| PAN card | JPEG or PDF | 2 MB | Mandatory for systems 3 kW and above |
| Cancelled cheque or bank passbook | JPEG or PDF | 2 MB | Account number, IFSC, and name visible |
| Roof photograph | JPEG | 2 MB | Clear image of installation area; geo-tagged if possible |
| NOC from building owner (if applicable) | JPEG or PDF | 2 MB | Required for tenants and flat owners in most states |

**Common upload errors and their fixes:**

- **Blurry scans**: photographs taken with a shaking hand or in poor light obscure numbers. Re-scan at 200 DPI minimum using a flatbed scanner or a scanning app like Adobe Scan.
- **Cropped documents**: Aadhaar card scanned with edges cut off, obscuring the Aadhaar number or address. Ensure all four edges are visible in the scan.
- **Wrong document in the wrong slot**: uploading the bank passbook in the electricity bill slot. The portal labels each slot but does not verify content type automatically. Double-check each upload.
- **Files over 2 MB**: the upload button appears to accept large files in some browsers but the submission fails silently. Compress images using a free online tool, targeting under 1.5 MB per file.
- **Password-protected PDFs**: some banks issue PDF statements with password protection. Open in a PDF reader and save without the password before uploading.

## Step 6: Bank Account Details for Subsidy Disbursal

Section 5 captures the bank account details for Direct Benefit Transfer. The solar panel subsidy of up to ₹78,000 is transferred to this account after the DISCOM confirms installation.

**Fields in Section 5:**

- **Account holder name**: must match your Aadhaar name exactly. If your bank account uses a shortened name, update the bank record before submitting.
- **Bank name**: select from the dropdown. For smaller cooperative banks not in the list, use the "Other Bank" option.
- **Account number**: the full account number as on your passbook. Re-enter in the confirmation field to prevent typing errors.
- **IFSC code**: the 11-character code for your specific branch. Verify on your bank's official website; branch codes change after bank mergers.
- **Aadhaar-bank linkage confirmation**: tick the checkbox only if the account is Aadhaar-seeded. Verify at the [NPCI Aadhaar mapper](https://www.npci.org.in/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} before ticking.

If your account is not Aadhaar-seeded, visit your bank branch with your Aadhaar card to complete seeding before submitting the form. The seeding reflects in the NPCI system within 24 to 72 hours. Submitting without a seeded account causes the subsidy to be returned by the bank after disbursal attempt, triggering a long re-processing cycle.

## Step 7: Installer (Vendor) Selection

Section 6 is the installer selection section. Selecting the wrong installer (particularly one who is not currently empanelled) is one of the fastest routes to application rejection.

**How to use the section:**

The portal displays a filtered list of empanelled vendors based on your state and pincode. Each listing shows the vendor's registration number, company name, contact details, and completed PM Surya Ghar installation count.

- **Verify empanelment is current**: empanelment can lapse. Even if a vendor shows on the list today, confirm their registration expiry date with the vendor directly.
- **Check installation count**: vendors with more completed PM Surya Ghar installations understand the portal submission requirements, which is as important as their technical competence.
- **Verify ALMM compliance**: contact 2 to 3 shortlisted vendors and ask them to share the ALMM certificate numbers for the specific solar panel and inverter models they propose. Cross-check against the live MNRE ALMM list at [mnre.gov.in/almm-list](https://mnre.gov.in/almm-list/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

The [ALMM list guide](/blog/almm-list-phase-iii-guide/) explains how to read the MNRE list and verify specific model compliance. The [empanelled vendor guide](/blog/empanelled-vendor-pm-surya-ghar/) provides additional criteria for evaluating vendor credibility beyond their portal registration status.

When comparing two empanelled vendors for your installation, use this framework to make the final choice:

| | Experienced Vendor (50+ installations) | New Vendor (5–20 installations) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Portal submission** | ✓ Knows the upload process; fewer errors | ✗ May need guidance on completion report upload |
| **ALMM equipment** | ✓ Usually stocks pre-verified ALMM items | ✗ May need prompting to confirm ALMM certificate numbers |
| **Speed** | ✓ Familiar with DISCOM timelines in your area | ✗ May not know DISCOM-specific quirks |
| **Pricing** | ✗ May charge a slight premium for experience | ✓ May offer lower price to build portfolio |
| **Service after sale** | ✓ Established local presence and track record | ✗ Difficult to assess long-term reliability |

After selection, the vendor receives a portal notification to upload the system design and Bill of Quantities. Follow up with your selected vendor within 48 hours to ensure they have received the notification and begun the upload process.

## Step 8: Declaration and Submission

Section 7 is the declaration section, the final step before submission. Read the full declaration text before ticking.

**The declaration covers:**

- Confirmation that all provided information is accurate.
- Acknowledgement that false information will result in rejection and recovery of any disbursed subsidy.
- Consent for the DISCOM to conduct a physical site inspection.
- Consent for DBT transfer to your declared bank account.
- Agreement that ALMM-non-compliant equipment voids the subsidy.

After ticking the declaration checkbox, click **"Submit Application."** The portal generates a confirmation screen with:

- **Application ID**: save this immediately (screenshot or write it down).
- **Submission timestamp**: date and time of submission.
- **DISCOM reference number**: for your DISCOM's internal tracking.

An SMS is sent to your registered mobile number with all three details. An email confirmation is also sent if you provided an email address during registration.

## The 5-Stage Application Tracker: What Happens After Submission

Once submitted, your application moves through five defined stages. Tracking these stages tells you whether action is needed from your side or whether the delay is on the DISCOM.

### The 5-Stage PM Surya Ghar Application Tracker

1. **DISCOM Document Review**: the DISCOM officer reviews documents for completeness, cross-references your consumer number, connection category, and identity documents. Expected duration: 7 to 21 working days. Action needed from you only if the DISCOM posts a correction request.
2. **Site Inspection**: a field officer visits your property or conducts a remote drone survey. They assess roof suitability, structural integrity, and grid connectivity. Expected duration: 15 to 30 working days from document approval. Action needed: ensure roof access is available and an adult is home if a physical inspection is scheduled.
3. **Technical Feasibility Approval**: the DISCOM issues a formal approval letter, uploaded to your dashboard. This authorises your installer to begin work. Expected duration: 5 to 10 working days after inspection. Action needed: notify your installer immediately upon approval.
4. **Installation and Net Meter Commissioning**: your installer proceeds with physical installation, then uploads the completion report and system photographs. The DISCOM schedules net meter installation. Expected duration: 30 to 60 days. Action needed: pay the installer as per your agreement; follow up if completion report upload is delayed.
5. **Subsidy Disbursal**: after the commissioning certificate is uploaded, MNRE processes the DBT via PFMS. Expected duration: 20 to 30 working days after commissioning certificate. Action needed: none, monitor your bank account for the credit.

> **45 to 90 days.** The typical end-to-end timeline from solar panel subsidy form submission to subsidy bank credit, based on applicant experience compiled by [Mercom India](https://www.mercomindia.com/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} in their Q1 2026 India Solar Market Report. Gujarat and Rajasthan average 45 to 60 days; Uttar Pradesh and Bihar average 70 to 90 days due to DISCOM capacity constraints.

## Escalation if the Form or Process Stalls

If your application shows no movement for more than 30 working days at any stage, use these escalation channels in order:

- **"Raise Grievance"** on the portal, under the "Help" menu. Carries a mandatory 30-day resolution obligation on the DISCOM.
- **National helpline 1800-180-3333**: toll-free, Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Have your Application ID ready.
- **State Nodal Agency**: each state's SREDA or energy department has a dedicated PM Surya Ghar officer for escalated cases.
- **SERC complaint**: for persistent DISCOM non-compliance, a consumer complaint to the State Electricity Regulatory Commission under Section 42 of the Electricity Act 2003. Contact details for all SERCs are available at [cerc.gov.in](https://www.cerc.gov.in/){target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

For a full list of state-specific supplementary forms (Gujarat Surya Gujarat form, Tamil Nadu TEDA form, Maharashtra MSEDCL physical net metering form), check your respective state DISCOM website. The [PM Surya Ghar Yojana complete guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-yojana-complete-guide/) links to each state's specific requirements.
