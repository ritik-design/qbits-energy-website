## What is BESS

BESS (Battery Energy Storage System) is a grid-connected or behind-the-meter battery storage installation that stores electrical energy in batteries for later discharge. BESS ranges in scale from kWh residential home batteries to GWh utility-scale grid storage. The term BESS is most commonly applied to utility-scale and large commercial deployments providing grid services.

> [!NOTE] In short
> - BESS is battery storage at any scale, from kWh residential home batteries to GWh utility installations, though the term is most often used for utility and large commercial deployments.
> - A complete BESS bundles the battery (usually LFP), a BMS, a Power Conversion System, an Energy Management System, thermal management and fire suppression.
> - India's Viability Gap Funding scheme backed 4,000 MWh of BESS in 2023 and a further 30 GWh in 2025; CEA's stated requirement is about 236 GWh by 2031-32.
> - BESS capex varies with chemistry, duration and scope and has been falling, so use recent tender results and current quotes rather than a fixed per-MWh figure.
> - BESS provides renewable firming, frequency regulation, peak shaving and blackstart, on top of storing solar and wind generation for round-the-clock dispatch.
> - Utility LFP BESS is rated for 15 to 20 years calendar life and 5,000 to 8,000 cycles.

A complete BESS includes:
- Battery (most commonly [LFP](/glossary/lfp-battery/) for stationary)
- [Battery Management System (BMS)](/glossary/bms/)
- Power Conversion System (PCS): bidirectional [hybrid inverter](/glossary/hybrid-inverter/)
- Energy Management System (EMS): control software
- Thermal management: cooling and ventilation
- Safety systems: fire suppression, monitoring against [thermal runaway](/glossary/thermal-runaway/)

For Indian solar, BESS is rapidly emerging as a critical complement to renewable generation. the [Viability Gap Funding](/glossary/viability-gap-funding/) (VGF) scheme approved in 2023 backed 4,000 MWh of grid-connected BESS, and a second tranche in 2025 supports 30 GWh. Major developers (Adani Green, ReNew Power, Tata Power Renewables, NTPC, JSW Energy) are building Indian BESS pipelines. CEA has put the requirement at about 236 GWh by 2031-32.

## Why BESS matters

Renewable integration. Enables high renewable penetration with grid stability.

Grid services. Frequency regulation, voltage support, blackstart.

Round-the-clock power. Solar plus BESS provides firm dispatch.

Peak shaving. C&I customers reduce peak demand charges.

Indian commitment. Aligns with 500 GW renewable by 2030.

Storage transition. Moves grid from synchronous to inverter-dominated.

### BESS economics

A BESS cost stack has cells, pack and BMS, power conversion system (PCS), containerisation, and EPC, civil and installation work. ## Best practices

Choose LFP for stationary BESS.

Specify quality BMS with thermal management.

Design for 4-hour or 2-hour application as appropriate, sized around usable capacity and [round-trip efficiency](/glossary/round-trip-efficiency/) rather than nominal capacity alone.

> [!CAUTION] Fire safety is part of the system, not an extra
> A complete BESS includes thermal management, fire suppression and monitoring against thermal runaway. The page's best practices call for fire suppression per UL 9540A and a quality BMS with thermal management, and UL 9540A is the fire test methodology in the standards table below.

Implement fire suppression per UL 9540A.

Plan replacement at expected end of life, or evaluate [second-life battery](/glossary/second-life-battery/) repurposing for retired cells.

Document grid services capability, including [grid-forming inverter](/glossary/grid-forming-inverter/) mode where required.

## Indian market context

VGF support has improved BESS economics.

MNRE's Solar Systems, Devices and Components Goods Order, 2025 brings storage batteries into a conformity regime; ALMM itself lists only PV modules and cells.

PLI for advanced chemistry cells (ACC) building domestic capability.

Major utility tenders attracting global and Indian developers.

State adoption: Karnataka, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh leading.
