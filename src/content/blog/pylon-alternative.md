## Why Installers Look for a Pylon Alternative

Pylon's strength is residential proposal velocity in the US. The tool moves quickly through a residential layout and produces a clean, sales-led proposal. Teams shopping for alternatives are typically not unhappy with the residential workflow. They are unhappy with what happens past the residential ceiling.

### Engineering depth is thin

Pylon's engineering tooling is shallower than Aurora, HelioScope, or SurgePV. 8,760-hour module-level shading is not the headline. Bankable P50, P75, P90 outputs are not produced. For installers growing into C&I or PPA-funded projects, this is a hard ceiling. SurgePV ships the full [8,760-hour shade analysis](https://surgepv.com/shadow-analysis) engine on every paid plan.

### Multi-country coverage is light

Pylon is US-focused. Code libraries for India (IS), Australia (AS/NZS), the UK (BS 7671), and Germany (VDE) are light or missing. Tariff coverage is similar. Teams expanding internationally hit the wall fast.

### Per-feature pricing

Pylon's pricing is structured around feature modules. As teams use more of the product, the realised cost rises. Flat per-seat plans like SurgePV's $1,299 to $1,899 per user per year are often cheaper at team scale once usage matures.
