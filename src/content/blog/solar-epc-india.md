The Indian solar sector is growing at unprecedented speed, with EPC companies at the front line. Success depends on delivering reliable, high-performance installations that generate consistent returns for clients. This guide walks through every phase of solar EPC delivery, from project planning to warranty management.

## Understanding Solar EPC Services

EPC encompasses the complete lifecycle of solar projects, integrating **engineering design, equipment procurement, and construction execution** into a single delivery model. The role is part technical consultant, part project manager, part operations lead.

### Key Challenges Facing Indian EPCs

- Equipment reliability under harsh weather conditions
- Warranty and support gaps impacting client relationships
- Monitoring and maintenance complexity
- High-wattage panel (750W+) compatibility
- Price pressure vs quality balance

## 1. Project Planning and Site Assessment

### Site Evaluation

- **Rooftop surveys**: structural load capacity, accessibility, condition
- **Shading analysis**: water tanks, parapets, adjacent buildings
- **Orientation documentation**: tilt, azimuth, distinct roof planes
- **Ground-mounted sites**: topography, soil conditions, accessibility

### Load Analysis and System Sizing

- Review **at least 12 months** of electricity bills
- Identify peak demand, seasonal variation, and time-of-day usage
- Optimise sizing for self-consumption vs grid export

### Technical Requirements and Compliance

All equipment must carry:

- **BIS certification** (Bureau of Indian Standards), mandatory
- **IEC 62109**: inverter safety
- **IEC 61683**: inverter efficiency

Document approval workflow early. Regulatory needs vary:

| Project type | Approvals |
| --- | --- |
| Residential | Net metering, electrical safety certificate, building permission |
| Commercial | Sanctioned load modification, fire safety, environmental impact |

## 2. Engineering and Design Phase

### System Architecture

Choose between:

- **On-grid**: simpler, lower cost, ideal for stable-grid sites
- **Hybrid**: battery integration, backup power, time-of-use optimisation

### Inverter Selection: The Heart of the Project

Critical criteria:

- **DC oversizing** support (100%+ for high-wattage panels)
- **Efficiency** (98%+)
- **Weather protection** (IP66-rated)
- **MPPT voltage range** and start-up characteristics
- **Monitoring** (AI analytics, mobile, instant alerts)
- **Warranty** (12-year full replacement)

### Electrical Design

- DC wiring from panels to inverter
- AC wiring to distribution board
- **DC and AC surge protection devices (SPDs)**
- Isolation switches and labelling per BIS/IEC standards

## 3. Procurement: Sourcing Quality Components

Procurement considerations:

| Factor | What to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Warranty | 12-year full replacement vs 5-year limited |
| After-sales | Service network coverage, India-based support |
| Quality testing | 1,000+ automated tests per unit |
| Components | German-grade electronics vs generic |
| Supply chain | Lead times, delivery reliability |
| TCO | 25-year cost analysis, not unit price alone |

## 4. Construction and Installation

### Safety Protocols

- Fall protection
- Electrical safety procedures
- Tool handling and PPE
- Daily safety briefings

### Mounting Structure

- Verify roof load capacity
- Waterproof penetration points
- Level and aligned mounting rails

### Panel Installation

- Planned orientation and tilt
- Consistent spacing for airflow and maintenance
- Tight, insulated electrical connections
- String configuration matching inverter MPPT specifications

### Inverter Positioning

- Shaded, well-ventilated locations away from direct sun
- Adequate clearance for heat dissipation (30–50 cm)
- Minimise exposure in coastal or industrial areas

## 5. Commissioning and Testing

### Electrical Testing

- **Insulation resistance** (DC+/ground, DC-/ground, AC/ground), must exceed 1 megohm
- **Polarity verification** on every DC string and AC connection
- **Grounding continuity**: target **<5 ohms**
- **String voltage and current** vs expected values

### Inverter Configuration

- Grid voltage and frequency limits per utility requirements
- Power factor and reactive power parameters
- Monitoring system connectivity
- Anti-islanding and SPD verification

### Performance Verification

- Operate under observation for several hours
- Document generation data and inverter efficiency
- Establish baseline reference data

### Client Handover Training

- Monitoring system interface walkthrough
- Performance data interpretation
- Alert notification setup

## 6. Advanced Monitoring Systems

### Real-Time Tracking

- Current power output and daily energy
- Inverter efficiency and grid voltage
- AI-powered anomaly detection

### WhatsApp Monitoring Integration

- Instant status notifications through a familiar channel
- Performance milestones and issue alerts
- Higher client engagement than traditional apps

### Remote Troubleshooting

- Detailed diagnostic data (string voltages, inverter temperature, error history)
- Parameter adjustment and simple fix guidance
- Significant reduction in costly site visits

### Preventive Maintenance Analytics

- Trend analysis identifies gradual degradation
- Soiling and shading change detection
- Scheduling based on actual conditions, not arbitrary intervals

## 7. Warranty Management and Long-Term Success

### Understanding Warranty Terms

A **12-year full replacement warranty** provides significantly more protection than 5-year pro-rated options.

### Standard Maintenance Tasks

- Panel cleaning (region-dependent frequency)
- Electrical connection inspection
- Inverter filter cleaning (where equipped)
- Performance verification vs baseline

### Common Issues in Indian Installations

- **Dust accumulation**: reduces panel output, causes inverter derating
- **Voltage fluctuations**: trigger protection mechanisms
- **High ambient temperatures**: cause thermal derating
