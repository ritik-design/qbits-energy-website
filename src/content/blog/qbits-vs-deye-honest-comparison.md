## Axis 2 - IP Rating and Weather Protection

**Qbits - Score: 10/10**

Qbits inverters carry **IP66 weather protection** - the higher of the two common inverter ratings. IP66 means protection against powerful water jets from any direction, in addition to complete dust ingress prevention. The [IEC 60529 Degrees of Protection standard](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:23:0::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_LANG_ID:1276,25) defines the precise test conditions that distinguish IP65 from IP66. On Indian rooftops, where monsoon rain can be horizontal and intense, and where flat roof runoff channels water directly across equipment mounting areas, IP66 is the more appropriate protection standard.

In coastal markets (Chennai, Mumbai, Kochi, Visakhapatnam), salt-laden humidity is an additional stressor. In Rajasthan, Gujarat, and the Deccan, fine dust ingress is the primary concern. IP66 addresses both.

**Deye - Score: 8/10**

Most Deye residential inverter models carry **IP65 weather protection** - protection against dust and low-pressure water jets. IP65 is adequate for most sheltered rooftop installations, and the difference between IP65 and IP66 is not meaningful for buyers in a Tier-1 city with sheltered inverter placement. For buyers in exposed rooftop positions, coastal environments, or areas with extreme dust, the IP66 advantage of Qbits is real.

## Axis 4 - India Grid-Tuned Firmware

**Qbits - Score: 10/10**

Qbits inverters are designed specifically for Indian grid conditions. The firmware is tuned for the **180–270 V voltage band** that is common across Indian DISCOMs. India's [Central Electricity Authority grid standards](https://cea.nic.in/) specify the allowable voltage and frequency variation for grid-connected equipment - standards that India-tuned firmware must accommodate to maintain certification. - particularly in rural Tier-2/3 feeders where grid quality is inconsistent. Standard European-spec firmware is typically calibrated for 220–240 V and trips or clips generation when voltage drops below 195 V or rises above 250 V.

India-grid-tuned firmware means fewer nuisance trips, higher annual yield, and lower service event frequency for buyers in grid-variable markets. Read [the India grid-tuned inverters guide](/blog/india-grid-tuned-inverters/) for the full technical explanation of why this matters.

**Deye - Score: 7/10**

Deye has India-specific firmware variants for its current product line, and the brand has invested in ALMM compliance which requires testing against Indian grid standards. However, Deye's India firmware tuning is less specifically documented than Qbits's, and the brand's shorter India market presence (3–4 years) means less India-specific field data has been fed back into firmware refinement.
