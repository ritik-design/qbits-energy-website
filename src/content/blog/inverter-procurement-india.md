For solar EPCs in India, inverter procurement decisions directly impact margins, timelines, and client relationships. With India's solar capacity additions exceeding **20 GW annually**, procurement teams face unprecedented pressure to source reliably while managing costs, lead times, and quality.

## Why Strategic Approach Matters

India's solar market presents unique challenges:

- Extreme weather across regions
- Diverse grid infrastructure
- Complex GST and import duties
- Evolving regulations

Mid-sized EPCs handling 50–100 projects monthly need repeatable processes to avoid cascading failures from delayed shipments or weak vendor support.

## Market Landscape

| Source | Lead time | Support | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic | 2–4 weeks | India-based, fast | Standard |
| Imported | 8–16 weeks | Delayed, often outsourced | Higher landed cost |

### Inverter Categories

- On-grid string
- Commercial / industrial
- Hybrid (with battery integration)

## Essential Technical Specs

- **Efficiency:** 98%+ target
- **IP66** weather protection minimum
- **Panel compatibility** for 600–750W+ modules
- **DC oversizing** up to 100%
- **AI-powered monitoring** with WhatsApp alerts
- **Warranty:** 12-year full replacement over 5-year terms

## Evaluation Criteria

Request detailed datasheets comparing:

- Efficiency curves across load range
- MPPT voltage ranges
- Grid certifications (BIS/IEC)
- Manufacturing QA processes (1,000+ tests per unit)
- After-sales infrastructure (service centres, response time)

## Common Procurement Mistakes

1. **Price-only optimisation** — ignores total cost of ownership
2. **Ignoring BIS certification** — legal and DISCOM exposure
3. **Underestimating import lead times** — schedule slips
4. **Neglecting after-sales support evaluation** — service nightmares post-install
5. **Failing to standardise across projects** — service complexity multiplies
6. **Skipping incoming inspection** — defects propagate to client sites
7. **Informal supplier relationships** — no contractual SLA when things go wrong

## Bottom Line

Inverter procurement is one of the most leveraged decisions in an EPC's business. Build standard evaluation processes, prioritise long-term partnerships with quality manufacturers, and treat warranty terms and service network as core procurement criteria — not negotiable extras.
