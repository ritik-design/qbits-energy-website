## 1. Brand Positioning Statement

Qbits Energy is the **engineering-first Indian solar inverter brand** built for installers and prosumers who are tired of choosing between cheap-but-unreliable local assemblers and expensive global OEMs (Sungrow, Solis, Growatt, Fronius). Where Tier-1 imports compete on legacy and local brands compete on price, Qbits competes on **transparent specifications, India-grid-tuned firmware, and installer-grade after-sales response times** — making it the credible "Made-for-India, Built-to-Spec" choice for the 1kW–50kW residential and C&I segment.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone — 5 Attributes

**1. Trustworthy & Evidence-Led**
- DO: "Tested to IEC 61727 at 45°C ambient — full nameplate output sustained."
- DON'T: "World's most powerful inverter!"

**2. Technical-yet-Accessible**
- DO: "MPPT efficiency 99.5% — meaning almost no sunlight is wasted between panel and battery."
- DON'T: "Advanced patented topology with proprietary algorithms." (jargon without payoff)

**3. Installer-First**
- DO: "Quick-mount bracket, 4-wire commissioning, app pairing in under 90 seconds."
- DON'T: Speak only to end-consumers and ignore the EPC who actually specs the brand.

**4. Confidently Indian**
- DO: "Built for 230V ±20% grids and 50°C rooftops."
- DON'T: Mimic German/Chinese brand voice with stock imagery of Alpine homes.

**5. Plain-Spoken & Numbers-Backed**
- DO: "₹/W landed cost, 7-year payback at ₹7.5/unit tariff."
- DON'T: "Empowering a sustainable tomorrow through clean energy synergies."

## 7. Three Immediate Quick Wins (next 7 days)

1. **Ship product datasheet PDFs + structured-data product schema** on every SKU page. Unlocks Google rich results and gives EPCs something to forward — single highest-ROI change for both SEO and B2B conversions.
2. **Publish 3 comparison blog posts**: "Qbits vs Growatt 5kW", "Qbits vs Microtek Hybrid", "Best On-Grid Inverter under ₹50,000". These target high-intent bottom-of-funnel queries competitors are already ranking for.
3. **Add a sticky "Get a Quote / Find a Dealer" CTA + WhatsApp Business chat** on every page. Indian solar buyers convert on WhatsApp far more than web forms — current site likely leaks 60%+ of warm intent.
