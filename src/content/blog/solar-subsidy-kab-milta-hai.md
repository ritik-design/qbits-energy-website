Solar lagwane wale har ghar ka sabse bada sawaal price nahi hota, timing hota hai। "Paisa kab wapas aayega" yeh question har quote discussion mein aata hai, aur yahi wo jagah hai jahan sabse zyada galat expectations set hoti hain। Kuch vendors bolte hain "do mahine mein subsidy aa jayegi", kuch bolte hain "installation ke saath hi adjust ho jayegi"। Dono statements aam taur par galat hain। Is guide mein poora process stage-by-stage tod kar rakha gaya hai, har stage ke realistic din ke saath, aur yeh bhi ki kis stage par sabse zyada delay hota hai। Agar aapko process ke steps aur eligibility samajhni hai to uske liye alag companion guide hai; yeh page sirf timeline par focus karta hai।

> **TL;DR**
> - Subsidy DBT ke through consumer ke bank account mein aati hai, aur sirf [DISCOM](/glossary/discom/) commissioning ke baad, installation ke pehle ya beech mein nahi।
> - Registration se paisa aane tak realistic total 3 se 6 mahine, best case 75 se 90 din।
> - Central subsidy pehle 2 kW par ₹30,000 per kW, teesre kW par ₹18,000, 3 kW band par cap ₹78,000।
> - Sabse lamba wait typically DISCOM feasibility approval aur net meter installation par hota hai, installation par nahi।
> - Claim submit hone ke baad DBT credit typically 30 se 60 din, agar bank details first attempt mein sahi hon।
> - Har number ek range hai, official SLA nahi। State DISCOM aur season se timeline badalti hai।

**Chhota jawab.** Solar ka subsidy DISCOM commissioning certificate milne ke baad milta hai, us se pehle kabhi nahi। [Commissioning](/glossary/commissioning/) ke baad aap portal par bank details ke saath subsidy claim submit karte hain, aur typically 30 se 60 din mein paisa Direct Benefit Transfer se aapke account mein aata hai। Registration se DBT tak poora elapsed time aam taur par 3 se 6 mahine rehta hai।

Yeh baat pehle hi clear kar lena zaroori hai kyunki isse aapka cash flow plan badalta hai। Aapko system ka poora paisa pehle dena hai, subsidy baad mein refund ki tarah aati hai। Process ke steps, documents aur eligibility ke liye [solar subsidy kaise milegi ka step-by-step guide](/blog/solar-subsidy-kaise-milegi-hindi/) padhein, aur scheme ke rules aur amounts ke liye [PM Surya Ghar Yojana ki complete guide](/blog/pm-surya-ghar-yojana-complete-guide/) dekhein।

## Poori Timeline Ek Table Mein

Yeh centrepiece table hai। Har stage ka typical din range, kaun act karta hai, aur wahan sabse common delay ka kaaran।

| Stage | Typical Days | Kaun Act Karta Hai | Common Delay Ka Kaaran |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National portal registration | 1 se 3 din | Consumer | Consumer number aur bill name mismatch, OTP issues |
| DISCOM feasibility approval | 7 se 30 din | DISCOM | Sanctioned load kam, transformer capacity, field visit backlog |
| Vendor selection aur agreement | 3 se 15 din | Consumer aur vendor | Quote comparison, ALMM verification, loan sanction wait |
| Installation | 2 se 7 din | Vendor | Material shortage, roof civil work, monsoon |
| Installation details portal par submit | 1 se 3 din | Vendor aur consumer | Photos, invoice aur serial numbers adhoore |
| Net meter application aur fee | 3 se 10 din | Consumer aur vendor | Fee payment receipt upload na hona |
| Net meter installation | 10 se 45 din | DISCOM | Bidirectional meter stock, field team availability |
| Inspection aur testing | 5 se 20 din | DISCOM | Inspector slot, earthing ya safety observation |
| Commissioning certificate issue | 3 se 15 din | DISCOM | Report ka portal par upload hone mein lag |
| Subsidy claim submission (bank details) | 1 se 3 din | Consumer | Cancelled cheque quality, IFSC ya name mismatch |
| DBT credit account mein | 30 se 60 din | Portal aur bank | Aadhaar seeding, account inactive, claim scrutiny |

Yeh saare numbers ranges hain, guarantees nahi। Har state ka DISCOM alag speed se chalta hai aur season ka bada asar padta hai। Gujarat aur Kerala jaise states ke kai divisions historically tez rahe hain, jabki high-volume urban divisions mein backlog lamba hota hai। Monsoon ke mahino mein field visits aur roof work dono slow ho jaate hain।

## Stage 1: National Portal Registration (1 se 3 Din)

Yeh sabse tez stage hai aur poori tarah aapke haath mein hai। pmsuryaghar.gov.in par state, DISCOM, consumer number aur mobile number daal kar account banta hai, phir latest electricity bill ke details verify hote hain।

Delay yahan sirf ek wajah se hota hai: bill par jo naam aur consumer number hai woh portal entry se match nahi karta। Purane ghar, inherited property, ya joint family connections mein yeh common hai। Agar bill kisi aur ke naam par hai to pehle DISCOM se name transfer karwana padega, aur woh apne aap 15 se 45 din ka alag process hai jo is timeline ke bahar hai।

Ek practical tip: registration ke waqt jo mobile number aur email dein, wahi aage tak use karein।

## Stage 2: DISCOM Feasibility Approval (7 se 30 Din)

Registration ke baad application aapke DISCOM ke paas technical feasibility ke liye jaati hai। DISCOM check karta hai ki aapka sanctioned load proposed solar capacity ke liye sufficient hai, aur local distribution transformer par aur solar load lene ki gunjaish hai।

Typically yeh 7 se 30 din leta hai। Approval milne par portal par status "Feasibility Approved" ho jaata hai aur tabhi aap vendor ke saath aage badh sakte hain।

Sabse aam rejection ka kaaran sanctioned load kam hona hai। Agar aapka sanctioned load 2 kW hai aur aap 3 kW system chahte hain, to pehle load enhancement application deni padegi। Yeh alag se 10 se 30 din add karta hai aur uski fees bhi alag hai। Isliye quote lene se pehle hi apne bill par sanctioned load dekh lein।

Doosra kaaran transformer saturation hai। Jis feeder par pehle se bahut saare rooftop systems hain, wahan DISCOM naya connection defer kar sakta hai jab tak infrastructure upgrade na ho। Yeh consumer ke control mein bilkul nahi hota।

## Stage 3: Vendor Selection Aur Installation (5 se 22 Din)

Feasibility approval ke baad aap portal par listed registered vendor choose karte hain। Vendor selection aur agreement mein aam taur par 3 se 15 din lagte hain, kyunki log 3 quotes compare karte hain aur kai baar loan sanction ka wait karte hain।

Actual installation surprisingly tez hai। Ek 3 kW residential rooftop typically 2 se 3 din mein lag jaata hai। 5 kW se bada system ya jahan structure ki height zyada honi hai, wahan 5 se 7 din tak jaata hai।

Delay ke asli kaaran installation ke andar teen hote hain। Pehla, material availability, khaas kar specific inverter model ya mounting structure ka stock। Doosra, roof par civil work, jaise waterproofing ya parapet strengthening, jo quote mein include nahi tha। Teesra, monsoon, jismein safe roof work ke din hi kam mil paate hain।

Installation ke baad vendor ko portal par installation details submit karni hoti hai: panel aur inverter ke serial numbers, invoice, aur site photos। Yeh 1 se 3 din ka kaam hai lekin adhoore documents se yahan hafte nikal jaate hain। Submission ke baad screenshot maang lein।

## Stage 4: Net Meter Installation (10 se 45 Din)

Yeh timeline ka sabse unpredictable hissa hai।

Net meter application vendor ya consumer DISCOM ke paas file karta hai, fees ke saath। Fee state ke hisaab se ₹1,000 se ₹5,000 ke beech hoti hai aur security deposit alag ho sakta hai। Application se meter lagne tak typically 10 se 45 din lagte hain।

Wajah simple hai: bidirectional meter DISCOM ka stock item hai। Kai divisions mein monthly allotment aata hai, aur agar aapki application allotment cycle ke baad aayi to aapko agle cycle ka wait karna padta hai। Isme vendor kuch nahi kar sakta।

> **Jo log yahan galti karte hain.** Log samajhte hain ki panels lag gaye to system chalu hai aur subsidy clock start ho gaya। Aisa nahi hai। Net meter aur commissioning ke bina system officially grid-connected nahi hai, aur subsidy claim ka darwaza khulta hi nahi।

Ek practical baat: net meter lagne se pehle system ko grid par mat chalayein। Kai DISCOMs isko violation maante hain aur inspection mein observation daal dete hain, jisse aur delay hota hai।

## Stage 5: Inspection Aur Commissioning Certificate (8 se 35 Din)

Meter lagne ke baad DISCOM ka technical officer site inspection karta hai। Woh earthing, AC aur DC isolation, inverter ki ALMM listing, safety labels aur meter connections check karta hai। Inspection slot milne mein 5 se 20 din lag sakte hain।

Inspection pass hone ke baad commissioning certificate issue hota hai, jo aam taur par 3 se 15 din leta hai kyunki report ko portal par upload hona hota hai।

Sabse common observations do hain: earthing resistance requirement se zyada, aur AC side protection devices ki rating drawing se match na karna। Dono cases mein vendor ko rectify karke re-inspection maangna padta hai, jo 7 se 21 din add karta hai।

Commissioning certificate hi wo document hai jiske baad subsidy claim active hota hai। Iski soft copy apne paas rakhein, sirf vendor ke paas nahi।

## Stage 6: Subsidy Claim Aur DBT Credit (31 se 63 Din)

Commissioning ke baad aap portal par subsidy claim submit karte hain। Isme bank account number, IFSC code aur cancelled cheque ya passbook ka first page upload karna hota hai। Yeh form bharne mein 1 se 3 din lagte hain।

Uske baad DBT credit typically 30 se 60 din leta hai।

Yahan teen cheezein sabse zyada claims atkati hain। Pehli, bank account ka naam electricity connection holder ke naam se match nahi karta। Doosri, cancelled cheque ki photo blurry hai ya IFSC padha nahi ja raha। Teesri, account inactive ya dormant hai, ya Aadhaar seeding pending hai।

Amount ki clarity bhi rakhein। Central subsidy pehle 2 kW ke liye ₹30,000 per kW hai, teesre kW par ₹18,000 additional, aur 3 kW band par total cap ₹78,000। 5 kW system lagane par bhi central subsidy ₹78,000 hi milegi।

| System Size | Central Subsidy Calculation | Total Central Subsidy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 kW | 1 × ₹30,000 | ₹30,000 |
| 2 kW | 2 × ₹30,000 | ₹60,000 |
| 3 kW | ₹60,000 + ₹18,000 | ₹78,000 |
| 5 kW | Cap applies | ₹78,000 |
| 10 kW | Cap applies | ₹78,000 |

Kuch states apni state subsidy alag se dete hain, jo alag timeline aur alag portal par chalti hai। State subsidy ka DBT central subsidy ke saath ek hi din nahi aata, aksar 1 se 3 mahine baad aata hai।

## Status Kaise Track Karein

Do jagah parallel track karein, kyunki dono systems hamesha sync nahi hote।

1. **National portal.** pmsuryaghar.gov.in par registered mobile se login karein aur application dashboard dekhein। Har stage ka status wahan visible hota hai।
2. **DISCOM consumer portal.** Apne net meter application number se DISCOM ki site par alag status check karein। Meter aur inspection ka actual movement aksar yahan pehle dikhta hai।
3. **SMS trail sambhal kar rakhein.** Har stage ka SMS timestamp ke saath aata hai। Escalation ke waqt yeh sabse useful evidence hota hai।

Ek habit bana lein: har 7 din mein ek baar dono portals check karein aur screenshot save karein date ke saath। Agar baad mein complaint karni padi to aapke paas exact timeline proof hoga।

## Kya Karein Agar Atak Jaye

Har stage ke liye alag action hai। Blanket "vendor ko phone karo" kaam nahi karta, kyunki aadhe stages vendor ke control mein hi nahi hain।

| Kahan Ruka Hai | 30 Din Baad Kya Karein |
| --- | --- |
| Feasibility approval | DISCOM ke sub-division office jaakar application number ke saath written follow-up dein। Sanctioned load issue ho to load enhancement parallel file karein। |
| Vendor installation | Agreement ki timeline clause quote karke likhit reminder bhejein। Vendor badalna ho to portal par vendor change ka option check karein। |
| Net meter | DISCOM ke nodal solar officer ko email karein aur meter stock status poochein। Fee receipt attach karein। |
| Inspection | Sub-division office se inspection slot ki date likhit maangein। Observation aaya ho to rectification report jaldi upload karwayein। |
| Commissioning certificate | Portal par upload pending hai ya physical issue pending, yeh clarify karwayein। Dono alag departments hote hain। |
| DBT credit | Bank details re-verify karein, naam aur IFSC match karein। 60 din se upar ho to helpline 15555 par complaint number lein। |

Escalation ka sahi order yeh hai: pehle vendor, phir DISCOM sub-division, phir DISCOM nodal solar officer, phir National Portal helpline 15555, phir state ka electricity ombudsman। Har step par complaint number lein aur email trail banayein। Sirf phone call ka koi record nahi bachta।

Ek aur cheez jo log miss karte hain: agar aapka vendor gayab ho gaya hai ya kaam adhoora chhod gaya hai, to apne area ke doosre certified partner se site rectification karwana zyada tez padta hai bajaye mahino intezaar karne ke।

## Timeline Ko Tez Karne Ke Practical Tarike

Poora control kisi ka nahi hota, lekin kuch cheezein genuinely fark daalti hain।

1. **Sanctioned load pehle check karein.** Bill par load dekh kar hi system size decide karein। Yeh akela step 15 se 30 din bacha sakta hai।
2. **Bank details registration ke waqt hi ready rakhein.** Connection holder ke naam wala active account, clear cancelled cheque scan, aur Aadhaar seeded।
3. **Net meter application installation ke turant baad file karein.** Ek din ka bhi gap na dein, kyunki yahan queue position matter karti hai।
4. **Documents har stage par khud verify karein.** Vendor ke upload ka screenshot maangein, blindly bharosa na karein।
5. **Monsoon peak avoid karein agar flexibility hai.** Field visits aur roof work dono slow hote hain us dauran।
6. **ALMM listed equipment hi lagwayein.** Non-listed equipment inspection par reject hota hai aur poora timeline reset ho jaata hai।

Ek nuance: tez hone ke chakkar mein cheap non-listed inverter mat lagwa lena। Woh 30 din bacha kar 90 din ka rework de sakta hai, aur worst case subsidy hi cancel ho sakti hai।
