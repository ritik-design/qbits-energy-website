## Hidden Costs to Watch For

1. **AI Mode add-on**, priced separately and not published. Ask for it in writing if you want the modern [AI solar design](/blog/ai-solar-design-software/) workflow.
2. **Sales Mode add-on**. Needed for native proposal output. Otherwise you bolt on [Solargraf](/blog/solargraf-alternative/) or a separate tool.
3. **Training or onboarding fees** for new teams. Ask whether any apply.
4. **CRM integration** work. Ask whether it is included.
5. **Multi-language proposals** are limited; cross-border teams often need a [separate proposal tool](/blog/solar-proposal-software/).
6. **8,760-hour shading** is tier-gated. Lower tiers ship a simplified shading model, so confirm which simulation tier your lender will accept before you pick a plan.
