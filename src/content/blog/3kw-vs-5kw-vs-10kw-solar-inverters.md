Choosing the right inverter capacity is critical for solar system design. Capacity (measured in kilowatts) determines how much DC power converts to usable AC electricity and influences performance, costs, and ROI.

## 3 kW Inverter Capacity

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Small apartments, 2–3 BHK homes with modest consumption |
| Daily generation | 12–15 kWh |
| Panel configuration | 8–10 panels = 3.5–4.5 kW DC array |
| Price range | ₹18,000–35,000 |
| ROI timeline | 4–5 years (high-tariff states) |

## 5 kW Inverter Capacity

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | 3–4 BHK homes (45–50% of residential market) |
| Daily generation | 20–25 kWh |
| Panel configuration | 12–16 panels = 6–7.5 kW DC array |
| Price range | ₹28,000–65,000 |
| Advantages | **Optimal cost-per-watt economics**, single-phase compatible |

The dominant market tier, best balance of capacity, cost, and complexity.

## 10 kW Inverter Capacity

| Spec | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Best for | Large villas, small commercial establishments |
| Daily generation | 40–50 kWh |
| Panel configuration | 12–24 high-wattage panels = 12–15 kW DC |
| Price range | ₹50,000–1,20,000 |
| ROI timeline | 3.5–4.5 years (commercial applications) |

## Performance Comparison

### Efficiency

- **Premium inverters:** 96–98% across load range
- **Budget models:** drop to 92–94%
- **Difference:** 150–200 extra annual units generated by premium

### Temperature Derating

- Quality inverters maintain full capacity to **45°C ambient**
- Budget units often derate significantly at **40°C**

## Common Capacity Selection Mistakes

1. **Undersizing to minimise costs**: saving ₹20,000 loses ₹2,00,000 over 25 years
2. **Overlooking DC oversizing** (supports 130–200% panel capacity)
3. **Ignoring future load growth** (EV charging, expansion)
4. **Choosing based solely on price**: TCO matters more

## Modern Panel Impact

High-wattage **650–750W panels** require inverters with:

- Wider voltage windows
- Robust MPPT capabilities
- Strong DC oversizing support

Modern units supporting aggressive oversizing can **increase total daily yield by 8–12%** in many Indian locations.

## Quick Sizing Decision

| Property | Inverter |
| --- | --- |
| 1 BHK / small apartment | 3 kW |
| 2–3 BHK home | 3–5 kW |
| 3–4 BHK home (most common) | **5 kW** |
| 4+ BHK villa | 5–10 kW |
| Small shop / office | 5–10 kW |
| Large villa / small commercial | 10 kW |

## Closing

The 3 kW / 5 kW / 10 kW choice is about matching consumption patterns to inverter capacity with appropriate headroom for future load growth and DC oversizing flexibility. The 5 kW tier wins in most residential applications by combining capacity, cost, and single-phase simplicity, but always run actual consumption analysis before defaulting to the popular choice.
