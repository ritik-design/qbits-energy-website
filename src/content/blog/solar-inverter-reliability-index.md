Evaluating inverter reliability evidence cannot be reduced to a brand score or a universal recovery deadline. Reliability depends on the exact model, design, installation, grid, environment, maintenance, firmware, and how a “failure” is defined and counted. Comparable field data is not retained for the brands discussed on this site.

> **Safety first:** Solar PV conductors can remain energised in daylight, and inverter internals can contain hazardous voltage. Do not open the enclosure or improvise electrical tests. Follow the exact manual and use a qualified technician.

## Capture evidence before changing the system

Record the model and serial number, time, display or LED state, fault code exactly as shown, monitoring screenshot, grid status, recent weather or electrical work, and any unusual sound, smell, heat, or visible damage. Do not repeatedly reset a fault or close a tripped protective device without identifying the cause.

## Separate external conditions from equipment faults

A stopped inverter may be responding to a grid outage, grid voltage or frequency outside its permitted window, an open isolator, a protection-device trip, insufficient irradiance, a communication failure, a DC-side fault, overheating, or an internal condition. Meanings and reset procedures vary by model. Use the model manual rather than a generic fault-code table.

## Reliability evidence worth requesting

| Evidence | Minimum context needed |
| --- | --- |
| Failure rate | Population, models, installation dates, operating exposure and failure definition |
| Return rate | Sales denominator, observation period, exclusions and repeat returns |
| Downtime | Start and end rules, parts delay, installer delay and geography |
| Environmental tests | Standard, severity, laboratory, report and exact model coverage |
| Warranty data | Claim denominator, approval rules, remedy and unresolved claims |
| Service performance | Ticket population, percentile, geography and contractual target |

Without this context, labels such as “most reliable,” “lowest RMA,” or “fastest service” are marketing claims, not comparable evidence.

## Qbits document limit

The retained Qbits datasheets provide model-specific specifications and describe an expandable warranty. They do not provide audited field failure rates, a universal service deadline, or complete warranty terms. Obtain the current manual, warranty, service process, and relevant certificates for the exact model.

## Safe escalation path

Contact the installer and the manufacturer or warranty provider using the channels stated in the project and warranty documents. Obtain a ticket number and written diagnosis. If there is burning smell, smoke, water ingress, damaged cabling, repeated protective-device operation, or a suspected insulation or arc fault, keep people away and have a qualified professional make the site safe.
