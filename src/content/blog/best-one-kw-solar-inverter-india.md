The **best 1 kW solar inverter in India is not a brand-wide winner**. It is the exact on-grid model whose voltage, current, string design, documents, and local support fit your installation.

The current India-facing evidence supports two comparable products: **Havells Enviro GTi 1.1KT-G3** and **Microtek Solar Grid Tie 1KW-M11**. Both publish 1,100 W rated output, even though buyers commonly search for a 1 kW inverter. Microtek publishes slightly higher maximum and European efficiency plus a complete maximum-PV-input figure. Havells publishes higher input-current limits. Neither public source reviewed here settles complete warranty terms or current exact-model certificate validity.

Qbits publishes this article and sells competing solar inverters. Qbits is not ranked because its current range starts at 1.5 kW. We did not laboratory-test either shortlisted product, inspect installations, collect failure data, or verify local prices, stock, service outcomes, warranty remedies, certificate validity, or DISCOM acceptance.

This is a **design-fit comparison**, not a reliability ranking. A 0.2 percentage-point efficiency difference does not establish better annual generation, and a larger input-current figure does not make an incompatible string safe.

## Why a "1 kW" product can publish 1,100 W

The search phrase and the technical rating do not line up neatly. Havells calls its model **1.1KT-G3**. Microtek calls its product **1KW-M11**, but the technical table identifies **GTI 1KW-M11** and publishes 1,100 W rated output. Both sources state 1,100 VA maximum apparent power.

That makes these two products comparable as the small, single-phase on-grid class buyers mean when they ask for a 1 kW inverter. It does not make 1,000 W and 1,100 W interchangeable in a design. Put the full model code and the 1,100 W technical rating on the quotation, string calculation, approval file, and handover record.

## How we selected and compared the models

A credible best list needs rules that can exclude products. We used five:

1. **Same operating class.** The product must be a single-phase, on-grid string inverter with rated AC output between 1.0 and 1.1 kW.
2. **Current primary evidence.** A current India-facing manufacturer page or PDF must identify the exact model.
3. **Enough exact-model detail.** The source must publish the electrical fields needed for a meaningful comparison.
4. **Same dimensions.** We compare output, voltage, current, MPPT design, efficiency, enclosure, communication, export control, physical data, and document gaps.
5. **Unknowns stay unknown.** We do not turn a standards list, logo, search snippet, dealer claim, or family-wide promise into exact-model proof.

Hybrid inverters, solar PCUs, home UPS products, microinverters, dealer-only listings, and products without current exact-model manufacturer evidence were excluded. Havells appears first alphabetically. Placement is not based on payment, sales volume, market share, or a private arrangement.

## When Havells is the stronger documented fit

Havells publishes 13 A maximum MPPT current and 17.5 A maximum short-circuit current for Enviro GTi 1.1KT-G3. Those limits are higher than Microtek's published 12 A and 15 A figures.

That difference matters only after the installer applies the module manufacturer's electrical data and the required safety or design factors. It does not mean every higher-current module is compatible. The planned string must remain within the inverter's operating-current and short-circuit-current limits under the conditions used for design approval.

Havells also publishes a 50 to 500 V MPPT range, 70 V starting voltage, IP65 enclosure, transformerless design, and a feed-in limitation function. The reviewed sheet labels the family BIS and IEC certified and prints registration number R-41165239. Treat that as a manufacturer datasheet statement, not proof that a current certificate covers the exact unit offered to you. Ask for the certificate file and verify its model scope and validity.

The main evidence gaps are equally important. The reviewed exact sheet does not state a maximum PV-input figure or complete warranty terms. Do not borrow the warranty shown for a larger Havells product or calculate a PV ceiling from an unrelated family member.

## When Microtek is the stronger documented fit

Microtek publishes a 1,500 W maximum PV input for GTI 1KW-M11. It also states a 110 to 450 V full-load DC range, alongside the broader 50 to 500 V MPPT range. Those fields give an installer more public information for checking a proposed array than the reviewed Havells sheet provides.

Microtek's technical table publishes 97.5% maximum efficiency and 96.9% European efficiency, compared with Havells at 97.3% and 96.7%. The difference is 0.2 percentage points in each named row. That is too narrow to support an annual-yield verdict without a site model, operating distribution, temperature data, losses, availability, and independent performance evidence.

The Microtek page also states optional zero export or adjustable export limiting and lists several communication routes. Confirm which logger, meter, current transformer, cables, software access, and commissioning work are included in the quoted package. An optional function is not an installed system.

One source-quality warning is visible on the page. Its promotional copy says "Max 07.2% Efficiency," while the technical table states 97.5%. We use the model-specific technical table because the promotional line is visibly malformed. A buyer should still request the current signed datasheet and make the supplier confirm the applicable value in writing.

## Check the string before choosing either model

Both inverters have one MPPT and one PV input or string. That keeps the architecture simple, but it leaves little room for mismatched roof planes. Do not combine differently oriented or differently shaded module groups merely to reach a convenient wattage.

Ask the installer to show these checks using the exact module datasheet:

1. **Cold open-circuit voltage.** Temperature-corrected string Voc must remain below 500 V.
2. **Operating voltage.** Expected string voltage should remain inside the useful operating window, including hot-weather effects.
3. **Starting behaviour.** The planned string must reach the published 70 V start threshold under realistic conditions.
4. **Operating current.** The string current must fit 13 A for Havells or 12 A for Microtek.
5. **Short-circuit current.** Corrected string Isc must fit 17.5 A for Havells or 15 A for Microtek.
6. **PV input ceiling.** Microtek publishes 1,500 W. Havells does not state an equivalent value in the reviewed sheet, so obtain it from the manufacturer before approving oversizing.

Our [solar inverter sizing guide](/blog/solar-inverter-sizing/) explains why the AC label is only one part of this calculation. If the installer cannot show the arithmetic, neither model is ready to buy.

## These on-grid inverters are not backup products

Havells Enviro GTi 1.1KT-G3 and Microtek GTI 1KW-M11 are on-grid inverters. Do not assume either will run lights, fans, a refrigerator, or other household loads during a power cut.

An on-grid inverter normally stops energising the circuit when the utility supply is absent. If backup is required, start again with the outage loads, required runtime, surge demand, compatible hybrid inverter, battery voltage and usable capacity, transfer behaviour, and protected-load wiring. A [broader inverter comparison by use case](/blog/best-solar-inverter-india/) can help separate grid-export and backup requirements before choosing a model.
