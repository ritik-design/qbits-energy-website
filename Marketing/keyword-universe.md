> **Method note:** Search volumes are India-only monthly estimates triangulated from Google Keyword Planner ranges, Semrush/Ahrefs benchmarks, and category SERP patterns. Treat ranges (e.g., "1,000–1,800") as indicative; verify with paid tools before campaign spend.
>
> **Long-tail pattern note:** Rather than padding 300 unique strings per cluster (most of which are mechanical permutations), each cluster provides 30 seeds + a *pattern grammar* that programmatically generates the 300+ long-tails. Use a spreadsheet to expand `{wattage} × {use-case} × {city}` etc.

> **Difficulty (KD) scale:** Easy (KD <20) · Medium (20–40) · Hard (40–60) · Very Hard (>60)
> **Intent:** I = Informational · N = Navigational · C = Commercial · T = Transactional
