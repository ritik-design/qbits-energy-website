**Solar dealer marketing starts with two listings done properly: a verified Google Business Profile set up as a shop (not as an installer), and a WhatsApp Business catalogue of product families with the facts a buyer asks for first.** Both have written rules. Four mistakes hurt dealers most. They are a profile name stuffed with brand and product words, the word "authorised" with no written appointment, reviews bought with discounts, and messages to people who never agreed to hear from you.

This guide is for a dealer, meaning a shop or trading firm that sells inverters, panels and balance-of-system items ([how a dealer differs from a distributor or EPC firm](/blog/dealer-vs-distributor-vs-epc/)). It covers the hands-on setup of those two tools only. The wider funnel (referrals, residents' welfare associations, partners, ads) and the consent rules of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act are in [how solar installers get customers](/blog/how-solar-installers-get-customers/), and this guide does not repeat them.

Every limit and rule below was read on the page named beside it on **30 October 2026** and opened again on **31 October 2026**. The wording of each rule we quote was the same on both days. Where a page did not open, or a limit was not stated on it, the guide says so instead of quoting from memory. Help pages change without notice, so re-check a limit before you rely on it.

> **Short version**
> - Set the profile up as a storefront if customers can walk in and a signboard shows your name. List a service area only for places you really visit or deliver to.
> - Choose the fewest categories that describe what the business is. A dealer and an installer sit under different categories in the list we could open.
> - Do not write "authorised dealer" or "official partner" for any brand unless you hold a written appointment for it. Google's own naming rule and the Code of the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI) both point the same way.
> - Never give a discount or gift for a review, and ask every customer, not only the happy ones.
> - In the WhatsApp catalogue, group by product family and capacity class. Give each item model, rating, phase and IP rating (the dust and water protection rating). Leave the price off unless it carries a date.

## Dealer, installer or both: decide what the profile is

Google asks you to describe what the business is, not everything it does. Its category help says to select categories that complete the statement "This business IS a", not "this business HAS a".

For a dealer this means the shop comes first. If you also survey and install, that is a second activity you add only if it is a real part of the business.

| Your set-up | Google's term | Address shown on the profile | What the page says |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shop with a signboard where customers walk in | Storefront | Yes | Businesses that show an address "should maintain permanent fixed signage of their business name at the address". |
| Shop that also sends a team to survey or deliver | Hybrid | Address and service area | The location "should be staffed by your team and able to receive customers during its stated hours". |
| Home office or godown where you do not receive customers | Service-area business | Hidden | A service-area business "should hide your business address from customers". It gets one profile for the whole area. |
| A rented mailing address or virtual office | Not eligible | Not applicable | A rented address where you do not operate (a virtual office) "isn't eligible for a Business Profile". |

Sources: [Guidelines for representing your business on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) and [Manage your service areas](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481), read 30 October 2026.

One more point from the same guidelines matters for small firms. There should be only one profile per business, and a second profile for the same shop can cause problems on Maps and Search. If your shop already shows up on Google because someone else added it, claim that listing instead of creating a new one.

## Solar dealer marketing on Google: set up the Business Profile

### Name: only what the signboard says

Google's rule is that your name "should reflect your business's real-world name, as used consistently on your storefront, website, stationery, and as known to customers". The guidelines list what a name must not carry. Four of them matter to a solar dealer:

- Marketing taglines.
- Business hours.
- Phone numbers or website addresses.
- "Service or product information". Google's own example of a name that is not acceptable is "Verizon Wireless 4G LTE", where only "Verizon Wireless" is.

So "Shree Ram Electricals" is a fine name. "Shree Ram Solar Inverter Panel Dealer Surat Best Price" is the kind of name that can get a profile suspended. It adds product information, a location and a promotional phrase. If your signboard carries a tagline or the word "authorised", the profile name still leaves it out. The next section says why you should also look hard at the signboard.

### The brands you sell: the one sentence to follow

The same guidelines have a rule for businesses that sell other companies' products, and it reads: "If your business sells another business brand's product(s) or service(s), use only the name of the business, excluding the name of the brand being sold, which can't have a Business Profile for this location."

There is one exception, and it is narrow: "if the business is an authorized and fully dedicated seller of the branded product or service, sometimes known as a 'franchisee', you may use the underlying brand name". A multi-brand dealer is not that. Put the brand names you stock in the description and in photos of the stock, and keep the profile name as your own.

This is also where the authorisation question gets decided. The ASCI Code (Chapter I, which we read on [ascionline.in](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code/) on 30 October 2026) says advertisements "shall neither distort facts nor mislead the consumer by means of implications or omissions". It also says an advertisement shall not, without permission, refer to a firm in a way that "confers an unjustified advantage on the product advertised".

ASCI defines an advertisement as a paid-for communication, so whether an unpaid listing or catalogue falls inside the Code is not something we can settle for you. We use it as the honesty standard. We also did not read the Trade Marks Act or any brand's dealer terms, so this is the practical rule, not legal advice:

| You can say | You should not say without a written appointment |
| --- | --- |
| "We stock inverters from [brand], supplied by their distributor." (only if true and invoiced) | "Authorised dealer", "official partner", "authorised service centre" |
| "We supply and can quote [brand] models." | Brand logos used as if the brand endorses you |
| "Datasheets available on request." | "Direct from the brand" when you buy from a distributor |

If a brand does appoint you, keep the appointment letter, use the exact wording the letter allows, and check what it says about logos. [The dealer agreement checklist](/blog/solar-dealer-agreement-checklist/) lists what to look for before you sign.

### Category: pick from a list you can see

Google's help says you select a specific category from the list in the editor and cannot create your own. It also says to "use as few categories as possible". It adds: "Do not select a category for every product or service." You can have a primary category and up to nine more.

We could not open Google's own category list without signing in. It appears in the Business Profile editor, and Google's [Business Information API reference](https://developers.google.com/my-business/reference/businessinformation/rest/v1/categories/list) describes a categories method that needs a signed-in Google authorisation. So we used a third-party article dated 17 June 2026, whose list is marked "Updated on: February 11, 2026", to see the names. Both days it showed the same category names. Treat it as a guide and confirm each name in your own editor.

| Business | Category names seen in the third-party list | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dealer shop selling inverters and panels | Solar energy equipment supplier | Primary |
| Dealer that also installs | Solar energy system service | Add only if installation is a real activity |
| Dealer that does service visits on installed systems | Solar panel maintenance service | Add only if you do this work |
| Dealer that also sells cables, switchgear and boxes | Electrical equipment supplier, Electrical supply store | Add only if the shop looks like one |
| Dealer with a wholesale counter | Electrical products wholesaler | Add only if you sell to the trade |

Two points from that list. It had no category with "inverter" in its name, so do not look for one. And it noted that the older "Solar energy contractor" name is now "Solar energy system service", which is the one an installer would pick. A dealer who picks the installer category as primary tells Google, and customers, that the business is something else.

On WhatsApp, the business profile takes up to three categories. The help page says a custom category is not possible, so use a general one or "Other Business" if nothing fits.

### Address, service area and hours

For a hybrid shop, show the storefront address and add a service area. Google lets you set up to 20 service areas, by city, postal code or other area, not as a radius. It says the area should not be more than about 2 hours of driving time from where you are based, and to "be as specific and accurate as possible". A dealer in Surat who delivers to Navsari and Bharuch can list those towns. Listing a whole state you never serve is the opposite of specific.

Set your customer-facing hours, not the hours the owner picks up the phone. The WhatsApp profile has its own hours field, and the WhatsApp away message can be limited to "Outside of business hours" only if those hours are set there.

### Verification

Google decides which verification methods you see, and you cannot change them. The page lists phone or text, email, a live video call, and postcard. For video it says to be at your business and ready to show outside signs, your products or branded equipment, and proof you manage the place. For a dealer, that means standing at the shop with the signboard and the inverter display in view.

For postcard, the page says most codes arrive within 14 days and expire after 30 days. You must not edit the name, address or category while you wait. Review after you submit takes up to 5 business days. It also says: "Google will never ask for your verification code." Anyone who phones you offering to "complete your Google verification" for a code is not following that rule.

### The description

Google limits the description to 750 characters, with no URLs or HTML. The guidelines also say it must not focus on "special promotions, prices, and offer sales", and no links of any type are allowed. Use it for what you sell, where, and what a customer can do next. A worked example is in the templates section below.

### Products and services: what is and is not available

The Products feature that shows your shelf on the profile is, per Google's help page, for retail businesses in the US, Canada, the UK and Ireland. It was not listed for India on the page we read, so your product list cannot live there. The services editor is described as having "limited availability" and says custom service names should avoid prices and phone numbers. If you see it for your category, add plain service names such as "Site survey" or "Inverter replacement" only if you offer them. Put the product list in the WhatsApp catalogue and on your own site.

Google's Q&A feature is also described as "available only for select business categories and regions", so do not plan around it.

### Photos

Google's photo page gives these standards: JPG or PNG, 10 KB to 5 MB, recommended 720 by 720 pixels, minimum 250 by 250. The photo "should be in focus and well lit, and have no significant alterations or excessive use of filters". Videos can run up to 30 seconds and 75 MB at 720p or higher. Photos and videos appear only after verification, and can take 24 to 48 hours to show.

A short shot list for a dealer:

1. The storefront with the signboard readable from the road. Google says an exterior photo helps customers recognise the business.
2. The counter and the display wall, with the inverter model labels readable.
3. Stock on the shelf, shot so brand labels are real and current.
4. Your team, in work clothes, at the shop.
5. Delivered or installed jobs, only with the customer's permission for the photo and the location.

Keep to real photos. A pack-shot copied from a brand's site is not your inventory and may not be yours to use.

### Posts

Posts can be Updates, Offers or Events. Google says posts older than 6 months are archived unless a date range is set, and an Offer needs a title, dates and a time. Posts that put a phone number in the text may be rejected if the number cannot be linked to the business, so use the call button instead. Each post shows a status: Live, Pending or Not approved.

For a dealer, one Update a week is enough: a new stock arrival, a datasheet explainer, a site photo. Avoid price claims in posts, because a price that goes stale is a claim you made.

### Reviews

Two Google pages set the rules. The [contributed content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114) does not allow offering "incentives, such as payment, discounts, free goods and/or services" for a review. It also does not allow discouraging negative reviews or selectively soliciting positive ones. It also bars reviews based on a conflict of interest, which includes current or former employment or a contractual relationship. So staff, family and installers you pay should not review the shop.

What Google's [review tips page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) says you can do: ask customers to visit a Google link or scan a QR code, and reply to reviews.

- Ask every customer after delivery, with the profile link or the QR code printed on the invoice.
- Do not offer a discount, a free accessory or a service credit in return.
- Reply to reviews in a calm tone. Google advises against sending the same "thank you" to everyone and suggests adding a helpful detail or answer. For a complaint, Google suggests you ask the reviewer to contact you by phone or email to settle it privately, and not share the reviewer's private information.
- If a review breaks Google's policies, flag it. Do not argue in public.

## Set up the WhatsApp Business app and catalogue

### What you are using, and what you are not

There are two products under the same name, and the policy page separates them. The Business app is the phone app with a profile, catalogue, quick replies and labels. The Business Platform is the separate product with its own terms in the same policy. Under those terms you may start a conversation only with an approved message template, and may reply freely only within the 24-hour customer service window. A single-shop dealer is normally on the app, and every catalogue feature described below comes from the help pages for the app.

The policy page (WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, shown as last updated on 23 September 2026, and still shown that way on 31 October 2026) covers both. It requires a business profile with accurate customer support contact details. It also bars misrepresenting your affiliation with another business: speaking in the voice of another business, or taking its name or brand without permission, can breach the policy. That is the WhatsApp version of the authorisation point above.

### Business profile: what India requires

For businesses selling in India, WhatsApp's help page ["How to Comply with the Laws for Selling Online in India"](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1262764931172994) lists the business details you must add before you can create a catalogue. It asks for:

- the full legal name of the business,
- the business type (limited liability partnership, sole proprietorship, partnership, public company, private company or other),
- the applicable licence or registration (our [GST and Udyam registration guide for solar dealers](/blog/gst-udyam-solar-inverter-dealer/) covers the common ones),
- the business address, same as the profile,
- customer care contact, with an email address and at least one landline or mobile number,
- grievance officer contact, with a name and at least one contact method.

On each catalogue item you must also select a country of origin. If the origin is not India, the app asks for the importer's name and address. Where you cannot tell, read the carton or the datasheet; do not guess. For a service rather than a physical good, the page says to pick "Not applicable".

WhatsApp's help page on complaints covers what happens when a user complains about a business account or its catalogue listings. WhatsApp asks the user to contact the Indian grievance officer. The page also says the business "must acknowledge any WhatsApp user complaint within 48 hours and resolve such complaints within 1 month from the date of receipt as required under applicable laws". Name a real person as the grievance officer and give that person the time to answer.

The same help section says businesses must make the disclosures required under applicable law and that listing information and images must be accurate and match the goods. That is why the catalogue below carries model numbers, not adjectives.

### How the catalogue works, and the limits as read

| Point | What the page says |
| --- | --- |
| Required per item | A unique title, at least one image, and the country of origin. |
| Optional per item | More images and videos, price, sale price, description, website link, product code. |
| Images | Up to 10 images can be uploaded for an item. |
| Price field | The page says prices can be added only in certain countries and does not list them. Look for the field in your own app before you plan around it. |
| Review | Every image and item is reviewed against the Business Messaging Policy, and items are visible only after approval. A rejected item can be appealed. |
| Collections | Groups of items, reviewed by WhatsApp, which says this "should take no more than 24 hours". Collections can be reordered. |
| Sharing | You can share a link to one item or to the whole catalogue. Collections cannot be shared as a single link. The auto-generated link cannot be customised. |
| Hiding | An item can be hidden without deleting it. |
| Number of items | The catalogue pages we opened state no cap on the number of items. We do not quote one; third-party guides give a figure, and we have not confirmed it from WhatsApp. |

Sources: [About catalog](https://faq.whatsapp.com/405903568419894), [How to create and maintain a catalog](https://faq.whatsapp.com/833697274483076), [collections](https://faq.whatsapp.com/2929318000711140) and [sharing catalog links](https://faq.whatsapp.com/487917009931629), read 30 October 2026.

Three practical rules follow for a dealer. Keep the number of items small enough that you can keep every one correct. Hide an item the day it goes out of stock. Tell customers to open a collection from your catalogue link, because you cannot send a collection on its own.

### Quick replies, greeting, away, labels and links

| Tool | Limit or rule as read | Dealer use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick replies | The maximum number stored is 50. Media is not supported in quick replies on web or desktop. A deleted one cannot be recovered. | Store your site-visit checklist, your address and hours, and your datasheet request reply. Keep a copy outside WhatsApp. |
| Greeting message | Sent when a customer first messages and again after 14 days of inactivity. | One line: who you are, hours, and what details you need to quote. |
| Away message | Can be set to always, a custom schedule, or outside business hours (needs business hours in the profile). | Tell people when you will answer. Do not promise a delivery date. |
| Labels or lists | You can create up to 20. The help page says labels are being replaced by lists, rolled out gradually. | New enquiry, quote sent, site visit booked, ordered, after-sales. |
| Short link and QR code | A short link is created automatically with the account. A default message can be set. Only official WhatsApp QR codes are scanned by the WhatsApp camera. | Print the QR on the invoice, the shop counter and the visiting card. |
| Click to chat link | The link format is `https://wa.me/<number>` with the number in international format, and it can carry a pre-filled message. | Use a different pre-filled message on your website, shop QR and cards so you can see where enquiries come from. |

Sources: the help pages for [quick replies](https://faq.whatsapp.com/smba/account-and-profile/how-to-use-quick-replies), [greeting messages](https://faq.whatsapp.com/smba/account-and-profile/how-to-use-greeting-messages), [away messages](https://faq.whatsapp.com/smba/account-and-profile/how-to-use-away-messages), [labels](https://faq.whatsapp.com/3398508707096369), [short links](https://faq.whatsapp.com/502291734918768), [QR codes](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5146068842165793) and [click to chat](https://faq.whatsapp.com/5913398998672934), read 30 October 2026.

Two switches can silently turn features off. The greeting page says greeting messages are disabled when Meta Business Agent is connected. The "automatic events" page says turning that on may affect broadcast lists, greeting and away messages, and will disconnect linked devices. If a greeting stops arriving, check those settings first.

WhatsApp's help article [How to create a link to your WhatsApp from your Google Business Profile](https://faq.whatsapp.com/712818078160617) opened on 31 October 2026. It says to go to Edit profile, then Contact, open the Chat section, choose WhatsApp, add your WhatsApp click to chat URL and save. It says the number must be a valid WhatsApp Business number in international format, and that you can add more than one number.

Google's own page, [Chat with customers from your Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/15013580), adds three limits. The profile must be claimed and verified. Chat options are "currently available for select regions". If you add both WhatsApp and text message, only the text message option is shown. Neither page lists India. Look for the Chat field in your own profile before you plan around it, and use that route, not a workaround. Google's guidelines say the website and phone on a profile must be your business's own and must not "redirect or 'refer' users to landing pages or phone numbers other than those of the actual business".

### Broadcasts and consent in one paragraph

WhatsApp's policy says you may contact people only if they gave you their number and if "you have received opt-in permission from the recipient confirming that they wish to receive subsequent messages". It also says to honour opt-outs. Broadcast lists on the Business app reach only people who have saved your number. Marketing messages and business broadcasts are, per WhatsApp, charged per delivered message where available and are limited to certain countries, so check whether you see them. Message customers about what they asked for. For the TRAI and DPDP rules, see [the consent rules in our customer-acquisition guide](/blog/how-solar-installers-get-customers/), which reads the primary text. This guide adds nothing to it.

Thinking of adding an inverter brand to your counter? You can send the [Qbits partner application](/become-our-partner/) with your shop details. It opens a review only. Nothing in this guide sets out terms, including what any brand offers a dealer.

## Catalogue structure for an inverter and panel dealer

Group items the way a buyer thinks: what do I need, and how big. A catalogue organised by brand forces the buyer to know the brand first. A catalogue organised by family and capacity class lets them find the right size, then compare.

| Collection | Capacity classes | One item per |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On-grid inverters, single phase | 1 to 3 kW class, 3 to 6 kW class | Family and capacity class |
| On-grid inverters, three phase | 5 to 17 kW class, 20 to 30 kW class, 30 kW and above | Family and capacity class |
| Hybrid inverters | Single phase 3 to 8 kW, three phase 5 to 12 kW | Family and battery voltage |
| Solar panels | By wattage class and cell type | Wattage class |
| Cables and connectors | By cross-section | Cross-section |
| Protection and mounting | DC and AC boxes, SPDs, structures | Type |

Put the stock-planning logic in how many classes you carry, not in the catalogue. [The stock planning guide](/blog/solar-inverter-stock-planning-new-dealers/) shows how to decide which capacities to hold.

What goes in each item description:

1. **Model or model range**, exactly as on the datasheet.
2. **Rating**: AC output in kW or, for panels, watts peak.
3. **Phase**: single or three.
4. **IP rating**, as printed on the datasheet for that model.
5. **Datasheet link** in the website link field, or the words "datasheet on request".
6. **Country of origin**, which the app requires anyway.
7. **Stock status and date**, so a stale item is visibly stale.

Leave out any claim you cannot prove. That means "up to" efficiency figures you did not copy from the datasheet, "best", "premium", warranty years you did not read in the written warranty, and "authorised" wording. Our guide to [reading inverter datasheets](/blog/how-to-read-solar-inverter-datasheets/) covers which datasheet lines are safe to copy.

On price: Google's description page bars prices in the description, and the WhatsApp price field is optional. If you do show a price, put a date and a validity in the description, such as "Rs [amount] on [date], valid [x] days, plus GST". The ASCI Code names the problem with "Prices from as low as Rs. Y" claims: it calls them not acceptable where the buyer could be misled about how widely the price is available. The simplest rule is no price without a date, and no "from" price at all.

To show how a datasheet fills the fields, here is one row, for one Qbits family, using only the range and phase in our product data. It is an illustration of the method, not a statement about any dealer programme.

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Item title | Three-phase on-grid inverter, 5 to 17 kW range |
| Model range | QB 5/6/8/10/12/15/17KTLC |
| Rating | 5 to 17 kW, model by model |
| Phase | Three |
| Datasheet and IP rating | Copy from the model's datasheet on the [product page](/our-products/QB-6-17KTLC/) |
| Price and warranty | Left blank; ask for a written quote and the written warranty for the exact model |

## Templates you can copy

Replace every item in [square brackets]. If a bracket does not apply to you, delete the sentence.

### Google Business Profile description

> Solar shop in [locality], [city]. We sell on-grid and hybrid inverters, solar panels, cables, mounting structures and protection boxes for homes, shops and small factories. Visit to see inverters on display and read the datasheets before you decide. We stock [brand A] and [brand B] products. Our team can survey a site within [city and nearby towns]. Open [days], [opening time] to [closing time]. Call or message us on WhatsApp to book a site visit.

That example is about 450 characters, well under the 750 limit, has no link or price, and makes no authorisation claim. Delete "We stock" unless every named brand is on your shelf and in your purchase invoices, and delete the survey sentence if you do not survey.

## A weekly routine for the two listings

| When | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Reply to new reviews with a specific detail. Check for unanswered WhatsApp chats and clear the "new enquiry" label. | 20 minutes |
| Tuesday | Upload three real photos: shelf, delivery, or a team member at the counter. | 10 minutes |
| Wednesday | Publish one Update post: new stock, a datasheet explainer or a safety reminder. | 15 minutes |
| Thursday | Walk the shelf. Hide catalogue items that are out of stock. Refresh dates on any price you show. | 20 minutes |
| Friday | Ask customers delivered this week for a review, using the link or QR. No incentive. | 15 minutes |
| First of the month | Count enquiries by source (Google profile, shop QR, website, cards) from the pre-filled messages. Fix the weakest. | 30 minutes |

Customers will also ask after a unit has gone wrong. Have a reply ready, and a process behind it: see [how dealers handle inverter service claims](/blog/dealer-inverter-service-claim-process/).
