## Why Installers Look for a Solar Ladder Alternative

### Engineering depth ceiling
Solar Ladder is positioned as a business operating system for Indian solar companies, with a sales-oriented design module rather than an engineering-first one. Lender-grade simulation, module-level shading, [single line diagrams](/blog/solar-sld-software/), and CAD handoff to a consultant are not what it markets itself on, so teams growing into C&I or [PPA](/glossary/ppa/) work usually add a design platform beside it. Confirm the current design module scope with the vendor. SurgePV ships [8,760-hour shade analysis](https://surgepv.com/shadow-analysis) and [bankable yield reports](https://surgepv.com/generation-financial-tool) on every paid plan, the same category our [solar shading analysis software](/blog/solar-shading-analysis-software/) guide covers.

### Where the bankable report comes from
Lenders want P50, P75, P90 outputs the project finance community accepts, and that output belongs to the design and simulation layer rather than the operations layer. SurgePV produces it out of the box, a workflow detailed in our [PV yield simulation software](/blog/pv-yield-simulation-software/) guide.

### Scope and workflow
For installers who want engineering, proposal, and CRM in one workflow, Solar Ladder is normally paired with a design tool. SurgePV plus QuickEstimate is the two-layer stack we recommend, with a clean API handoff between them.
