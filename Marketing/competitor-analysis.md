## 3. Three Messaging Angles No Competitor Owns

1. **"AI-tuned for the Indian grid"** — Sungrow/Growatt do AI globally; nobody localizes the narrative. Own the *"trained on Indian voltage patterns"* story.
2. **"Engineering accountability with a face"** — Founder-led, Indian-named engineering team. Imports are anonymous; legacy brands are corporate. Qbits = "you know who built it."
3. **"The installer's brand, recommended by EPCs, not Amazon"** — Position explicitly as channel-protected. Every other brand secretly dumps on marketplaces; making this a public promise is differentiating.

## 5. Installer Community Gap — The Whitespace

**Nobody owns a true Indian EPC installer hub.** Solis Academy and Waaree's franchise portal come closest, but both are gated and product-narrow. The opportunity:

**"Qbits Installer Academy"** — a free, public-but-registered resource hub containing:
- Video commissioning library (Hindi/Tamil/Telugu/Marathi)
- DISCOM-by-DISCOM net-metering paperwork templates
- ROI calculators installers can white-label for their customers
- Lead-share program (Qbits routes website leads to certified installers by pincode)
- Monthly installer leaderboard + spotlight content
- Spare-parts SLA tracker (public dashboard — radical transparency)
- WhatsApp community + monthly virtual office hours with Qbits engineering
- "Field Issue Friday" — real RMA cases solved on video
- Tiered certification (Bronze/Silver/Gold) with margin + lead-quality benefits

**Why this wins:** It converts SEO traffic (top-of-funnel homeowners) into a lead pool that *the installer network monetizes for Qbits.* Every installer becomes a distribution node — without paying performance marketing for each lead.
