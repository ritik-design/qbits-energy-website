For commercial and industrial facility owners in India investing in solar energy, understanding inverter depreciation is essential to maximising ROI. Solar inverters represent significant capital expenditure, and proper depreciation planning unlocks substantial tax benefits.

## Depreciation Rates

| Method | Annual rate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WDV (Written Down Value)** | **40%** | Standard method - front-loaded |
| Straight Line Method (SLM) | ~13.91% | Equal annual deductions |

Inverters are classified as "plant and machinery" under the Income Tax Act 1961. Both on-grid and hybrid inverters receive identical treatment.

## Accelerated Depreciation Benefits

- 40% depreciation claim in **first year**
- Example: **₹40 lakh inverter** investment → ₹16 lakh year-one depreciation
- At 30% tax rate → **₹4.8 lakh immediate tax savings**
- Five-year cumulative savings can exceed **26% of initial investment**

## Method Comparison

### WDV Method

| Pro | Con |
| --- | --- |
| Front-loads depreciation | Lower deductions in later years |
| Better time-value alignment | Requires year-by-year calculation |
| Improves project IRR | - |

### SLM Method

| Pro | Con |
| --- | --- |
| Equal annual deduction | Lower overall tax benefits |
| Predictable expense pattern | Worse time-value alignment |

## Maximising Tax Deductions

### Checklist

1. Proper asset capitalisation with detailed records
2. Accurate commissioning documentation
3. Appropriate depreciation method selection
4. Detailed asset registers per unit
5. Coordination with annual tax filings

## Inverter Selection Criteria for Depreciation Planning

- **Extended warranty coverage** (12+ years preferred)
- Proven reliability and low failure rates
- High efficiency ratings (98%+)
- Strong manufacturer support and documentation
- Digital warranty system for accurate records

## Closing

Depreciation is the most underutilised tool in commercial solar economics. Combine 40% WDV with a 12-year warranty (protecting asset value during the critical 1–7 year period) and proper documentation, and the effective cost of solar drops by 25–40% - turning marginal payback into compelling returns.
