## What a Solar Inverter AMC Actually Covers

An **Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)** is a paid yearly agreement where a service provider takes responsibility for keeping your solar system maintained. The word sounds standardised. It is not. Two AMCs at the same price can cover completely different scopes.

A typical full-scope AMC in India bundles five things:

1. **Panel cleaning.** Monthly to quarterly visits, depending on dust load. This is the bulk of the actual work in most contracts.
2. **Inverter inspection.** Visual check, error-log review, cooling fan and heat-sink cleaning, terminal torque check, and firmware updates.
3. **Electrical safety checks.** Earthing resistance test, insulation check, surge protection status, and connector inspection.
4. **Monitoring and reporting.** Generation review against expected output, with a flag when performance drops.
5. **Priority breakdown service.** A promised response time (48 to 96 hours is common) when something fails.

Premium contracts add thermal imaging of connectors, IV-curve testing of strings, and replacement of minor consumables like fuses and MC4 connectors. A coastal AMC with quarterly thermal imaging and six-monthly torque checks runs ₹1,000 to ₹1,500 per kW per year, according to Heaven Green Energy (2026). Inland pricing sits at ₹500 to ₹1,000.

Notice what is usually missing: inverter parts. Most AMCs cover labour and consumables. If the inverter board fails, the part cost lands on you or on the manufacturer warranty, not on the AMC. That single exclusion changes the entire value equation, and it is buried in clause 11 of the contract you are about to sign.

The [solar inverter maintenance guide for India](/blog/inverter-maintenance-india/) details what genuine inverter upkeep involves, so you can check whether the AMC scope lists real work or filler.

## What Drives the Price Up or Down

Five factors legitimately move AMC pricing. Knowing them lets you tell a justified premium from padding.

**Location and dust load.** Rajasthan, Gujarat's arid belt, and Delhi NCR need weekly to fortnightly cleaning in the dry season. Skipping a ₹1,000 per kW cleaning plan in these regions costs ₹4,000 to ₹8,000 per kW per year in lost generation. That figure comes from Heaven Green Energy (2026). Here, a cleaning-heavy AMC genuinely pays for itself. In a clean coastal Kerala town with regular rain, the same cleaning frequency is waste.

**Roof access.** A flat terrace with a staircase is cheap to service. A tiled sloped roof needing safety gear and two technicians doubles visit cost.

**Response-time SLA.** A 48-hour guaranteed response costs the vendor real money in staffing. A vague "best efforts" clause costs them nothing. Pay only for the first.

**Scope of inverter coverage.** Contracts that include inverter parts and board-level repair cost more, and should. This is the one premium worth examining closely, because inverter replacement of a 5 kW unit runs ₹40,000 to ₹70,000 out of warranty.

**Who holds the contract.** Your original installer knows the system but may exit the business. Third-party national service firms survive installers but may not know your DISCOM paperwork or site history. Manufacturer-backed service sits between the two on both counts.

## Red Flags in AMC Contracts: What a Rip-off Looks Like

After reviewing how these contracts are structured across the dealer network, these are the clauses that separate a fair AMC from a rip-off.

**1. Sold as inverter protection on a warrantied inverter.** If your inverter has years of manufacturer warranty left, an AMC priced around inverter risk is double payment. Ask the vendor: "What does this cover that my warranty does not?" The honest answer is cleaning, checks, and response time. If the pitch leans on inverter failure fear, walk.

**2. Comprehensive in the brochure, parts excluded in the fine print.** The single most common trap. Comprehensive AMC should mean parts included. In many contracts it means labour only, with inverter parts billed separately. Read the exclusions page, not the sales page.

**3. No response-time SLA.** "We will attend at the earliest" is not an SLA. A fair contract states hours: 48, 72, or 96. It also states the penalty or service credit when the SLA is missed.

**4. Visit caps below the cleaning schedule.** A contract promising monthly cleaning but capping total visits at six per year is a contract designed to be breached. Count the visits.

**5. Escalation above 10 percent per year.** AMC pricing should track technician wages, roughly 5 to 8 percent annual escalation. Clauses allowing 15 to 20 percent hikes, or worse, unilateral revision, convert a fair year-one price into a rip-off by year three.

**6. No performance commitment.** Better AMCs include a generation or availability commitment. If measured performance falls below a threshold for maintenance-related reasons, the vendor acts at their cost. Absence of any such clause means you are paying for activity, not outcomes.

**7. Non-transferable and non-cancellable.** If you sell the house, a fair AMC transfers to the buyer or refunds pro-rata. Lock-ins with no exit are a sign the vendor profits from your inertia.

**8. Third-party AMC that voids your installer warranty.** Some installation workmanship warranties require the original installer to perform maintenance. Signing an outside AMC can quietly void that. Check both contracts before signing either. ## When an AMC Is Unnecessary

Here is the opinion, backed by the numbers above: most Indian homes under 10 kW do not need a full AMC. The maintenance a residential system actually requires is modest, and buying it piecemeal costs half to a third of the typical AMC fee.

Skip the full AMC when all of these are true:

- **Your inverter carries a long full replacement warranty.** Failure risk is already covered. The [12-year solar inverter warranty](/blog/12-year-solar-inverter-warranty/) explains what full replacement coverage means in practice, including the 72-hour RMA commitment.
- **You have monitoring with fault alerts.** Qbits systems push fault alerts and daily generation over WhatsApp. You know within hours when something is wrong, so priority-response promises lose their value.
- **Your roof is safely accessible.** Cleaning is a ₹400 visit or a careful DIY job with plain water and a soft brush.
- **Your local grid and climate are kind.** Low dust, regular rain, no coastal salt, no extreme heat. Maintenance frequency drops, and so does AMC value.
- **You are comfortable booking per visit.** If scheduling a cleaner twice a quarter does not burden you, pay-per-visit wins on cost every year.

> **The exception:** dusty regions, coastal salt-mist zones, and systems above 15 to 25 kW. It also covers roofs needing professional access equipment, and owners who travel or run the site remotely. In those cases a well-written AMC is not an expense. It is cheaper than the generation you would lose.

Also consider [solar insurance in India](/blog/solar-insurance-india/) for the risks neither warranty nor AMC touches: theft, storm damage, and fire. Owners sometimes buy an AMC expecting catastrophe cover. It is not that product.

## How to Decide: The 4-Question AMC Test

Run these four questions before signing anything. The test works for homes and small commercial sites.

**1. What failure coverage do I already have?** List your inverter warranty term and type. Add the panel product warranty (usually 12 years), the performance warranty (25 to 30 years), and your installer's workmanship warranty. Whatever the AMC claims to cover must be checked against this list first. Overlap is money wasted.

**2. What does the same work cost per visit?** Price the cleaning visits, one electrical check, and one inverter inspection at local rates. For most homes this total is ₹4,000 to ₹7,000. Compare the AMC quote against this number, not against other AMC quotes.

**3. What is a day of downtime worth to me?** Multiply your daily units by your tariff. Below roughly ₹500 per day of downtime cost, a response-time SLA is a convenience, not a necessity. Above it, an SLA earns its premium. Commercial sites with export penalties or production dependence should weight this higher.

**4. Does the contract survive the red-flag list?** Parts exclusion, missing SLA, visit caps, escalation above 10 percent, no exit clause. Two or more red flags means renegotiate or reject. A vendor who will not put a response time in writing will not honour one verbally either.

If the answers point away from an AMC, set a phone reminder for fortnightly cleaning in the dry season. Book one annual electrical check each November before winter generation peaks. Keep your warranty documents and installer contacts in one folder. That folder, plus monitoring alerts, is a complete maintenance strategy for most homes.
