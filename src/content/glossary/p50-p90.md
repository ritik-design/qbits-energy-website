## What are P50 and P90

P50 and P90 are statistical confidence levels used in [solar yield](/glossary/solar-yield/) estimation to express the probability distribution of expected plant generation. The values come from variability in weather (year-to-year irradiance fluctuation), data uncertainty (irradiance database accuracy) and modelling assumptions ([degradation](/glossary/degradation/), [soiling](/glossary/soiling/), equipment performance).

- P50: median yield. 50 percent probability of exceeding this value in any given year. Best estimate for project planning.
- P90: conservative yield. 90 percent probability of meeting or exceeding. Used by lenders for debt sizing.
- P75: intermediate, 75 percent probability.
- P95: highly conservative, 95 percent probability.
- P99: very conservative, near-worst-case.

For Indian solar, P90 yield typically falls 90-95 percent of P50. A 1 MWp plant with P50 of 1,700 MWh/year may have P90 of 1,580 MWh/year. Lenders model debt service against P90; developers report P50 to customers.

> **TL;DR**
> - P50 is the median yield estimate (50 percent probability of exceeding it); P90 is the conservative estimate lenders use to size debt.
> - Indian P90 typically runs 90 to 95 percent of P50, based on inter-annual irradiance variability, data uncertainty and modelling assumptions.
> - Bankable P90 needs at least 10 years of irradiance data, with 20+ years preferred.
> - Indian lenders (PFC, REC, SBI, Tata Capital, IREDA) size debt against P90; developers plan and market against P50.
> - P75 supports mezzanine debt and conservative equity; P95 and P99 are used for stress testing and insurance.
> - For Indian utility solar, P50 typically lands 1,650 to 1,900 kWh/kWp/year, with P90 around 1,500 to 1,750 kWh/kWp/year.

## Why P-values matter

Project finance. Lenders size debt against P90 to ensure debt service coverage.

Risk transparency. P-values express yield uncertainty.

[PPA](/glossary/ppa/) structuring. P50 supports tariff bids, P90 supports debt.

Equity returns. P50-P90 spread affects equity risk.

Insurance. P-values inform policy structuring.

Combined with [Performance Ratio](/glossary/pr/) and [CUF](/glossary/cuf/), P50/P90 estimates form the backbone of Indian project bankability.

## P50 and P90 calculation

1. Collect multi-year irradiance data (10-20+ years), often anchored by on-site land feasibility and site survey work.
2. Build a [yield simulation model](/blog/pv-yield-simulation-software/) incorporating system specifications.
3. Propagate weather, data and modelling uncertainties through yield model.
4. Generate distribution of annual yield.
5. Extract P50, P90, P95, P99 percentiles.

Tools such as SurgePV's [solar simulation software](https://surgepv.com/solar-simulation-software/) automate this workflow for EPCs and lenders producing bankable yield reports.

## Technical deep dive

### Typical P-value distribution

| Percentile | Description |
|---|---|
| P50 | Median, 50% prob of exceeding |
| P75 | 75% prob, used by some lenders |
| P90 | 90% prob, standard bankable |
| P95 | 95% prob, conservative bankable |
| P99 | 99% prob, near worst case |

### Indian P50 to P90 ratio

| Component of uncertainty | Magnitude |
|---|---|
| Inter-annual irradiance | ±4-6% |
| Irradiance data uncertainty | ±3-5% |
| Yield model uncertainty | ±2-3% |
| Degradation prediction | ±2% |
| Combined | P90 = 90-95% of P50 |

### Example: 1 MW plant in Maharashtra

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| P50 yield | 1,700 MWh/year |
| P75 yield | 1,640 MWh/year |
| P90 yield | 1,580 MWh/year |
| P95 yield | 1,540 MWh/year |
| P50 revenue at ₹2.75 | ₹46.75 lakh |
| P90 revenue at ₹2.75 | ₹43.45 lakh |
| Debt service capacity (DSCR 1.3) | Based on P90 |

### Lender perspective

| Aspect | Methodology |
|---|---|
| Debt amount | DSCR × P90 cash flow |
| Debt service | Must be covered by P90 cash flow |
| Equity cushion | P50 - P90 buffer |
| Stress scenarios | P95 or P99 |

## Best practices

Use multi-year ground-validated [irradiation data](/blog/solar-irradiance-data-india-statewise/) (10+ years).

Apply Perez sky model for POA calculation.

Include realistic uncertainty bands.

Document P50, P75, P90, P95 in yield reports.

Update P-values after first 3-5 years of operation.
